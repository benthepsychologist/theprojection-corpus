## 2026-09-28 — Thread opened; the transition so far

- **Venezuela's interim government freed 39 more political prisoners Sunday, including a 2024 opposition presidential candidate and a campaign figure for opposition leader María Corina Machado, in a second round of releases following talks between the opposition and the government installed after Nicolás Maduro's January removal.** Rights group Justicia, Encuentro y Perdón said the releases included Biagio Pilieri and former electoral official Enrique Márquez, plus eight women held since 2021 on "narco-terrorism" charges; the government said as of 09-18 it still held 344 people it classifies as political prisoners, down from 391 on 08-10. The releases follow a Tuesday meeting between interim President Delcy Rodríguez and President Trump at the UN General Assembly, where Rodríguez pledged elections as part of a transition to "full democracy" without setting a date. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/27/venezuela-frees-39-political-prisoners-after-post-maduro-talks), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/09/26/venezuela-political-prisoners-release-delcy-rodriguez/3d775e1e-ba08-11f1-94cb-d3d8f22a8c8b_story.html)) ⟨daily 2026-09-27⟩
- **Hundreds of Machado supporters, in her Vente Venezuela party's sky-blue colors, rode motorcycles and drove through Caracas and other state capitals (Valencia, Maracaibo, Mérida) Sunday demanding her safe return from exile and a presidential-election date.** No date has been set; Machado and 2024 apparent election winner Edmundo González Urrutia remain outside the country. ([AP via WSLS](https://www.wsls.com/news/world/2026/09/27/venezuelans-rally-to-demand-opposition-leaders-safe-return-and-presidential-elections/)) ⟨daily 2026-09-27⟩
