## 2026-08-25 (added 2026-08-29) — A think tank names the actual structural barrier on the AI-procurement side: 13 jurisdictions, not funding

- **The C.D. Howe Institute (a nonpartisan Canadian economic-policy think tank) published a Council Report, "Scaling Up: Commercializing Health AI in Canada," on 2026-08-25**, writing up its Health Sector Economic Growth and Resilience Working Group's fourth meeting with Ontario's Auditor General, Canada Health Infoway, and Accenture. It puts a national scale number on AI-scribe adoption for the first time on this thread's record — Canada Health Infoway's national AI Scribe Program drew 10,000+ provider registrations within two weeks of launch and has now supported 11.5M+ patient encounters — and names **fragmented procurement authority across 13 provincial/territorial jurisdictions as the "principal structural obstacle"** to scaling health AI in Canada, not funding or appetite. **It does not touch mental-health funding at all** — a reminder that the AI-procurement and MH-funding sides of this thread's asymmetry are institutionally disconnected, not just politically so; nobody is arguing the tradeoff explicitly, they're just two different bureaucracies moving at two different speeds.
  ([C.D. Howe Institute](https://cdhowe.org/publication/scaling-up-commercializing-health-ai-in-canada/)) ⟨daily 2026-08-29⟩

## 2026-07-23 → 07-31 — The 2027 cliff gets a month and a standoff ⟨cold rotation⟩

*Logged 2026-08-18, nineteen days late.*

- **The bilateral health funding agreements lapse in March 2027**, per the
  Charlottetown First Ministers' communiqué — refining this thread's prior
  "lapsing 2027, unreplaced" to an actual month. The mental-health and
  substance-use stream is inside that lapse.
  ([CPAC](https://www.cpac.ca/headline-politics/episode/pm-carney-and-premiers-on-us-tariff-threat-health-care--july-23-2026)) ⟨daily 2026-08-18⟩
- **Premiers pressed PM Carney directly for renewal certainty at the
  07-23 meeting, and he did not commit** — punting to a future
  finance-and-health-ministers meeting **with no date set.** This is the
  thread's central question moving from a budget-document absence into an
  on-the-record federal–provincial standoff.
  ⟨daily 2026-08-18⟩
- Follow-on coverage through 07-27/07-31 has premiers describing a coming
  **"fiscal cliff"** and advocates publicly urging Ottawa to renew, with
  the federal government still uncommitted as of 2026-08-18.
  ⟨daily 2026-08-18⟩
- **Nothing new on the AI side of the ledger.** Amii's $10M Health
  Innovation Lab, Ontario's hallucinating-scribes audit and the Santé
  Québec pilot all resurfaced in generic coverage this window but all
  pre-date 2026-07-25 as events. **The asymmetry this thread names — AI
  infrastructure funded, mental-health funding unrenewed — is unchanged.**
  ⟨daily 2026-08-18⟩


## 2026-07-28 — Private AI-health capital keeps outpacing public MH money

- **French insurtech Alan (first new Canadian health insurer since
  1957) is scaling its AI-powered care-navigation model in Canada** —
  a $780M Series G ($8.9B valuation) funds expansion beyond its ~4,100
  members/100 employers, with an AI health assistant slated for launch
  next year. The counter-signal keeps recurring: private AI-health
  capital moves fast in Canada while the federal MH funding side (the
  2027 bilateral-agreement cliff) stays static.
  ([The Logic](https://thelogic.co/news/alan-insurance-expansion-ai/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩
