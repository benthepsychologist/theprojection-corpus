# Yemen's civil war — timeline

*Watch:* The Houthi-versus-government ground war inside Yemen — distinct
from the Houthi maritime campaign and its oil read, which stay on the Red
Sea threads: the western-coast offensive toward Mokha and the heights over
Bab al-Mandab, whether government forces hold what they claim to retake,
the daily toll, Houthi threats to close the strait if Gulf states join the
wider war, and whether the UN envoy's warning of a return to full-scale war
becomes a formal collapse of the 2022 truce.

## 2026-09-05 — A second day of the heaviest fighting in years: more than sixty killed, including six civilians in a bus strike in Taiz

- **More than sixty people were killed on 09-05 in fighting between Houthi forces and Saudi-backed government forces — 26 government troops, 31 Houthi fighters and six civilians, the last in a missile strike on a bus in Taiz** — on the second day of a Houthi western-coast offensive that has pushed toward the port of Mokha and the hills overlooking the Bab al-Mandab strait. ⚠️ Single-outlet count for the day; the previous day's toll is wire-corroborated below. ([Times of Israel liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-05-2026/)) ⟨daily 2026-09-05⟩

## 2026-09-04 — A Houthi ground offensive toward Mokha and Bab al-Mandab kills more than 120 in two days, the worst clashes in years

- **A Houthi ground offensive launched 09-03/04 in western Yemen pushed toward the Red Sea port of Mokha and the hills overlooking the Bab al-Mandab strait, with the toll from Thursday climbing past 120 (AFP, Arab News) and to 129 (Gulf News) by Friday, including a missile strike on a restaurant in al-Makha with civilian casualties.** Government forces claimed the same day to have recaptured some of the hills; Houthi officials renewed threats to close Bab al-Mandab if Gulf states join the wider war. The heaviest fighting inside Yemen since the 2022 truce, and the front the UN's envoy warned in August could return the country to full-scale war. ([Arab News, AFP](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/more-than-120-killed-in-yemen-clashes-since-thursday-sources-3000492), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/4/yemens-army-says-recaptured-key-territory-as-clashes-with-houthis-continue), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/yemen/yemen-sees-worst-clashes-in-years-as-fighting-kills-129-1.500664105)) ⟨daily 2026-09-05⟩
