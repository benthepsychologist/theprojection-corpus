# Allianz AI claims automation — timeline

*Watch:* Allianz went from Solvency-II-constrained capital pool to the
#1-ranked insurer on the 2026 Evident AI Index, backed by a live agentic
claims system (Project Nemo, ~80% faster on small claims in Australia) and
a named global partnership with Anthropic (Claude free to all employees,
Claude Code already in developer use, co-developed agentic claims AI for
motor/health). Track: whether Project Nemo or the Anthropic-built claims
agents expand beyond their initial scope/geography, and whether any
EU/Solvency-II regulatory response to agentic claims AI emerges.
