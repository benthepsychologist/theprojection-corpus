## 2026-09-18 — Russia's three-day Duma election opens with Yabloko barred as the war grinds on; the EU disburses €3.3bn for Ukrainian missiles and drones

- **Russia opened three days of voting (09-18 → 09-20) for all 450 State Duma seats, the first parliamentary election since the full-scale invasion, with only pro-war parties on the ballot** — United Russia, the Communist Party, A Just Russia, the nationalist LDPR and New People. Yabloko, the sole openly anti-war party, was barred after Russia's Supreme Court cancelled its federal candidate slate in August on a nationalist party's copyright/foreign-funding complaint; it argues voters are better off not voting at all. **Independent monitors (Meduza, EPDE) reported irregularities on day one:** online-vote counts running roughly 100x the 2021 election's pace while physical polling stations showed near-zero turnout at midday; organised transport of voters to polling stations in several regions; public-sector workers and teachers pressured to vote online and report their own and their parents' turnout; and Russian-occupied Donetsk ("DNR") claiming 75%+ of registered voters — over a million people — had voted early. Final results, and this thread's own standing question of "whether any anti-war voice appears on a ballot at all," resolve on 09-20.
