# Berkshire's AI capital stance — timeline

*Watch:* Berkshire — famously the tech-skeptic conglomerate, and sitting on
the largest true dry powder on the board (~$550B+) — has built a real,
fast-growing Alphabet position it explicitly frames as an AI-capex bet,
even as new CEO Greg Abel draws an institutional line against "AI for the
sake of AI." The tension between Buffett's individual stock-picking (he
says he personally initiated the Alphabet buy) and Abel's stated
operational caution is the thread's core watch. Track: the Q2 2026 13F
(due within 45 days of June 30) for whether the Alphabet position kept
growing or any other AI-adjacent name appears; any further Buffett/Abel
commentary; and the GEICO/insurance-AI-liability strand as a secondary,
lower-priority watch.

## 2026-08-08 — Q2 earnings: Abel ends the 14-quarter selling streak, doubles down on Alphabet

- **Berkshire's Q2 2026 earnings answered this thread's core watch directly: Abel became a net buyer of stocks for the first time in 15 quarters, and the single largest move was $10B more into Alphabet — pushing it to Berkshire's 5th-largest holding on a 224% single-quarter surge.** Net profit doubled to $25.7B (from $12.4B); operating earnings rose to $13.0B (from $11.2B); buybacks resumed at $4.5B for the quarter (more than $3.3B continuing into July). Cash/T-bills only ticked down modestly ($344.1B at June 30 vs. $347.7B at March 31) — the "~$500B pile" framing several outlets used overstates what was actually deployed, but the direction (buying, not hoarding) is the real signal. - **Opened following a board-pass audit that found the entire insurance
  pocket with zero live thread coverage.** Berkshire's own board entry
  already tracks its general M&A/capital behavior; this thread is

  story that entry doesn't cover.
- **The Alphabet position built rapidly across the last year**: first
  disclosed Q3 2025 (17.8M shares, ~$4.3B) -> ~54M shares by Q1 2026 ->
  a $10B private placement on 2026-06-01 when Alphabet raised $80B in new
  equity specifically to fund AI infrastructure (split $5B Class A / $5B
  Class C) — combined position estimated $28-41B depending on the
  article's date. ⟨crawl 2026-08-04⟩
- **Buffett, on CNBC 2026-07-15, said he personally initiated the position**
  and tied it directly to the AI spending race: "The real question with
  Google and all of its competitors now, because they're all laying out
  hundreds of billions, and that's real money." He called the buildout
  "less attractive" than other Berkshire holdings and said he regretted
  not buying in earlier — pragmatic acknowledgment of the AI capex race,
  not tech enthusiasm. ⟨crawl 2026-08-04⟩
- **Abel, at his first annual meeting as CEO (2026-05-02), drew the
  opposite institutional line**: "We're not going to do AI for the sake of
  AI" — AI gets adopted only where it "clearly benefits" Berkshire's core
  businesses. The meeting opened with a staged AI deepfake of Buffett
  himself, deliberately used to spotlight deepfake/AI-security risk; Abel:
  "It's a great reminder for our team... That is a significant risk across
  Berkshire that we're managing every day." Buffett separately called
  deepfake risk "scary," citing specifically a convincing deepfake of a
  nuclear-armed head of state. ⟨crawl 2026-08-04⟩
- **Apple + Alphabet are now ~30% of Berkshire's ~$351B equity portfolio**
  (2026-07-14), after Abel cut the book from 42 to 29 holdings in Q1 2026
  alone. No Nvidia or other pure-play AI-infrastructure name appears in
  the Q1 2026 13F; Q2's wasn't yet public as of this crawl. ⟨crawl 2026-08-04⟩
