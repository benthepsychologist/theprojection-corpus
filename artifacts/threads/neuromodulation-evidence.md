## 2026 — The confirmatory trial lands, four years after clearance — and replicates at half the touted magnitude

- **Kratter et al. published a sham-controlled confirmatory RCT of
  Stanford Neuromodulation Therapy (SAINT) in *World Psychiatry* (n=48,
  24 active / 24 sham): remission 50.0% vs 20.8% (p=0.035), response
  54.2% vs 25.0% (p=0.039)** — replicating the direction of effect four
  years after the 30-patient trial that carried the FDA 510(k)
  clearance, at far more modest magnitude than the "80-90%" figure that
  circulated off the original. The study also reports left-ACC beta
  power as a predictive EEG biomarker — a treatment-selection angle the
  matching strand (mh-q02) tracks separately. (PMID 41536095)

## 2022 — Clearance and commercial rollout on a 30-patient trial

- **FDA cleared SNT via 510(k) (K220177) on Cole et al.'s original
  *American Journal of Psychiatry* RCT (n=30), and commercial SAINT
  rollout followed nationwide** — deployment preceding confirmatory
  evidence by roughly four years, the same pattern the psychedelic
  sprint is running now. The difference, and the reason this thread
  exists: here the confirmatory trial eventually happened and held.
