## 2026-08-15 — The Pentagon's data-platform rename draws scrutiny over whether it's real consolidation

- **DefenseScoop reported doubts that the Pentagon's "War Data Platform" —
  the rebrand of the former Advana system, the Chief Digital and AI
  Office's common data foundation meant to feed AI tools across the
  military — actually fixes what plagued the original program**, after
  Accenture Federal Services was awarded an up-to-$821M task order (via
  the CDAO with GSA as acquisition partner) to lead the integration
  effort. A former senior defense official quoted in the piece: "You don't
  fix a broken program by changing the name on the cover sheet and then
  hiring the same type of firm, and many of the same characters, that
  produced the original problems" — a direct hit on this thread's core
  question of whether "consolidation" is structural or cosmetic.
