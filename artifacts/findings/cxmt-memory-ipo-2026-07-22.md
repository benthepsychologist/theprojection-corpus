# CXMT's IPO — the capital story behind the memory squeeze — backstory finding

**The throughline:** a state-built champion (founded Hefei 2016; 36.29%
state-owned pre-IPO incl. the Big Fund) is listing at ~$85B while
Entity-Listed — record demand in absolute terms, yet read by the market as
*cautious* for a Chinese mega-IPO, priced into a STAR index down ~20% from
its July peak partly on fear this listing drains liquidity. The proceeds
race capacity (320k→420k wafers/month by 2027) against a 2–3 year HBM
technology gap — and US lawmakers say the squeeze doesn't ease before
~2028 regardless.

## The arc

- **2016→2025 — the state build.** Founded 2016 (chairman Zhu Yiming, of
  GigaDevice); world's #4 DRAM maker by 2025 (~7.7% share; revenue share
  4.1%→7.6% Q1'25→Q1'26 vs Samsung 38 / SK Hynix 29 / Micron 22); Q1
  revenue +719% YoY to 50.8B yuan. US listings: reportedly Commerce
  Entity List (late 2024) *and/or* DoD §1260H — two sources each name only
  one; no primary confirmation (open question).
  ([Yahoo explainer](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/explainer-cxmt-did-become-chinas-092012402.html) ·
  [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10812818) ·
  [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/07/17/chinese-memory-ban-would-cut-off-rampocalypse-relief/5273993))
- **07-13→14 — pricing.** Book-building >2× target (11,537 funds / 333
  institutions); priced 8.66 yuan → base ~$8.5B, up to ~$9.8B with
  greenshoe; ~$85B market cap — biggest-ever Chinese A-share semiconductor
  IPO, past SMIC's 2020 record.
  ([BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/503cd6f5-6bde-4e19-8e0c-e4bd74249f8b) ·
  [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10812818) ·
  [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/cxmt-ipo-shanghai-record-85-billion-valuation))
- **07-15 — prospectus.** Filed for STAR; leadership includes
  Micron/Samsung alumni (Digitimes, headline-verified).
- **07-16→17 — subscription.** Retail ~212× oversubscribed (9.4M+
  accounts; ~0.47% winning rate — highest on STAR record); institutional
  463×. Yahoo's framing: demand "much lower than most recent Chinese
  IPOs" — caution amid the tech rout. High-Flyer Quant (DeepSeek founder
  Liang Wenfeng's fund) bid for 12.55B shares. STAR50 −20% from July 1
  peak on liquidity-drain fears; official press reassured. Six banks split
  ~$41M in fees — 0.48% of proceeds vs the 4.52% 2026 A-share average.
  ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/article/3360892/chinese-memory-giant-cxmt-oversubscribed-212-times-mega-shanghai-ipo) ·
  [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/cxmts-shanghai-ipo-more-200-114816593.html) ·
  [DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/chinese-banks-cxmt-ipo-489355))
- **07-19→22 — demand-side lockup, allotment.** Dell/HP/Lenovo/Apple
  reported to have reserved CXMT DRAM through 2027 (low-confidence
  aggregator); Apple reportedly sought US approval just to *test* CXMT
  chips (buying ≠ supplying under the Entity List; possible iPhone-18-cycle
  decision late 2026). Final allotment results due today ahead of the
  **07-27 listing**.
  ([eTeknix](https://www.eteknix.com/pc-makers-reserve-cxmts-dram-supply-until-2027/) ·
  [explosion.com](https://www.explosion.com/201522/apple-tests-memory-chips-from-banned-chinese-supplier-cxmt/))
- **The squeeze verdict.** Counterpoint: ~9% of global DRAM bits → 11% by
  2028. Tech gap: competitors ship HBM4/4E; CXMT is in HBM3 development,
  HBM3E mass production targeted 2027. Capacity: 320k→420k wafers/month by
  2027, ×2 by 2030, ×3 by 2035 — but fabs take 4+ years to volume, so US
  lawmakers expect elevated memory prices "through at least 2028," and
  export-control status caps how much flows West anyway (overseas sales:
  2.79% of revenue; customers are Alibaba Cloud/ByteDance/Xiaomi/Honor).
  ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10812818) ·
  [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/07/17/chinese-memory-ban-would-cut-off-rampocalypse-relief/5273993))
