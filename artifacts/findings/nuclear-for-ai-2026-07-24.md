# Finding — Nuclear for AI — crawl 2026-07-24

**Verdict:** Power is the binding constraint, and the announced-to-
operational gap is nearly the entire story. As of 07-24, ~0 net-new nuclear
MW power any AI datacenter; the first firm electrons arrive 2027 from a
*restarted* reactor (TMI-1/Crane, ~835MW, still in SAFSTOR, NRC final
finding ~Sept 2026), not a new build. Every genuinely new build (Google/
Kairos, Amazon/X-energy, Meta/TerraPower + Oklo) is a 2030-2039 SMR/Gen-IV
project on designs not yet operating in the US.

**The scale check:** Carnegie's "Beyond the Hype" (06-02) pegs the ~13GW of
announced hyperscaler nuclear at <20% of projected datacenter demand through
2035; one year of planned US solar (43GW) dwarfs the whole mid-2030s nuclear
pipeline. Near-term "firm" adds are restarts/uprates that re-label existing
MW via PPA, not new generation.
