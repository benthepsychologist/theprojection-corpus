# The statehouse wave, mapped — backstory finding

**The throughline:** the wave is roughly twice as wide as commonly
summarized, and it has two distinct species: **therapy-practice laws**
(who may deliver therapy — Colorado's real-time-therapist mandate, the
IL/NV/UT bans, Vermont's bill) and **companion-safety laws** (disclosure
cadences, minor protections, crisis protocols — CA/CT/GA/NH/WA/NY/RI).
Nothing found challenges these laws in court yet — the DOJ task force's
one visible suit targets Colorado's *discrimination* law — and the Setzer
settlement (Jan) removed the marquee case that started it all.

## Corrections to our seed (important)

- **"7 states (IL/NV/UT/TN/VT/RI + 1)" was wrong in composition.**
  Tennessee: *no source found* — unverified, dropped pending evidence.
  Vermont's ~06-17 enactment: last live confirmation is 05-07 (pending
  Senate third reading) — treat as reported, not confirmed. IL/NV/UT:
  referenced everywhere, bill numbers found nowhere this pass.
- **Undercounted:** Colorado, Connecticut, Georgia, California, Texas,
  New Hampshire, Washington, Idaho, Iowa (+ Nebraska/Oregon named in
  comparison lists) all have enacted instruments; NY's companion
  *disclosure* law (GBL §1700 et seq.) has been effective since Nov 2025 —
  separate from the pending under-18 ban.

## The surrounding tracks

- **The case that started the wave settled:** Google/Character.AI agreed
  in January to settle the Sewell Setzer III wrongful-death suit
  (undisclosed terms).
  ([ABA Journal](https://www.abajournal.com/magazine/article/just-like-a-bad-suitor-ai-personalities-can-be-dangerous-some-states-say))
- **Preemption is real but hasn't touched these laws yet:** the Dec-2025
  EO's DOJ AI Litigation Task Force backs xAI's 07-12 suit against
  Colorado's *algorithmic-discrimination* law — no suit found against any
  therapy/companion chatbot law.
  ([Westword](https://www.westword.com/news/trump-administration-targets-colorado-ai-law-40911198/))
