# Finding — In-House Silicon — crawl 2026-07-24

**Verdict:** The custom-silicon revolt is real but uneven — diversification
at the margin and on inference, not a rout. Genuine displacement is
concentrated in two anchor cases: Anthropic trains/serves Claude largely on
Amazon Trainium (Project Rainier ~500k Trainium2, up to 5GW committed, >$100B
to AWS) AND has committed to up to 1M Google TPUs. The economics back it
(Ironwood ~44% lower TCO than a GB200 server; Trainium3 up to 50% lower
cost). But it's workload-specific: Meta's MTIA ("hundreds of thousands"
deployed) is inference/ranking, NOT frontier training; Microsoft is the
laggard (Maia 200 inference-only, "Braga" delayed, still Nvidia-reliant).

**The real winners are the co-designers:** Broadcom's custom-AI revenue hit
$10.8B in a quarter (+143%, >$100B 2027 target, ~6 XPU customers); Marvell
~$11B in 2026. "In-house" is largely outsourced. Nvidia's absolute demand
still grows; the CUDA-vs-XLA moat keeps it the default without a captive
anchor.
