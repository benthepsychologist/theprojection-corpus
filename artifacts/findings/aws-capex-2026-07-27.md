Ben's brief: reconstruct the destination of Amazon's ~$151B TTM
purchases-of-property-and-equipment / ~$200B 2026 capex plan against the
2026-07-23 AGI-team cuts. Crawled via Google News RSS + direct WebFetch

**Housekeeping note:** the literal "**$151B TTM**" figure named in the
brief was **not independently confirmed** this crawl — Amazon's own IR
pages could not be fetched (see gaps). What was directly confirmed: Q1
2026 capex of **$43.2–44.2B** (source disagreement between the earnings
transcript and press paraphrase), up from $25B in Q1 2025, and a
full-year 2026 guide of **~$200B**, "more than 50% higher than 2025."
Treat $151B as **plausible but this crawl's own inference**, not a cited
figure — the 2026-07-30 earnings call is where to verify it.

**Verdict up front:** the capex is not primarily buying Amazon its own
path to AGI — it's buying **rentable capacity that two other frontier
labs are contractually obligated to fill**. Anthropic alone committed
**over $100B to AWS over the next decade** (2026-04-20) and OpenAI signed
a separate **$100B, 8-year AWS compute deal** (2026-02-27, alongside a
$50B Amazon-into-OpenAI investment). Read against the 2026-07-22 AGI-team
cuts (post-training/model-customization roles on Amazon's own Nova
effort), the pattern is **coherent, not contradictory**: Amazon is
retreating from competing on frontier *research* while doubling down on
being the *landlord* frontier labs build on. The backlog evidence for
this: AWS's Q1 2026 RPO (contracted-but-unfulfilled backlog) hit **$364B,
up from $244B the prior quarter — and that figure explicitly excludes**
the Anthropic $100B+ deal (announced after the quarter closed).

## 2. SILICON — Trainium vs. Nvidia, and what AWS actually serves Anthropic

- **Scale:** Amazon has deployed **1.4 million Trainium chips** across
  all three generations combined; Trainium2 handles the majority of
  Bedrock inference traffic. (TechCrunch, 2026-03-22, directly fetched —
  **high**: `https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/22/an-exclusive-tour-of-amazons-trainium-lab-the-chip-thats-won-over-anthropic-openai-even-apple/`)
  Trainium3 claims up to **50% lower cost** than comparable Nvidia
  performance, pitched explicitly against "backlogged, hard-to-acquire"
  Nvidia GPUs (same source). Trainium3 UltraServers reportedly reached
  general availability 2025-12-01 (About Amazon per RSS — **thin**, not
  directly fetched). Jassy has separately stated AWS is still "buying a
  lot of Nvidia" even as Trainium3 ships (CRN/CNBC, Nov–Dec 2025,
  RSS-only — **thin-medium**).
- **The Anthropic question, answered directly:** the brief asked what AWS
  serves Anthropic now that Anthropic's headline TPU deal went to Google.
  **Answer: more, not less.** On **2026-04-20** — ten days after
  Anthropic's Google/Broadcom TPU expansion (2026-04-06/07, adding
  Broadcom-supplied Google TPU capacity from 2027) — Anthropic and Amazon
  announced an *expanded* collaboration:
  - Anthropic already running on **over 1 million Trainium2 chips**
    (via Project Rainier).
  - **Nearly 1GW** of combined Trainium2+Trainium3 capacity landing by
    end of 2026, headroom for **up to 5GW** total.
  - Amazon investing **$5B immediately** in Anthropic, up to **$20B
    more** available (**$25B total**).
  - Anthropic committing **over $100B to AWS over the next decade** —
    Jassy: Anthropic's commitment to "run its large language models on
    AWS Trainium for the next decade."
  (Anthropic's own press release, directly fetched, **high**:
  `https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-amazon-compute` — corroborated
  by 30+ outlets same week including CNBC, GeekWire, Forbes.) The
  Google TPU deal (2025-10-23/24, up to 1M TPUs, tens of billions, 1GW+ —
  WSJ/CNBC/DCD, RSS-only, **medium**) reads as **diversification, not
  displacement** — no source found gives a numeric split of Anthropic's
  compute across AWS/Google/Microsoft, but the AWS-side commitment grew
  in dollar and chip terms in the same window Anthropic was also
  expanding with Google, not shrinking.

## 3. POWER — PPAs, nuclear, GW secured

- **Talen/Susquehanna (operating, contracted):** AWS signed a **17-year
  PPA for 1,920 MW (1.92GW)** of nuclear power from the Susquehanna
  plant, through 2042 with extension options, structured "front of the
  meter" specifically to avoid the FERC approval that sank the original
  2024 co-located interconnection deal (FERC rejected that one in
  2024-11 over utility cost-shifting objections; Talen sued, then this
  PPA replaced it). Reported value **~$18B**. (Talen Energy IR press
  release, primary, 2025-06-11/12 — **high** on the PPA terms; **medium**
  on the $18B figure, single outlet, not in Talen's own release.)
- **X-Energy SMRs:** X-Energy IPO'd on Nasdaq **2026-04-23/24**, raising
  **~$1.02B**, shares surging ~27–31% to imply a ~$12B market cap,
  reported as "Amazon-backed" (Reuters + 3 converging outlets — **high**).
  Most recently, X-Energy joined **Project Prometheus**, a DOE Genesis
  Mission initiative led by Idaho National Laboratory, alongside NVIDIA
  and AWS (**2026-07-22**, X-Energy's own press release, primary,
  **high**) — same release states Amazon/X-Energy/Dow retain the
  **option to deploy more than 5GW of new nuclear by 2039** to support
  AWS data centers.
- **Total GW:** Amazon's standing public target is **5+GW of new nuclear
  by 2039** (via SMRs, reaffirmed 2026-07-22), distinct from the
  **1.92GW already under firm PPA** (Talen, operating capacity). No
  clean peer (Google/Microsoft/Meta) GW comparison could be sourced —
  the one article that looked purpose-built for this (Carnegie
  Endowment, "Beyond the Hype," 2026-06-02) could not be fetched in full
  text this session — **flagged as not found rather than guessed.**

## 4. PAYOFF CLAIM — growth attribution, the cuts, and the OpenAI deal

- **Q1 2026 (reported 2026-04-29):** AWS revenue **$37.6B, +28% YoY —
  fastest growth in 15 quarters**; operating income $14.2B (37.7%
  margin). **Backlog (RPO) hit $364B, up from $244B** the prior quarter
  — Jassy, on the earnings call (Motley Fool transcript, primary,
  **high**): *"The backlog for Q1 is $364 billion. That does not include
  the recent deal that we announced with Anthropic for over $100
  billion."* Olsavsky: *"Our AI revenue is growing triple digits year
  over year."* Custom silicon (Graviton/Trainium/Nitro combined) topped
  a **$20B annual revenue run rate**, growing triple digits YoY.
- **The AGI cuts (2026-07-22/23):** Amazon cut roles in its Artificial
  General Intelligence group — the unit behind the in-house Nova
  models — specifically **model-customization/post-training** roles,
  described across outlets (Reuters, CNBC, GeekWire, Tech Times — 4+
  converging, **high** on the core fact) as "sharpening its AI focus
  toward tools for business customers." This is Amazon's second
  AI-adjacent cut in 2026, after a ~16,000-role reduction company-wide in
  January. **On the contradiction itself:** the only explicit framing
  found in fetchable text argues coherence, not contradiction —
  "deployment beats research in the AI money race" (Startup Fortune,
  **medium**, snippet-level) — i.e., the infra spend is read as serving
  paying customers (Anthropic, OpenAI, enterprise Bedrock) rather than an
  in-house frontier-model bid, and Amazon is retreating from the latter
  while expanding the former. No source found arguing the cuts and
  capex *are* contradictory in analyst terms — the Tech Times explainer
  cited in the thread's opening entry is the piece that most directly
  poses the tension but returned 403 on every direct-fetch attempt this
  session; its headline framing stands, its analyst-level argument
  doesn't, **flagged thin.**
- **OpenAI deal — status: CONFIRMED, not rumored.** Amazon is investing
  **$50B total in OpenAI** ($15B initial + $35B conditional), alongside
  an **expanded $100B, 8-year AWS compute agreement** (superseding an
  earlier $38B deal), officially announced **2026-02-27** (About Amazon,
  primary, **high**, corroborated by CNBC/GeekWire/Axios/Business
  Insider). AWS is providing OpenAI **~2GW of Trainium capacity**
  (Trainium3 now, Trainium4 from 2027) and becomes the **exclusive
  third-party cloud distributor** for OpenAI's "Frontier" agent
  platform. This triggered a Microsoft exclusivity dispute
  (2026-03-17/19, Microsoft considered legal action since the deal
  conflicted with Microsoft's API-exclusivity terms) that **resolved
  2026-04-27** (TechCrunch, directly fetched, **high**): Microsoft's
  exclusivity ended, OpenAI can serve any product on any cloud (new
  products launch "first on Azure" unless Microsoft can't support them),
  the OpenAI-Microsoft partnership now has a hard 2032 end date, and
  OpenAI separately committed **$250B in additional Microsoft cloud
  purchases** — so this reads as OpenAI multi-clouding across AWS and
  Azure both, not an AWS-displaces-Microsoft story.
