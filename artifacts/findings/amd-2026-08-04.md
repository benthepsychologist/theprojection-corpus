# Finding — AMD — crawl 2026-08-04

**Verdict:** AMD spent the last ten months converting "credible #2 AI-GPU
challenger" from a narrative into a compute-deal backlog: three
back-to-back hyperscaler/frontier-lab agreements (OpenAI Oct-2025, Meta
Feb-2026, Anthropic Jul-2026) covering up to 14GW of future Instinct GPU
deployment, each with an AMD-stock-price-linked equity kicker attached.
The MI400/Helios platform that has to deliver on all three went from
announcement to volume-production reveal at the Jul-22/23 Advancing AI
2026 event, with Microsoft Azure and Oracle Cloud named as additional
early customers. None of the OpenAI or Meta warrants have vested yet —
both are explicitly contingent on shipment milestones and an AMD share
price as high as $600 — and Q2 2026 earnings, due today (2026-08-04)
after market close, are the first real test of whether Data Center
revenue is starting to reflect this pipeline rather than just the
announcement cycle.

## 1. The OpenAI deal — the opening move, still unvested

- **2025-10-06** — AMD and OpenAI announced a strategic partnership to
  deploy **up to 6 gigawatts of AMD Instinct GPUs** across multiple
  hardware generations, with the first 1GW deployment (AMD Instinct
  MI450-based) targeted to begin in H2 2026. AMD stock jumped ~23% same
  day.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/06/openai-amd-chip-deal-ai.html) ·
  [AMD IR](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1260/amd-and-openai-announce-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-6-gigawatts-of-amd-gpus))
- **Warrant structure**: AMD issued OpenAI a warrant for **up to 160
  million AMD shares** (~10% of shares outstanding), exercise price
  $0.01/share, vesting in tranches tied to (a) shipment milestones from
  1GW up to 6GW, (b) AMD share-price thresholds, and (c) OpenAI hitting
  technical/commercial deployment milestones. **The final tranche
  requires AMD stock to reach $600/share.** Warrants run roughly five
  years, through October 2030.
  ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/gpus/openai-signs-6gw-amd-gpu-deal))
- **As of AMD's FY2025 10-K/A (year ended Dec 27, 2025), none of the
  warrant shares had met vesting or exercise conditions** — zero
  financial-statement impact through year-end 2025.
  ([SEC EDGAR — AMD 10-K/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000021/amd-20251227.htm))
- This is the deal the board note already flags: **contingent, not
  counted as thrust until funded.** Nothing found this crawl changes
  that — the 1GW tranche is still described as an H2-2026 start, not a
  completed shipment.

## 2. The Meta deal — a second 6GW, same warrant playbook

- **2026-02-24** — AMD and Meta announced an expanded partnership to
  deploy **6 gigawatts of AMD Instinct GPUs**, days after Meta had also
  committed to millions of Nvidia GPUs — explicitly framed by outlets as
  Meta breaking Nvidia's de facto exclusivity. First-gigawatt shipments
  (custom MI450-based GPU + 6th-gen EPYC "Venice" CPUs, Helios
  rack-scale architecture, ROCm software) are scheduled to begin **H2
  2026**.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/24/meta-to-use-6gw-of-amd-gpus-days-after-expanded-nvidia-ai-chip-deal.html) ·
  [AMD IR](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1279/amd-and-meta-announce-expanded-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-6-gigawatts-of-amd-gpus))
- **Same warrant mechanic**: a second performance-based warrant for **up
  to 160 million AMD shares**, vesting on the same three-part structure
  (1GW-to-6GW shipment tranches, AMD share-price thresholds, Meta
  milestone achievement). One trade estimate put OpenAI's + Meta's
  combined warrants at ~20% of AMD's share count, exercise cost ~$3.2M
  combined against a stated potential value of ~$192B if fully vested at
  $600/share — illustrative of the scale, not an AMD-confirmed figure.
  (medium confidence, single-source figure — [x.com/ai
  thread](https://x.com/ai/status/2026396297540858360))
- No AMD-disclosed dollar value for the Meta deal; outside reporting
  (Introl) put it near **$100B** in implied hardware spend, not sourced
  to AMD or Meta directly (medium-low confidence).

## 3. The Anthropic deal — different structure: real equity, not a warrant

- **2026-07-22** — AMD and Anthropic announced a strategic partnership:
  Anthropic will deploy **up to 2 gigawatts of AMD Instinct MI450 Series
  GPUs**, with the first gigawatt beginning **H1 2027**. Separately, AMD
  committed a **strategic equity investment of up to $5 billion in
  Anthropic**, tied to deployment milestones, plus a joint engineering
  agreement to optimize AMD's ROCm software for Claude.
  ([AMD IR](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1292/amd-and-anthropic-announce-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-up-to-2-gigawatts-of-amd-instinct-mi450-series-gpus))
  Lisa Su (AMD): *"This collaboration brings together Anthropic's
  leadership in frontier AI with the full strength of AMD high-performance
  computing."* Tom Brown (Anthropic Co-founder/Chief Compute Officer):
  *"By partnering with AMD across the stack, we are securing the capacity
  we need and optimizing it for training and serving Claude."*
- **This is a real equity stake, not a warrant** — which is why it
  already counts in the board's thrust axis (the amd-node bundle,
  2026-07-28, puts thrust at ~$5.3B/yr, dominated by this $5B commitment
  under the stakes rule). It diversifies Anthropic's compute supply chain
  beyond Google TPUs/Cloud and gives AMD its second marquee frontier-lab
  customer after OpenAI.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/22/amd-anthropic-ai-chip-investment.html))

## 4. Advancing AI 2026 — MI400/Helios reach volume production

- **2026-07-22/23**, San Francisco — AMD's Advancing AI event: Lisa Su
  presented the first **volume-production** details of the **MI400
  accelerator family**, the **Helios rack-scale platform** (72 Instinct
  MI455X GPUs per rack, priced $5.0-5.5M/rack), and 6th-gen **EPYC
  Venice** CPUs.
  ([wccftech](https://wccftech.com/watch-amd-advancing-ai-2026-event-live-here/) ·
  [NAND Research](https://nand-research.com/amd-launches-helios-rack-scale-platform-mi400-series-and-strong-partnerships/))
- **Microsoft Azure confirmed the same day** it will deploy Helios racks
  — AMD expects to deliver Helios to Microsoft (and other customers)
  before end of 2026, for frontier-model inference, customer AI
  workloads, and Azure AI services.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/amd-helios-microsoft-ai-nvidia.html))
- **Oracle Cloud** committed to a rollout of **50,000 GPUs starting Q3
  2026**; **Tata Consultancy Services** (India) also named as a
  deployment/commitment partner.
  ([Oracle newsroom](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/ai-world-oracle-and-amd-expand-partnership-to-help-customers-achieve-next-generation-ai-scale-2025-10-14/) —
  original Oracle-AMD partnership dated 2025-10-14, expanded at the
  2026-07 event per follow-on coverage)
- Coverage described OpenAI's and Meta's combined commitments as **12GW**
  of AMD accelerator capacity at this point — consistent with 6GW +
  6GW from the two deals above; adding Anthropic's 2GW puts AMD's total
  disclosed forward GPU-deployment pipeline near **14GW**, none of it
  yet reflected in trailing revenue.
  ([techwireasia](https://techwireasia.com/2026/07/amd-advancing-ai-2026-helios-openai-meta-anthropic/))

## 5. China export exposure — resumed under a revenue-share, tightened again in January

- **2025-08** — AMD disclosed an estimated **~$800M revenue hit** from US
  export restrictions on its China-specific MI308 accelerator.
  ([qz.com](https://qz.com/amd-lisa-su-china-export-restrictions-commerce-department))
- **2025-08/09** — MI308 exports to China **resumed** under a
  conditions-based arrangement requiring AMD to share a portion of
  China-sales revenue with the US government (the same mechanism
  reported for Nvidia's H20).
  ([ainvest.com](https://www.ainvest.com/news/amd-resumes-mi308-ai-chip-exports-china-trump-administration-reverses-policy-2507/))
- **2026-01** — A **new case-by-case licensing regime** began requiring
  individual US approval for **MI325X** shipments to Chinese buyers —
  tighter than the MI308 revenue-share arrangement, and still in effect
  as of this crawl. Lisa Su has said China remains **~20% of AMD
  revenue** despite the restrictions. (medium confidence — single-source
  figure on the 20% number)
  ([cryptobriefing.com](https://cryptobriefing.com/amd-china-revenue-export-restrictions/))
- **Not found this crawl:** any China-policy update dated after January
  2026 — treat MI325X licensing as the current-standing regime, not
  confirmed unchanged since.

## 6. ZT Systems — acquired, then partly divested (older context, confirmed current)

- AMD's **$4.9B ZT Systems acquisition closed March 2025**; AMD had
  pre-announced (May 2025) that it would divest ZT's US data-center
  infrastructure **manufacturing** business to **Sanmina** for ~$3B
  cash+stock (incl. up to $450M contingent) — that divestiture **closed
  2025-10-27**. AMD retained ZT's rack-scale AI-solutions design and
  customer-enablement teams, which is the piece doing the Helios
  integration work described above.
  ([AMD newsroom](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-completes-divestiture-of-zt-systems-data-cente/) ·
  [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/amd-completes-49bn-acquisition-of-hyperscale-server-maker-zt-systems/))
  No Xilinx-specific integration news surfaced this crawl (that
  acquisition closed 2022; treat as settled background, not an open
  thread).
