# Kaiser vs. its therapists over AI — backstory finding

**The throughline:** this isn't a vibes backlash — it's a two-year labor
and regulatory escalation over two specific systems (the "E-Visit"
auto-triage questionnaire and unlicensed phone triage), culminating in
behavioral health's **first union strike over AI** (03-18), a formal
DMHC/DOL complaint (07-20), and a city-government hearing (07-21) —
against an employer already carrying ~$230M+ in MH penalties since 2023.
Mediation starts ~August.

## The arc

- **2023 — the predicate.** Kaiser signs the **$200M DMHC settlement**
  acknowledging insufficient BH providers/wait times. Late 2023: NorCal
  rolls out **"E-Visit"** — an online questionnaire (partial PHQ-9/GAD-7)
  auto-generating care referrals, replacing licensed triage clinicians.
  Jan 2024: phone triage shifts to unlicensed "Telephone Service
  Representatives" (<3-minute scripted calls).
  ([NUHW complaint PDF](https://nuhw.org/wp-content/uploads/NUHW-KaiserNorCalE-VisitComplaint.7.20.2026.pdf))
- **2025-02-25 — the regulator's own finding.** DMHC's 88-page
  non-routine survey cites Kaiser for failing **suicide-risk screening in
  triage** consistent with professional standards.
  ([DMHC survey](https://www.dmhc.ca.gov/desktopmodules/dmhc/medsurveys/surveys/055_nr_full%20service_022525.pdf))
- **2025 — the contract fork.** Kaiser signs "AI will assist, **not
  replace**" language with its SoCal union — then deletes the same
  paragraph from its NorCal counterproposal, telling NUHW it wants
  "flexibility" to increase AI and reduce staffing. The IBHS contract
  expires 09-30 with no successor.
  ([KQED](https://www.kqed.org/science/1999553))
- **2026-01→03 — escalation to the strike.** ULP charge over the
  unilateral triage overhaul (01-13); **92% strike authorization** by
  ~2,400 clinicians (02-23); Kaiser signs a **$31M DOL parity settlement**
  (02-10); **one-day ULP strike 03-18** — BHB calls it behavioral
  health's first union strike over AI; ~23k CNA nurses in solidarity.
  ([NUHW](https://home.nuhw.org/2026/03/23/mental-health-clinicians-hold-major-strike-with-support-from-kaiser-permanente-nurses-engineers/) ·
  [DOL](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/ebsa/ebsa20260210) ·
  [BHB podcast](https://behavioral-health-business.castos.com/episodes/inside-behavioral-healths-first-union-strike-over-ai))
- **2026-04→06 — bargaining stalls on the "Terrible Three."** Kaiser
  rejects NUHW's AI/compensation counters (04-24); its standing proposals
  (unchanged since 10-15-25): AI-replacement at will, layoffs/outsourcing
  to third-party BH contractors, "sole discretion" over clinical
  decisions. 06-04: Kaiser offers a quarterly, non-binding "Committee on
  Artificial Intelligence" — NUHW: "toothless." Parallel legislative
  track: NUHW-co-sponsored **SB 903** (would bar health plans from using
  AI instead of licensed clinicians for screening) passes the CA Senate,
  clears Assembly Privacy without opposition (cross-ref
  `state-therapy-chatbot-bans` — a practice-law in the making).
  ([NUHW bargaining updates](https://home.nuhw.org/2026/06/05/ibhs-bargaining-update-june-4-2026/))
- **2026-07-20/21 — the week the digest caught.** NUHW files a 29-page
  **formal complaint to DMHC + US DOL** (E-Visit + TSR triage as illegal
  unlicensed triage; instant unreviewed AI referrals, dated screenshots
  April–June); the **SF Board of Supervisors sits as Committee of the
  Whole** (File 260747, Sup. Chen) — clinician and expert panels; Kaiser
  declines to present; hearing is procedural ("heard and filed"). Kaiser's
  line: AI doesn't make care decisions; NUHW is "pushing a false
  narrative."
  ([complaint PDF](https://nuhw.org/wp-content/uploads/NUHW-KaiserNorCalE-VisitComplaint.7.20.2026.pdf) ·
  [SF Board agenda](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/bag072126_agenda.pdf) ·
  [BHB](https://bhbusiness.com/2026/07/21/kaiser-permanente-continues-to-face-clinician-backlash-over-ais-use-in-behavioral-health-care/))

## Open questions (feed the watch)

- **Mediation ~August** — does the AI-replacement language survive it?
- DMHC/DOL response to the 07-20 complaint (the 2025-07 predecessor
  complaint is "still unaddressed").
- **SB 903's** July Assembly hearing and floor vote — if it passes,
  Kaiser's E-Visit model becomes a statutory question, not a bargaining
  one.
- Whether other systems face the same (Kaiser Hawaii survey: 84% of
  clinicians report understaffing — the template is portable).
- Second strike risk if mediation fails.
