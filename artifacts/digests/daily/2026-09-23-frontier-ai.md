## Policy & governance

- **Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar introduced legislation to permanently ban artificial superintelligence, pause development of the most advanced AI systems until new federal rules exist, and create a Department of Artificial Intelligence** — advanced systems would then need federal approval before deployment, with penalties of up to 20 years in prison in some cases; employees of OpenAI and Google DeepMind endorsed it in their personal capacity, and the bill faces long odds in the Republican-controlled Congress. ([AP via ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Technology/wireStory/sen-bernie-sanders-unveils-bill-ban-artificial-superintelligence-136676811))

It was announced on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly.
