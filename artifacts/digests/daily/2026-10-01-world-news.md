## Ukraine

- **A Russian drone hit a school in Kyiv while children and staff sheltered, and two drones hit the Pivdennyi bridge between the city's east and west, suspending metro service and ground public transport on Thursday.** Mayor Klitschko said there were no immediate reports of casualties at the school, and the BBC reports its warhead did not explode; the BBC says Kyiv's bridges had not been hit before, and city authorities found no critical damage to the bridge. Ukraine's air force counted 3,177 Russian drone attacks in September through the 29th, up from 2,850 in August, and Britain announced 31 new sanctions on Russia, including eight people it accuses of torturing Ukrainian civilians and seven over the indoctrination of Ukrainian children.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/1/russian-drone-hits-school-in-ukraines-kyiv-as-moscow-presses-air-assault), [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmn8e8344v5qo))
