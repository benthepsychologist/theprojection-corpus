## Power & lobbying

- **USTR Ambassador Jamieson Greer published the fuller US-China trade detail this map's ledger was waiting on for today — but it is a set of RECOMMENDATIONS from a newly established "US-China Board of Trade," not final rates or an effective date.** The lists cover $30 billion of "non-sensitive" goods each way: 1,619 US items (agricultural goods, fish/seafood, logs/wood, cosmetics, medical devices, coal) proposed for entry into China, and 77 Chinese categories (small appliances, toys, holiday decorations, children's car seats, fireworks, tableware, glass/wood ornaments, soccer balls) proposed for entry into the US. Greer's own statement: "As a direct result of the strong relationship between President Trump and President Xi, the United States and China, under the auspices of the new Board of Trade, have recommended $30 billion of trade in non-sensitive goods on each side that could benefit from more favorable tariff treatment in the future." Neither the USTR release nor the paired White House release discloses an exact tariff-rate percentage or a start date — one wire report cites over 90% of products landing at "most-favored-nation" levels, unconfirmed against the primary text — and chips, EVs and batteries are explicitly excluded. This satisfies the ledger's `what_confirms` bar (a product-level list) without being the fuller rates/date detail the claim had hoped for; logged as `hit` below with that caveat carried into the evidence.
  ([USTR](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/september/ambassador-greer-issues-statement-announcement-recommendations-us-china-board-trade), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-28/us-releases-details-on-30-billion-of-goods-with-tariff-cuts), [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/28/business/us-china-tariff-cuts-60-billion-intl), [US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/us/articles/2026-09-28/us-and-china-detail-list-of-products-agreed-under-30-billion-tariff-cut-deal-after-trump-xi-meeting))
