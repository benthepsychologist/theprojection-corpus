## Today's throughline

Sunday was a day of reciprocal escalation on two fronts with the
diplomacy still formally alive underneath both. Ukraine and Russia traded
their largest drone exchange in weeks — roughly 600 Ukrainian drones at
Moscow Oblast, 822 claimed shot down nationwide, one dead and a
methodical strike on Wildberries logistics warehouses; Russia's return
barrage killed at least seven across Ukraine and set Kyiv's historic book
market on fire. Israel named the Hezbollah commander killed in Saturday's
strikes and vowed more, while confirming an eighth round of talks for
Rome on September 1. Eight Arab and Muslim-majority states issued a joint
statement blaming Israel for the collapse of the Gaza peace track on the
same day Israeli strikes killed two more in Khan Younis and Nuseirat and
a Hamas delegation landed in Cairo. The pattern across all three: the
fighting intensified without any of the negotiating tracks formally
breaking.

## Items

- **Ukraine sent roughly 600 drones at Moscow Oblast in one of the war's
  largest single barrages, killing one and gutting Wildberries'
  logistics network.** An 83-year-old man was killed and several injured
  as warehouses burned at Podolsk, Chekhov and Domodedovo; Ukraine
  reportedly disabled seven of the ten largest logistics centres of
  Wildberries, Russia's dominant e-commerce retailer, making this an
  economic-infrastructure strike rather than a purely military one.
  Russia's Defence Ministry claimed 822 drones downed nationwide.
  ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-attacks-across-ukraine-kill-at-least-7-injure-51/),
  Reuters via The Moscow Times,
  CBS News)

- **Russia's overnight return barrage killed at least seven across
  Ukraine and set Kyiv's historic book market alight.** Fifty-one people
  were injured; three were hurt in the book-market fire and four at an
  Odesa postal facility. In a related incident the same night, a Spanish
  F-18 flying NATO air policing shot down a suspected Russian drone that
  crossed into Romanian airspace near the Moldovan border — the fourth
  such NATO shootdown over Romania in this war, and the detail that makes
  this exchange more than symmetrical tit-for-tat.

  wrecking the Gaza peace track, in the sharpest collective diplomatic
