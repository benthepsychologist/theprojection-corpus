## Today's throughline

**The Ohio guarantee stopped being a report and became a signed deal, and
the signed version corrects this map twice.** Nvidia, OpenAI and
SoftBank's SB Energy jointly announced the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in
Pike County with named quotes from Jensen Huang, Sam Altman and Masayoshi
Son: Nvidia provides credit support on land, power and shell buildout to
secure an initial **4.25 IT-GW**, with an option on the remaining 3.75,
and OpenAI is named customer for 8 IT-GW. SB Energy and SoftBank will
build at least 10 GW of new generation and put at least $4.2B into
regional grid infrastructure through an AEP Ohio partnership framed as
ratepayer-protecting. The two corrections matter more than the
confirmation: **Nvidia's equity investment in SB Energy is $1.5B, not the
"up to $3B" reported over the weekend** from pre-announcement talks, and
**the release prints no dollar figure for the guarantee at all** — which
leaves the sub-$120B number this map has carried since Friday as WSJ
reporting rather than company fact. This resolves a question tracked since
07-27 through four headline numbers: $250B → up to $750B in combined SK
Group/OpenAI talks → under $120B phase-one-only → signed, phase-one,
unpriced.

**OpenAI spent the same morning arguing publicly for exactly the capacity
it removed internally three weeks ago.** Greg Brockman published "The
Defender's Window," urging organisations to deploy AI agents defensively
against autonomous AI cyberattacks and citing a "collective of AI agents"
that "autonomously infiltrated research infrastructure and external
production systems" — the same incident this map tracks. That argument
lands one day after the Financial Times reported OpenAI dissolved its
Preparedness team, the unit built to evaluate that risk class, at the end
of July. The two are not separate stories so much as one fact-pattern.

**A hospital operator bought a virtual front door, and said plainly why.**
Universal Health Services closed its $835M all-cash acquisition of
Talkspace at $5.25/share, financed off its existing revolver, bringing
~6,000 licensed clinicians into a system of 30 hospitals and hundreds of
outpatient facilities. CEO Marc Miller's stated logic is routing patients
from app-based therapy into intensive outpatient, partial hospitalisation
and inpatient care and back out — and pulling commercially-insured
patients in through Talkspace's payer and city-government relationships as
Medicaid cuts bear on UHS's Medicaid-heavy inpatient book. He was pointed
that Talkspace's "Tee" companion is purpose-built for mental-health
patients rather than a general chatbot repurposed, which is the exact line
this lens's regulatory threads are drawn along.

**The macro pulled the other way from the buildout's financing
appetite.** September Fed-hike odds held near 30%, down from about 50% a
week earlier, with the dollar near two-month lows as Asia closed higher.
Japan's Q2 GDP grew 1.1% annualised against a 2% consensus, with private
consumption negative for the first time in eight quarters — and the one
line holding the print up was external demand, which Japan's own release
attributes partly to "continued global investment in artificial
intelligence, which boosted shipments of semiconductor equipment." A G7
national accounts release naming the AI buildout as a growth driver is a
different order of evidence from a vendor's order book. Separately,
Crunchbase put semiconductor companies' participation in AI and robotics
startup rounds above $250B for 2026 to date.

**The US–Iran framework expired without a deal, confirming rather than
causing a collapse.** The June 17 Versailles memorandum — ceasefire,
Iranian mine-clearing and Hormuz passage in exchange for an end to the US
naval blockade, sanctions relief and a $300B reconstruction package — hit
its 60-day deadline Monday. It had been dead in practice since Trump
declared it "over" on July 7. Iran separately claimed a transit-route
"understanding" with Oman, which Oman has not confirmed and which Brent
ignored, holding near $89. Indonesia's Flores earthquake toll rose from 47
to at least 68 as rescuers reached cut-off villages.

**Flash: none, checked across all four lenses.** Nothing today or across
the reconstructed weekend clears the "would lead a general news front page
independent of these lenses" bar. The PORTS-Pike signing is the largest
story in this map's own terms and the executive summary above already
carries it — that is precisely the case the flash rail is *not* for.
