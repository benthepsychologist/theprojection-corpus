## Deals & filings

- **CoreWeave beat Q2 consensus on both lines — $2.575B revenue (+112%
  YoY) against a ~$2.56B estimate, and a narrower-than-expected adjusted
  per-share loss of $1.03 against the $1.20-$1.27 range this map was
  carrying — while GAAP net loss nearly doubled to $626M (~$1.14/share
  diluted) from $290M a year earlier, driven by net interest expense
  that climbed to $640M from $267M.** Backlog stood at $104B at
  quarter-end before more than $25B of net-new Q3 commitments pushed the
  total toward ~$129B; the company also disclosed over $10B in new
  unsecured debt/converts plus a separate $3.1B delayed-draw term loan
  at SOFR+450 (a 1.35x debt-service-coverage covenant from 09-30-26), on
  top of the $2.6B SOFR+550 facility this thread already tracked.
  Management raised full-year revenue guidance to $12.4-13.2B (from
  $12-13B) and guided Q3 to $3.4-3.6B; shares jumped as much as 14%
  after hours. The rising interest expense is the real-money
  confirmation this map has been waiting for: backlog growth and
  financing cost are now visibly moving together, not separately.
  ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001769628/000176962826000362/crwv-20260811.htm), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/coreweave-crwv-q2-earnings-report-2026.html), [Quartz](https://qz.com/coreweave-q2-2026-earnings-revenue-losses-interest-costs-081126))
