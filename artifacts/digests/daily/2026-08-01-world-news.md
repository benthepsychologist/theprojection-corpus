## Today's throughline

The war that was ordered on Friday was called off on Saturday night, and
the two sides do not agree that anything was agreed. Trump posted late on
08-01 that the United States was "locked and loaded" against Iran but
that he had cancelled the attack because "the perimeters of a deal has
been agreed to" — a deal he described as requiring the complete reopening
of the Strait of Hormuz and an end to Iran's nuclear threat, with Israel
joining the commitment. Within hours Iranian state media called it
"simply a new lie." A cancelled strike is the single largest thing that
happened today, and it rests entirely on one side's account.

Underneath the diplomacy, the physical war did not pause. An LNG tanker
was struck in Hormuz, Russia and Ukraine traded their heaviest exchange
of the week — Ukraine reaching a strategic bomber base 600km inside
Russia — and Spain's Ceuta crisis stopped being a Spanish story and
became an EU institutional one, with 22 heads of government writing to
demand emergency talks and two member states floating Spain's suspension
from Schengen.

## Ceuta & the European response

- **Spain's official death toll reached 67** over the course of 08-01,
  up from the 57 carried at the end of 07-31, with Sky News reporting
  local Ceuta officials putting it as high as 86 — an outlier no other
  outlet corroborated.
  ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/01/europe/spain-morocco-migrants-intl))
  <!-- k: t= e= axis=europe -->
- **Roughly 48,300 people had returned to Morocco** by the evening of
  07-31, voluntarily or via removal, and Spain described most of those
  who crossed as returned by 08-01 — while roughly 3,000 security
  personnel remained deployed in Ceuta "for as long as necessary."
  ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/08/01/migration-spain-ceuta-morocco/c24a1edc-8d81-11f1-8912-d71e69d679d7_story.html))
  <!-- k: t= e= axis=europe -->
- **A Spanish Supreme Court ruling from 2026-06-29 sits underneath the
  whole crisis** — it bars summary "hot returns" of sea arrivals,
  requiring individual immigration procedure, and is both what slows
  formal deportation now and what Morocco cites as having been
  misinterpreted by would-be crossers as an open door.
  ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/01/europe/spain-morocco-migrants-intl))
  <!-- k: t= e= axis=europe -->
- **22 EU heads of government demanded an emergency Interior Ministers
  meeting — and blamed Spain in the same letter**, accusing it of
  creating a migration "pull factor" through its mass-regularisation
  scheme. Solidarity with the member state under pressure is not what
  this letter is.
  ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/01/22-eu-leaders-call-for-emergency-talks-over-ceuta-migrant-crisis))
  <!-- k: t= e= axis=europe sev=major -->
- **Italy and Finland went further than border checks and called for
  suspending Spain from Schengen outright**, a materially harder step
  than the checks France and Italy reimposed from 07-31; Austria,
  Belgium, Czechia, Denmark and Sweden voiced support without
  implementing.
  ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/01/22-eu-leaders-call-for-emergency-talks-over-ceuta-migrant-crisis))
  <!-- k: t= e= axis=europe -->
- **Morocco denied loosening its border and rejected the pressure
  framing**, its ambassador expressing regret and citing smuggling
  networks and public misreading of the Spanish court ruling — while
  Spain's own Defence Minister publicly accused Morocco of "blackmail,"
  and a Moroccan minister tied the episode to Spain hosting Polisario
  Front leader Brahim Ghali.
  ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/01/22-eu-leaders-call-for-emergency-talks-over-ceuta-migrant-crisis))
  <!-- k: t= e= axis=europe -->
- **The US State Department raised Ceuta to a Level 3 travel advisory**,
  citing "unpredictable and dangerous security situations," and Spain
  began installing a 500-metre floating breakwater barrier at the Tarajal
  crossing.
  ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/01/europe/spain-morocco-migrants-intl))
  <!-- k: t= e= axis=europe -->
