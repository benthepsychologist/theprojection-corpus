directions on the same morning. The Houthis widened their targeting
again — a drone attack on an Aramco refinery in Jazan (fire, quickly
extinguished, no injuries) and missiles/drones on Mocha port, explicitly

Houthi-held Hajjah and Saada — while Iran's Revolutionary Guards went
