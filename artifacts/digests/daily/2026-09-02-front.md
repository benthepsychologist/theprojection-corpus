## Today's throughline

Yesterday's US strike wave on Iran hit roughly 100 targets, not the
seven-plus first reported, and included the first direct American strikes on
Iranian government tankers under a newly approved "tanker for tanker"
retaliation policy. Drones fired into the engine rooms of two tankers
anchored north of the naval blockade line; officials describe the aim as
buying "at least a month of lowered threat levels" for Hormuz shipping
through a strategy of repeatedly degrading rebuilt Iranian capability.
Roughly 40 ships transited the strait anyway. Iran had already answered by striking or
targeting four countries in one wave — Jordan, Bahrain, Kuwait and Iraq —
the first time Bahrain and Iraq have both appeared in the same response.
Today Trump moved further from a deal rather than toward one, and oil made
it a fourth straight up session at roughly $96.

Underneath the war, the long end went to a three-year high and the labour
market went soft within the same hour, arguing opposite directions on the
same September decision. The 10-year printed about 4.81%, the highest since
November 2023, inside a selloff that is genuinely global — UK, German,
French and Japanese yields all rose in the same session, which is a term
premium repricing rather than any one government's fiscal problem. Then ADP
came in at 38,000 private payrolls against a ~48,000 consensus, the softest
month since January. Neither the tape nor the coverage resolved the tension;
equities bounced modestly into it and gave part of it back.

Two long-running fights closed today, both against the direction this map
had them pointed. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick said publicly that
**"we trust Anthropic"** and that the company is "back on the right side"
with the administration, ending a dispute that ran most of 2026 through a
Pentagon supply-chain-risk designation, a presidential instruction to stop
using Claude, and export controls on two model families — **the export
controls are already lifted.** What it does not resolve: Claude is still
absent from GenAI.mil, where ChatGPT and Grok were added on 08-31, and the
stay on last week's court ruling lapses tomorrow with no notice of appeal on
the docket. Separately, Elon Musk put a specific day on Grok 4.7 for the
first time — **September 12**, at 2.1 trillion parameters — replacing a
rolling window this map's ledger has watched slip twice since July.

Two wars produced institutional responses rather than new events. Ukraine,
seven nights into sustained bombardment, **split its air-raid siren in two**
— yellow for drones, red for missiles, with Kyiv's transport running through
yellow alerts instead of stopping. That is a country conceding it cannot
shelter every night and still function, which measures what seven nights
have cost better than a casualty count does. And in Lebanon, a date this map
had logged as passed-silent turned out to be a **two-week slip**: a Lebanese
broadcaster puts the eighth Rome round at September 15-16.
