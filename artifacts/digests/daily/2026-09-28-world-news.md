## Horn of Africa

- **Ethiopia's rebel coalition struck a Tigray regional television station with a drone Monday and continued advancing in Afar toward the town of Afdeera, a week into an offensive humanitarian sources say has displaced more than 150,000 people.** The push follows Sunday's capture of the Afar town of Erebti; analysts and wire reporting describe the Afar campaign as aimed at cutting the road corridor that carries almost all of landlocked Ethiopia's imported cargo, including fuel, from the port of Djibouti — a strategic target well beyond the original Tigray front. The federal government has not announced a response to the drone strike on the TV station. This builds directly on Sunday's escalation (captured in the 09-27 digest's finalize pass: Erebti's fall and Ethiopia's army accusing both Eritrea and Sudan of backing the coalition).

## Iran / UK

- **The five British men arrested near RAF Fairford — the UK air base the US has used to strike Iran — were released on bail Monday, and counterterror police named "multiple lines of inquiry," including whether a foreign state (Iran or Russia named specifically) or a non-state group used "proxies or individuals, either knowingly or unknowingly" to carry out the suspected plot.** This corrects the 09-27 digest's own account: NPR and PBS, the sources originally cited for a claim that four of the five men were "confirmed Iranian nationals," did not in fact report any nationality — both explicitly said police had given none — and today's reporting is explicit that all five are British nationals from London in their 20s. Police said no viable explosives were found; Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood said Monday afternoon the scene is now safe, and Prime Minister Andy Burnham chaired a COBRA emergency-committee meeting Monday "to make sure we have the full picture." Iran's London embassy has "categorically" denied involvement.
  ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/28/g-s1-145245/britain-us-base-suspected-foiled-attack), [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/uk-police-say-5-men-arrested-near-a-u-s-run-air-base-will-be-released-on-bail))
