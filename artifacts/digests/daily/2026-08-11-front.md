# The Projection — 2026-08-11

## Today's throughline

A quieter day that mostly resolved into governance, personnel and
waiting. Anthropic began embedding invisible C2PA watermarks in
everything Claude produces, worldwide rather than only in Europe —
compliance with the EU AI Act's transparency code built once and shipped
everywhere, which is a different posture from geofencing it. OpenAI's
long-serving COO Brad Lightcap announced he is leaving, the latest in a
year of senior departures. A two-month-old startup founded by an xAI
co-founder raised $1.1B. And the security theme that dominated yesterday
kept going: researchers found a zero-click Zoom takeover bug using fewer
than twenty prompts against publicly available models, in under a day.

In markets, Brent extended its run to $92.54, up 5.69% and roughly 10%
across two sessions — a move now well outside noise, tracking the
Trump-Iran compensation stand-off. EPFR's weekly commentary reads the
same oil move as one of two forces pushing inflation risk back up, the
other being what it calls "chipflation": AI buildout costs in chips and
electricity feeding through into tech goods and utility bills.

**What today is waiting on.** Three dated expectations sit within
thirty-six hours and none can be called yet. CoreWeave's Q2 earnings
land at 5pm ET tonight, against a consensus loss of $1.18-$1.27 a share
on about $2.56B — and it closed a $2.6B term loan three days beforehand
at a yield that priced wider than guidance, which is context worth
holding while reading the print. Kaiser Permanente and NUHW began
mediation today over AI's role in behavioural health; both parties' own
channels were checked directly this afternoon and neither has posted a
readout, which is a confirmed absence rather than a gap. Colorado's
HB 26-1195 takes effect tomorrow, with no delay, amendment or challenge
anywhere in the legislative record.

Overseas, Iran's Hormuz negotiation moved again and again away from
signing: Tehran's foreign ministry called the Oman talks "progressing
smoothly" and confirmed the shipping-route map is agreed, while Trump
enlarged his compensation demand to cover "50 years" of alleged US
losses — a second precondition stacked on Iran's own. UNIFIL's
south-Lebanon mandate is meanwhile heading toward a year-end phase-out,
its force already down from 22,000 to about 7,000.

**Flash: active.** A magnitude 7.4 earthquake struck western Colombia
yesterday at 12:34 UTC, 110km deep, and the US Geological Survey has
assigned it a red alert — its highest impact category. A national state
of emergency is declared and international assistance is arriving.
Casualty figures are still being established. The full entry, and what is
confirmed versus reported, is on the rail.
