## Today's throughline

The Hormuz diplomatic track produced its clearest offer-and-refusal yet:
Bahrain — hit by Iranian-linked strikes on its own soil this year — refused
to even attend Monday's planned Iran-Gulf talks in Oman until Tehran restores
relations Manama cut in 2016, while Iran's president went further than any
Iranian official has before, telling Indian media the Strait of Hormuz will
reopen if the US ends its naval blockade. That is a real, concrete
linkage — stated by Pezeshkian himself — between the blockade and any
reopening, running alongside Iran's separate plan to sign an Oman shipping
corridor deal Monday regardless of whether the Gulf-wide talks happen.
Elsewhere, the war's other two fronts held their now-familiar shape: Zelensky
named Miami's December G20 as a place he'd meet Putin and the Kremlin called
it impossible, and Gaza recorded two more deaths from the low-boil
post-ceasefire strike pattern this thread has tracked for weeks — a delayed
death from an earlier gunshot wound and a separate tent strike, both in
Khan Younis.

## Diplomacy

- **Bahrain's Foreign Ministry said it will not attend Monday's planned
  Salalah, Oman meeting between Iran and Gulf Cooperation Council foreign
  ministers on Strait of Hormuz security, until diplomatic relations with
  Tehran — cut by Bahrain in 2016 — are restored.** Manama cited recent
  strikes on an ammonia tank in Bahrain and drone attacks on Saudi Arabia's
  East-West pipeline as evidence civilian infrastructure remains under
  threat, rejecting what it called a "policy of appeasement," and set four
  conditions for lasting peace: an end to attacks, accountability and
  compensation for damages, respect for sovereignty, and legal settlement of
  disputes.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/12/bahrain-says-it-will-not-participate-in-irans-proposed-hormuz-meeting),
  [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/bahrain-will-not-attend-gulf-meeting-with-iran-on-strait-of-hormuz-3001472),
  [TASS](https://tass.com/world/2186801))

- **Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian told India Today the Strait of
  Hormuz will reopen if the US ends its naval blockade and sanctions, and
  that Iran is prepared to back an "internationally recognised maritime
  framework" for the strait on that condition.** Iran's Foreign Ministry
  separately confirmed Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi will travel to Oman
  Monday to sign an agreement with Gulf counterparts establishing a joint
  Oman-Iran shipping route through Hormuz and to notify the International
  Maritime Organization. This is the clearest linkage yet, stated directly by
  Iran's own president, between the blockade and any reopening.
