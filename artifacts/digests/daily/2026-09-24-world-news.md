## Today's throughline

## Horn of Africa

## Iran

- **Yahya Rahim Safavi, a senior adviser to Iran's supreme leader Mojtaba Khamenei, warned Thursday that Iran may expand the war to the Indian Ocean if the US or Israel attacks again, saying that since the conflict has spread from the Persian Gulf and Hormuz to the Red Sea, "the front will expand even further, reaching the Indian Ocean and perhaps beyond."** He spoke in a video released by the Fars news agency. Kurdistan24 says it is the first reported instance of an adviser to the supreme leader naming the Indian Ocean and notes that the US-British base at Diego Garcia lies there, nearly 4,000 km from Iran; the statement is a threat, not an action, and no attack has been reported.
  ([Kurdistan24](https://www.kurdistan24.net/en/story/941576), [AFP via NAMPA](https://www.nampa.org/text/23023434))

## Russia / Ukraine
