## Items

- **The 60-day US–Iran memorandum of understanding expired Monday with
  no deal, formally closing a framework that had been dead in practice
  since July.** The June 17 Versailles MoU ran to 14 points — an
  immediate multi-front ceasefire covering Lebanon and Gaza, Iranian
  mine-clearing and safe passage through Hormuz, and in exchange a US
  pledge to lift its naval blockade, end sanctions and fund a $300B
  reconstruction package. It had been effectively void since Trump
  declared it "over" on July 7, following a June 25 tanker strike and a
  June 27 US retaliation. Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Esmail
  Baghaei said no talks were ever initiated, citing the US's "gross and
  widespread violation" of the memorandum. The substance here is
  confirmation, not change — worth logging precisely because a deadline
  passing is the kind of event that reads as news while resolving
  nothing.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding-expires-how-and-why-it-fell-apart),
  Washington Post,
  NBC News)

- **Indonesia's Flores earthquake death toll rose to at least 68 from 47,
  as rescuers reached previously isolated villages on Independence Day.**
  More than 200 are injured and roughly 5,000 displaced across East Nusa
  Tenggara province; nearly 250 homes were destroyed and over 1,300
  damaged, with 1,576 aftershocks recorded since Saturday's magnitude-7.7
  quake. President Prabowo Subianto led a moment of silence for the
  victims during the national Independence Day ceremony. The toll
  progression through the window — 47 → 51 → 53 → 68 — is itself the
  story: the rise tracks access to cut-off villages rather than any new
  event.
  ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/indonesia-earthquake-death-toll-flores-island-independence-day/),
  NPR)
