## Policy, regulation & legal

- **Minnesota's HF1606 "nudify" ban took effect today as scheduled** —
  prohibiting the "nudifying" of images or video of real people, with
  civil penalties up to $500,000 per violation and a private right of

  not stop it: Judge Donovan W. Frank denied the TRO on 07-31, holding
  that the company's near-three-month delay between signing and suit
