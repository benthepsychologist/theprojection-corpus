## Today's throughline

The Iran "deal" Trump announced over the weekend is not holding on the
water, and the gap between what Washington says and what Tehran says is
widening rather than closing. Trump said Monday talks were resuming and
called it Iran's "last chance"; **Iran's Foreign Ministry flatly denied any
US talks are scheduled** — spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei said "at present, we
are not having any negotiations with the US," and Iran said the only
channel open is with **Oman**, on safe shipping passage, not with
Washington. In the same window a **second Qatari-linked LNG tanker, the
GasLog Shanghai, was hit transiting the Strait of Hormuz** — the second
such vessel in roughly a month — pushing Brent above $90 intraday before
markets chose to read the day as de-escalation anyway. No strikes were
executed by either side; Trump's weekend cancellation still holds,
conditionally, and Israeli and US military officials reportedly learned of
it from his social-media post rather than through channels. This is a
claimed peace being priced against a burning tanker in the very strait the
claimed deal was meant to reopen.
