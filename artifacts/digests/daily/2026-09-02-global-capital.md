## Today's throughline

The long end went to a three-year high and the labor market went soft on
the same morning, and the market chose to read the second one. The 10-year
printed roughly **4.81%** — CNBC frames it as the highest since **November
2023**, a materially stronger claim than the "since January 2025" this map
carried two days ago — inside a selloff that is genuinely global rather than

session. Then ADP came in at **+38,000** private payrolls against a ~48,000
consensus, the softest month since January. **Those two facts argue opposite
