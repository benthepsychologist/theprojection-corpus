## Today's throughline

Ethiopia and Tigray went to war on Wednesday as Tigrayan forces seized three airports, Iran's president defied Trump at the UN, and Russia hit Kyiv with drones by day and ballistic missiles overnight. Ethiopia's government and Tigray's forces each accused the other of opening the new war, with Tigrayan fighters taking airports at Mekelle, Shire and Axum, holding the federal police there prisoner and fighting federal troops in Afar and Amhara, while a TPLF vice president called it "the beginning of the war" and IGAD, the UN and the African Union urged de-escalation. At the UN General Assembly, Iran's president Masoud Pezeshkian answered Trump's "annihilate" threat by holding up photos of Iran's assassinated former supreme leader and of schoolgirls killed in a US strike, saying "we did not bow our heads or bend the knee" while also saying "Iran extends its hands towards the world"; a senior Iranian official told Reuters the two sides remain far apart after Tuesday's Qatari-mediated talks, while Iran's neighbours began cutting flights under US aviation sanctions and Tehran threatened the airports of any that comply. Ukraine's Volodymyr Zelensky told the assembly that citizens of 47 countries are fighting for Russia, hours after Russian drones killed at least two in daylight strikes on Kyiv; Russian ballistic missiles then struck the capital in three waves overnight, killing two more and damaging a maternity hospital, and suspected Russian drones crashed in Romania and Moldova after a Russian military helicopter briefly crossed into Polish airspace from Kaliningrad. Secretary of State Marco Rubio met Russia's Lavrov, who said Moscow will not pause its war during any talks even as the US invited Vladimir Putin to December's G20 summit in Miami, and the US-backed Board of Peace unveiled a $2.45 billion Gaza reconstruction plan while Israeli strikes killed at least seven more Palestinians.
