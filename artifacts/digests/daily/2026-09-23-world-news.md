## Today's throughline

region on Wednesday, with Tigrayan forces seizing airports in Tigray and
federal officials saying Tigray's forces had launched an offensive into Afar

## Horn of Africa

- **Tigrayan forces seized airports across the Tigray region and launched an offensive against federal positions in the neighbouring Afar and Amhara regions early Wednesday, Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's adviser Getachew Reda said; Tigray's authorities say they are fighting a "defensive war" against a federal "invasion."** Ethiopian Airlines suspended flights to three northern Tigray airports, residents of Mekelle told the BBC that local forces took over its airport from federal police, and clashes were reported in Afar at Abala and Berahle. AFP reported that humanitarian staff in Afar said fighting between the TPLF and federal forces broke out at Abala and Erebti late Tuesday, and a foreign security source said the TPLF controlled Abala. The fighting comes three days after the Tigray People's Liberation Front announced an alliance with six other armed groups to remove Abiy's government, and a day after Tigrayan authorities said federal strikes killed at least 14 in Alamata; the BBC could not independently verify either side's account of who attacked first. The International Crisis Group's Alan Boswell said "if we're not all the way back into something like a full war, we are very, very close," and that Eritrea's alignment with the TPLF threatens to make it regional.
  ([Al Jazeera, AFP](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/23/ethiopian-airlines-suspends-flights-to-three-northern-tigray-airports), [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cry8zwwq21pxo), [Africanews, AFP](https://www.africanews.com/2026/09/23/ethiopia-tigray-forces-seize-airports-as-fighting-spreads-to-afar/))

## Iran

- **Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, addressing the UN General Assembly, answered Trump's speech directly: "The US president described us as terrorists. We have been the victims of terrorism," saying the US "bombed a school" and that Iran "responded, but we did not hit civilian populations."** The US delegation walked out as he spoke. On Hormuz he said "we cannot let some have free access and gain their interest from a waterway, while at the same time using that waterway to impose their aggressions upon us... to forbid us access to our own waterways," and warned that any "expansion of warfare will not result in security"; on the nuclear file, "no nuclear weapons and no limitations to peaceful nuclear technology." The Guardian's live blog had reported the speech only up to those passages when this was written, and no offer of specific terms had appeared in it.
  ([Guardian live blog](https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/sep/23/united-nations-general-assembly-unga-zelenksyy-trump-xi-china-ukraine-russia-europe-latest-news-updates))

## Russia / Ukraine

- **Russian drones struck Kyiv in daylight on Wednesday, killing at least two people and injuring dozens — 23 in early reports, 41 to 43 in later ones — setting off fires and damaging buildings across the capital, including near the central railway station, after 161 attack drones overnight.** Ukraine's air force said 119 of the drones were downed by 8am, with 18 locations hit; a Ukrainian-crewed cargo ship's captain was killed in a Russian strike in Odesa region, and a shopping-mall fire in Zaporizhzhia injured two. Zelensky had warned Kyiv of a possible new large-scale strike based on intelligence reports.
  ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/23/russia-pounds-kyiv-with-drones-killing-two-and-wounding-23-hours-before-zelenskyys-un-addr), [UA.NEWS, Ukrainian Air Force figures](https://ua.news/en/war-vs-rf/rosiia-atakuvala-ukrayinu-dronami-ppo-znishchila-ta-podavila-119-tsilei))

A Board of Peace six-month, $2.45 billion, 66-project Gaza recovery plan — temporary housing, rubble clearance, hospital repair and a multinational security force — is reported, per Axios, to be unveiled at a meeting on Wednesday.
