## Horn of Africa

- **Tigrayan forces seized airports across the Tigray region and launched an offensive against federal positions in the neighbouring Afar and Amhara regions early Wednesday, Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed's adviser Getachew Reda said; Tigray's authorities say they are fighting a "defensive war" against a federal "invasion."** Ethiopian Airlines suspended flights to three northern Tigray airports, residents of Mekelle told the BBC that local forces took over its airport from federal police, and clashes were reported in Afar at Abala and Berahle. AFP reported that humanitarian staff in Afar said fighting between the TPLF and federal forces broke out at Abala and Erebti late Tuesday, and a foreign security source said the TPLF controlled Abala. The fighting comes three days after the Tigray People's Liberation Front announced an alliance with six other armed groups to remove Abiy's government, and a day after Tigrayan authorities said federal strikes killed at least 14 in Alamata; the BBC could not independently verify either side's account of who attacked first. The International Crisis Group's Alan Boswell said "if we're not all the way back into something like a full war, we are very, very close," and that Eritrea's alignment with the TPLF threatens to make it regional.
  ([Al Jazeera, AFP](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/23/ethiopian-airlines-suspends-flights-to-three-northern-tigray-airports), [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cry8zwwq21pxo), [Africanews, AFP](https://www.africanews.com/2026/09/23/ethiopia-tigray-forces-seize-airports-as-fighting-spreads-to-afar/))

- **TPLF Vice President Amanuel Assefa told AFP "it is the beginning of the war," saying Tigrayan forces took prisoner the federal police guarding Mekelle, Shire and Axum airports and that ground forces attacked Tigray from the south and east, while the African Union called for "immediate deescalation."** A former senior TPLF official told AFP the rebels control several towns in the region and federal forces are "fast-retreating" — claims AFP could not independently confirm and Addis Ababa had not yet commented on. Conflict-monitoring group ACLED said Tigrayan forces also attacked positions in Amhara alongside the Fano militia, part of the new anti-government alliance, warning "today's episode will cause worry that the other agents in the alliance are about to act in their areas."
  ([AFP via Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/ethiopias-tigray-rebels-seize-airports-clash-with-government-forces/))

## Iran

- **Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, addressing the UN General Assembly, answered Trump's speech directly: "The US president described us as terrorists. We have been the victims of terrorism," saying the US "bombed a school" and that Iran "responded, but we did not hit civilian populations."** The US delegation walked out as he spoke. On Hormuz he said "we cannot let some have free access and gain their interest from a waterway, while at the same time using that waterway to impose their aggressions upon us... to forbid us access to our own waterways," and warned that any "expansion of warfare will not result in security"; on the nuclear file, "no nuclear weapons and no limitations to peaceful nuclear technology." The Guardian's live blog had reported the speech only up to those passages when this was written, and no offer of specific terms had appeared in it.
  ([Guardian live blog](https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/sep/23/united-nations-general-assembly-unga-zelenksyy-trump-xi-china-ukraine-russia-europe-latest-news-updates))

Iran said remaining international flights, including to Istanbul, operate as scheduled; Iraqi and Omani authorities had not commented.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/22/iran-flights-to-baghdad-muscat-cancelled-ahead-of-us-aviation-sanctions), [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/23/iran-denies-dropping-preconditions-very-productive-three-hour-un-talks-new-york))

## Russia / Ukraine

- **Russian drones struck Kyiv in daylight on Wednesday, killing at least two people and injuring dozens — 23 in early reports, 41 to 43 in later ones — setting off fires and damaging buildings across the capital, including near the central railway station, after 161 attack drones overnight.** Ukraine's air force said 119 of the drones were downed by 8am, with 18 locations hit; a Ukrainian-crewed cargo ship's captain was killed in a Russian strike in Odesa region, and a shopping-mall fire in Zaporizhzhia injured two. Zelensky had warned Kyiv of a possible new large-scale strike based on intelligence reports.
  ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/23/russia-pounds-kyiv-with-drones-killing-two-and-wounding-23-hours-before-zelenskyys-un-addr), [UA.NEWS, Ukrainian Air Force figures](https://ua.news/en/war-vs-rf/rosiia-atakuvala-ukrayinu-dronami-ppo-znishchila-ta-podavila-119-tsilei))

  ([Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/09/23/russian-military-helicopter-violates-polish-airspace/))

## Gaza & Lebanon

- **Gaza's Health Ministry said three more Palestinians were killed and 18 injured in the 24 hours to Wednesday, taking the post-ceasefire toll to 1,402 killed and 4,881 wounded and the overall war toll to 73,922.** The ministry gave no circumstances for the latest deaths.
  ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/israel-kills-3-in-gaza-raising-death-toll-from-genocide-to-73-922-health-ministry-/4066156))
  <!-- k: t=gaza-war axis=gaza -->
- **The US-backed Board of Peace unveiled a $2.45 billion, 66-project Gaza reconstruction plan Wednesday at a meeting on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly, confirming the report this map carried this morning on Axios alone; high representative Nikolay Mladenov said the plan is "not bound by a calendar" and depends on Hamas's disarmament, with full reconstruction projected at about $35bn over the medium term and $71.4bn over the next decade.** Al Jazeera's Kristen Saloomey, reporting from the announcement, said "little progress has been made on the ground" since Israel has not withdrawn troops as committed in July; separately Wednesday afternoon, Israeli strikes and gunfire killed at least seven more Palestinians across Gaza, apart from the Health Ministry's morning count — a drone strike on a vehicle in al-Mawasi near Khan Younis killed two (Osama Abu Khater and Mohammad Abu Elwan, one of whom Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said was a "senior Hamas finance official"), a strike on a vehicle in Deir el-Balah killed two more, a Gaza City strike killed one, and a man and a woman died of wounds from earlier Israeli gunfire.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/23/board-of-peace-plans-2-4bn-gaza-reconstruction-as-israel-kills-seven))

- **A massive Israeli detonation overnight in the south Lebanon border town of Khiam caused widespread damage in nearby villages and a blackout across the Hasbaya, Shebaa and Arqoub regions, Lebanese outlet Naharnet reported, and Israeli artillery later shelled several villages including with white phosphorus.** Prime Minister Nawaf Salam was in New York meeting Arab and world leaders to rally support for Lebanon, and the US Embassy said it delivered 16 million rounds of ammunition to the Lebanese Army. The report rests on Naharnet alone; no Israeli comment was found.
