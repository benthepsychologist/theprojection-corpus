## Today's throughline

The Iran war moved from shipping lanes to refineries. Saudi Aramco
facilities were struck again on Monday, with the 400,000-barrel-a-day
**Jizan** refinery hit for the third time since the summer — and Jizan
sits on the Red Sea, which is the point of it: Saudi Arabia has been
routing crude west specifically to avoid the Strait of Hormuz, so the
strike lands on the workaround rather than the chokepoint. No group had
claimed it and Aramco had not commented at this cut. In the same day the
declaratory war escalated on both sides: Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth
said the United States "will destroy (and sink)" Iranian oil tankers if
Iran fires on US vessels — past CENTCOM's own 09-05 strikes, which it
said deliberately disabled three tankers rather than sinking them to
limit spill risk — and Iran's parliament speaker Ghalibaf answered on X,
"Strike our assets and you get struck." Iran's security-council secretary
Mohsen Rezaei separately announced a "restricted zone" from the US
blockade line through Hormuz into the Gulf, with no boundaries and no
date beyond "coming days and weeks." Brent rose 1.5% to **$97.73**, its
highest since 23 July; WTI rose 1.8% to $93.10. US cash markets were
closed, so no American price has yet absorbed any of it.

The deadliest event of the day was in Lebanon. Israeli strikes on **Kfar
Roummane** destroyed a residential building overnight, killing eleven
people including two children and four women, with a paramedic killed in
a second strike — twelve dead, and at least 27 over three days per
Lebanon's health ministry, a sharp escalation past the four this map
logged for Sunday. It is also the item the run nearly missed: the first
world-news sweep ran out of time before reaching that thread and said so

find it. Elsewhere the direction was better — Ukrainian technicians
restored external power to the **Zaporizhzhia** plant after eighteen days

second night with one to run.

## 🔴 What the 09-06 finalize found — a number this map defended against correct evidence

The coverage pass produced one real miss and one genuine error, and the
error is the more important of the two.

**This map carried Brent's Friday 09-04 settlement as `$92.68` for four
days. It was `$96.28`** — a digit transposition. It entered on the 09-04
afternoon run and spread into the 09-04, 09-05 and 09-06 global-capital
digests, the 09-05 and 09-06 front digests, the 09-05 interpretation
sidecar, the `red-sea-oil-shock` timeline, `readouts.json` and the live
public site.

**The disproof was on the same line the whole time.** Recorded beside the
level were "+0.8% on the day" and "+7.6% on the week." Against the real
series — 08-28 $89.31, 09-03 $95.52, 09-04 $96.28 — those are +0.80% and
+7.8%. From $92.68 the same day would be **−3.0%**. WTI's $91.48 and +10%
week were transcribed correctly throughout, so exactly one number was
wrong. And the map **noticed**: its own secondary sources reported $95-97,
and the 09-04 run wrote a note declaring the transposed settlement
authoritative while dismissing those correct reads as "intraday or a
different contract." That note was copied onto the thread timeline. Both
are now inverted in place and marked rather than deleted.

**The lesson, worth more than the fix:** this lens prints a level and a
percentage change side by side every single day. They check each other in
one line of arithmetic, for free, and that check has never once been run.
Three oil-number errors have now reached this record in four days.
