## Today's throughline

The war has finished arguing with the oil market and started arguing with
the Fed. Brent is at $104.80–105.07 this morning, up another 3.5–3.8% from
Wednesday's $101.21 settle, and WTI at $99.63 — but the number that matters
is what sits beside it: the US 10-year at 4.926%, the highest since 2023,
with the 2-year at 4.522% and the 30-year at 5.345%, both at or near
52-week highs. Yields rose *with* oil rather than falling into it, gold
fell $58 to $4,402.54, and the VIX added 8.2% to 17.81 while equity futures
slipped ~0.7–1.0%. That combination is not a flight to safety; it is an
inflation repricing, and it lands eleven days before a rate decision where
hike odds have already moved from 49% to 60% on a strong August payroll
print. Underneath the macro, two things the map had simply missed came back
from the coverage critic: SoftBank is retiring its $40bn OpenAI-stake
bridge loan six months early, and PIF let LIV Golf go into Chapter 11 —
one loosening the AI-credit knot, one tightening the read on Saudi fiscal
