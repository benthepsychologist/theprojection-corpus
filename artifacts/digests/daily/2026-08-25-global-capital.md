## Today's throughline

**Everything in this lens this morning converges on one point: tomorrow
afternoon.** Four separate threads moved today, and every one of them is
either a preview of, or a fact positioned ahead of, Nvidia's Wednesday
Q2 print — the same convergence the frontier-ai page notes from the
supply side. This page carries the demand-side and price-side version of
the same story.

**Nvidia is in its longest losing streak since September 2022 — before
ChatGPT existed — and on the same day its own CEO admitted AI hasn't had
its breakout moment yet.** The stock closed Monday at $208.48, down
2.91%, its seventh straight decline, roughly 8% down over the run (a
materially smaller drop than 2022's 24% seven-day slide, but the same
shape of signal). On a podcast released the same day, Sam Altman said AI
has "not had the iPhone moment," comparing today's AI adoption to the
pre-iPhone Palm Treo era. Coming from OpenAI's own CEO rather than a
short-seller, that is a demand-side admission distinct from the
circular-financing mechanics this map tracks elsewhere.

**Nvidia is also reported doubling down on the financing side of the
loop.** The Information reports Nvidia is in talks for a new stake in
Perplexity AI at a valuation of more than $30bn — up 50% from roughly
$20bn a year ago — adding to a position it already holds alongside Jeff
Bezos and SoftBank. Same pattern this thread already tracks via
Poolside, SSI, Groq and Cloverleaf, one company further down the stack.

**Outside the Nvidia orbit, two threads moved on their own news.**
HUMAIN landed a new named partner — Mistral AI, in a deal described as
worth "hundreds of millions of Euros" — direct evidence that outside
demand, not only Saudi state capital, is filling its compute. And a
Houthi ballistic missile struck a Saudi VLCC off Yanbu the same day
China's Foreign Ministry gave its first on-record response to Monday's
Iran sanctions package, warning against disrupting its relationship with
Tehran.
