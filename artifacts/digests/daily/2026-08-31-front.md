## Today's throughline

**The day's biggest story for this map was signed five days ago and
arrived this morning through a machine, not a person.** Executive Order
14421 declares a national emergency over foreign-produced grid equipment
— generation turbines, large and backup generators, substation

circuit breakers — and gives the Energy Secretary until **2026-12-24** to
publish the rules that will decide which countries and companies are
actually covered. **The order's own recitals say why the threat sharpened:
"the rapid growth of advanced manufacturing, data centers, artificial
intelligence, and defense production."** The trade press that covered it
