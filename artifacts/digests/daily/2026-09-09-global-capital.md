## Today's throughline

Two balance sheets under strain both reached for outside capital and debt
in the same window, while a shooting war got sharply more direct. The US
disabled five Iranian tankers and Iran hit back at ten ships and a
Jordanian air base — the most direct US-Iranian-Navy exchange of the war
so far — and Brent crossed $100 a barrel for the first time since July on
it. Treasury tripled its long-bond buyback to $6bn, the program's first
actual operation at the expanded size, and the bond market read it as
fragility rather than relief: the 10-year hit a fresh high since October
2023 on the announcement instead of falling. Several thousand miles away
and on a much longer fuse, Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN confirmed today what
this map's `pif-ai-buildout` thread has been watching for since it opened
in August — that PIF's fiscal squeeze would eventually reach the AI
buildout itself, not just the giga-projects it already curtailed. Two
sovereign balance sheets, two different reasons, the same instrument:
lean on debt and outside capital rather than the state's own book.
