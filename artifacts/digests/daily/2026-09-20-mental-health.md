## The IIT Bombay death — the account changes

- **The account of why Sahil Wakode died has shifted from the institute's
  to his family's, and the two do not agree.** IIT Bombay's own statement
  said he had been caught using ChatGPT in an examination and that no
  punitive action was taken. His father alleges three months of casteist
  remarks by the exam invigilator, and Mumbai police have filed a first
  information report naming Professor Suryanarayana Doolla on
  abetment-of-suicide charges with Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes
  (Prevention of Atrocities) Act provisions invoked; the Crime Branch is
  reported to be considering questioning him. A campus group, the
  Ambedkar Periyar Phule Study Circle, separately disputes the institute's
  "no punitive action" claim, alleging Wakode had been threatened with a
  one-year suspension. **The institute publicly denies both the casteism
  allegation and the suspension claim.** ⚠️ Every clause here is an
  allegation or a denial and they are in direct conflict; a criminal
  investigation is open and nothing is established. This digest records
  the dispute as a dispute and does not resolve it.

## New Jersey — a bill to watch, not a new state on the list

- **New Jersey Assembly bill A-4732 was approved by the Assembly
  Children, Families and Food Security Committee on 2026-09-17.** It
  would require AI "companion" platforms — Replika- and
  Character.AI-type services specifically, not general-purpose chatbots —
  to disclose that the user is not talking to a human, repeating the
  disclosure at least every three hours, with a $15,000-per-violation
  civil penalty. Sponsored by Assemblywoman Marisa Sweeney with two
  co-sponsors, it is one of a five-bill AI package introduced 2026-03-10.
  **It has had no floor vote in either chamber and no Senate companion
  was found**, so it does not join the enacted-instrument list that
  `state-therapy-chatbot-bans` tracks for California, Connecticut,
  Georgia, New Hampshire, Washington, New York and Rhode Island — a
  committee-approved bill in one chamber is a different fact from a law.
  ⚠️ **Sourcing caveat:** `njleg.state.nj.us` and `pub.njleg.gov` both
  refused connections from this environment, so the bill text and status
  could not be read from the legislature's own site. This rests on New
  Jersey Globe and a law-firm alert quoting the bill's provisions.
  Primary verification is still owed before this is treated as settled.

## ⏳ The regulatory clock

- **All four California bills are still enrolled and unsigned on Governor
  Newsom's desk, with the 09-30 deadline ten days away** — checked
  directly against the legislature's own bill-history tables rather than
  coverage of them:

  | bill | subject | enrolled |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | **AB 1979** (Bonta) | brings consumer health chatbots under the Confidentiality of Medical Information Act | 09-01 |
  | **AB 2575** | liability for AI-caused clinical harm | 09-04 |
  | **SB 903** | bars marketing a chatbot as "therapy"; requires licensed-professional involvement | 09-04 |
  | **SB 503** (Weber Pierson) | obligations on developers and deployers of clinical-decision-support tools | 08-27 |

  No chaptering and no veto is logged for any of the four. A
  cross-check of the governor's own press-release list found four
  signings dated 09-19, none of them these bills, and nothing dated
  09-20. Taken together the four would be the most substantial state
  regulation of AI in mental-health care anywhere in the US, and they are
  all still in the same place they were a week ago.
