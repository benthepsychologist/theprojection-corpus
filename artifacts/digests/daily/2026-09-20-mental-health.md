## New Jersey — a bill to watch, not a new state on the list

- **New Jersey Assembly bill A-4732 was approved by the Assembly
  Children, Families and Food Security Committee on 2026-09-17.** It
  would require AI "companion" platforms — Replika- and
  Character.AI-type services specifically, not general-purpose chatbots —
  to disclose that the user is not talking to a human, repeating the
  disclosure at least every three hours, with a $15,000-per-violation
  civil penalty. Sponsored by Assemblywoman Marisa Sweeney with two
  co-sponsors, it is one of a five-bill AI package introduced 2026-03-10.
  **It has had no floor vote in either chamber and no Senate companion
  was found**, so it does not join the enacted-instrument list that
  `state-therapy-chatbot-bans` tracks for California, Connecticut,
  Georgia, New Hampshire, Washington, New York and Rhode Island — a
  committee-approved bill in one chamber is a different fact from a law.
  ⚠️ **Sourcing caveat:** `njleg.state.nj.us` and `pub.njleg.gov` both
  refused connections from this environment, so the bill text and status
  could not be read from the legislature's own site. This rests on New
  Jersey Globe and a law-firm alert quoting the bill's provisions.
  Primary verification is still owed before this is treated as settled.
