## Today's throughline

**Hormuz moved for the first time since the deal collapsed — but what
moved is an announcement, not an agreement.** Iran named a temporary
transit corridor through the strait and the Revolutionary Guard said
Iran and Oman had "reached agreements" on splitting transit revenue.
Oil fell roughly 6% across two days on it. Every official who described
the arrangement also disclaimed it: the coordinates are unset, the start
date is unset, the revenue mechanics are unset, and Iran's own position
is that the strait **remains closed** until Washington accepts terms.
This map has watched two Hormuz arrangements collapse mid-negotiation
this year, so the discipline that matters today is refusing to score an
announcement as a settlement — the ledger entry stays unresolved, and
the reasoning is written into it.
