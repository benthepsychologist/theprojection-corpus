sides said so.** Ukraine's long-range strikes hit Russian oil, port and
logistics targets across Samara, Krasnodar, Belgorod, Sevastopol and
occupied Luhansk; Russia struck Kyiv's rail network and, for the second
night running, sent ballistic missiles at the capital and at Boryspil,
