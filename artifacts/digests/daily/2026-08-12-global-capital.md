## Deals & filings

- **Lambda's Nvidia-GPU loan priced at a final $926 million and picked up
  a Moody's Baa2 rating — the first investment-grade-rated Term Loan B a
  private neocloud has ever carried.** The loan priced up from the
  $917M/S+375bps preliminary terms reported earlier in the day to a
  final $926M at SOFR+300bps (tightened 75bps during syndication) and
  99.5 cents, maturing 2030-12-31 with full amortization — Morgan
  Stanley led as arranger/administrative agent with MUFG as joint
  bookrunner. It funds Lambda's side of a ~$1.5B, 18,000-server contract
  under which Nvidia leases back the compute it sold — meaning Nvidia
  sits simultaneously as Lambda's investor, chip supplier, largest
  customer, and counterparty to the debt that pays for all three. The
  Bank for International Settlements flagged this exact circular
  structure — echoing Google's own TPU model — in a June 2026 warning
  that vendor-financed deals are "typically poorly disclosed, with risks
  of the same asset being pledged multiple times." A second neocloud,
  after CoreWeave, now showing this shape of borrowing — and the first
  to get an investment-grade stamp on it from a rating agency, not just
  investor demand (order books reached nearly $2B against the raise).
  ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/lambda-917-million-gpu-leveraged-loan-nvidia-morgan-stanley), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/ai-cloud-provider-lambda-prices-loan-for-nvidia-tied-chip-deal), [Yahoo Finance/Lambda release](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/lambda-prices-926-million-senior-202000064.html))
