## Today's throughline

The AI trade got the single best print it has had all year and the bond
market ignored it. Oracle's contracted backlog came in at $664bn, up $209bn
year over year, on $30bn of new AI cloud contracts closed in one quarter —
the most concrete answer yet to whether Stargate-scale commitments are real
paper or announced intentions. The stock went up 7%, CoreWeave and Nebius
followed it, and the Nasdaq managed 0.88%. Meanwhile the US 10-year touched
4.954%, its highest since October 2023, inside a genuinely global selloff
that had UK 30-year gilts at their highest since 1998 — one day after
Christine Lagarde stood up and named AI financing needs as a driver of the
long-end stress she is watching. The two facts are the same fact. What
Oracle proved is that the demand is contracted; what the curve is saying is
that somebody has to fund it, and Oracle's own free cash flow this quarter
was minus $5bn.
