## Horn of Africa

- **Phone and internet networks across Tigray were disrupted on Friday, and four sources including a diplomat told Reuters the Tigray People's Liberation Front had ordered the two telecom operators to shut them down, an order a source close to the TPLF denied, blaming the federal government.** One source said the restrictions may be a TPLF tactic to blunt the government's advantage in using battlefield drones, which rely on the mobile network; NetBlocks confirmed the disruption, and calls to Ethio telecom numbers were not going through Friday. The shutdown has revived fears of the 2020-2022 war, when a federal-government-imposed blackout accompanied famine-like conditions in Tigray; residents rushed to withdraw cash and stock supplies, and one resident of Maichew town told Reuters TPLF fighters were going house to house looking for young men (the TPLF denies forced recruitment). Ethiopia's army said it had killed 436 Tigrayan troops, wounded 220 and captured 44 in fighting since Wednesday, an army claim; the source close to the TPLF said federal troops had been put on the defensive. Tigrayan forces and allies have seized three local airports and pushed into Afar and Amhara in what they call a defensive war; Ethiopia's federal government has accused Eritrea of financially backing the TPLF and its allies, which Asmara denies. The UN and EU have called for restraint and a return to the African Union-brokered peace process.
