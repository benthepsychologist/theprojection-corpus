## Today's throughline

Wall Street ended Wednesday lower as the 10-year Treasury yield closed near 5.11%, its highest since 2007, after a hot flash PMI, a weak 5-year auction and a Fed governor's call for more rate hikes. The S&P 500 fell 0.75% to 7,706.03, the Nasdaq 1.13% to 26,936.04, retreating from Tuesday's record close, and the Dow 352 points to 51,511.59, while Brent settled up 3.9% at $103.08 a barrel and the dollar index reached its highest since late July, and Meta's Muse AI agent kept driving a selloff in travel and consumer stocks, with Expedia and Airbnb each down more than 7%.
