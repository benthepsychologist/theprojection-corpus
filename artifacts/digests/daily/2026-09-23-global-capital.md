## Today's throughline

A much stronger-than-expected September flash PMI pushed Treasury yields back
toward 5% and pulled stocks lower Wednesday morning, with the 10-year up about
7 basis points to roughly 5.04%, the S&P 500 down 0.3% and the Nasdaq down
0.5% from Tuesday's record close as of about 10am ET. S&P Global's flash
composite output index jumped to 58.4, the fastest growth in more than five
years, and input costs rose at their steepest rate in four years, which lands
directly on a Fed that just hiked and whose officials are openly arguing for
more. Oil gave back its overnight decline, with Brent near $100 to $101 after
dipping toward $98.50, and SoftBank's roughly $11bn OpenAI bond, with more than $20bn of orders
already on the book, is due to price after books close at noon ET.
