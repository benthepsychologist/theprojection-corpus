## Today's throughline

The 10-year Treasury yield jumped more than 14 basis points to 5.116% on
Wednesday, its highest since July 2007, after a hot flash PMI, a weak 5-year
auction and a Fed governor's call for more rate increases. Fed Governor
Michael Barr said further increases are likely needed, and CME FedWatch's
odds of an October hike rose to about 73% from 55% a day earlier. S&P Global's flash composite output index hit 58.4,
the fastest growth in more than five years, with input costs rising at the
steepest rate in four years; the 5-year auction cleared at 5.033% with

about 0.7% and the Nasdaq about 1% as of 3pm ET, off Tuesday's records, and
