## Yemen / Saudi-Yemen front

- **Yemen's own army (distinct from Saudi Arabia's counter-strikes already
  tracked) intensified airstrikes on Mokha, Dhubab, Khokha and Taiz as a
  stated "preparatory phase" for a ground offensive to retake the Bab
  al-Mandab coast the Houthis seized earlier this month; officials claim
  over 200 Houthi fighters killed (unverified), with 95,000+ people now
  displaced across six government-held provinces.**
  ([Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/middle-east/yemeni-army-steps-up-strikes-on-houthis-near-bab-al-mandab-ahead-of-possible-ground-offensive-3001828), [Anadolu Agency](https://aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/yemeni-army-says-more-than-20-airstrikes-hit-houthi-targets-in-central-yemen/4055653))

- **Saudi Arabia said it intercepted a Houthi drone before it reached
  Mecca's airspace and declared the holy city a "red line" — the first
  attempted Houthi target on Islam's holiest site in almost a decade, per
  Saudi-led coalition spokesman Turki al-Maliki, who said the kingdom
  "will not hesitate to take the necessary and deterrent measures."** The
  Houthis categorically denied it, calling the Saudi account
  "misinformation" and saying their targets remain oil facilities and

  Saudi F-15 shot down over Yemen's Marib governorate the same day — Al
  Jazeera says it cannot independently verify this, and Saudi Arabia has
