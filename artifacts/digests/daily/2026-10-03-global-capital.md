- **US talks with Russia on ending the war in Ukraine have expanded to include a multibillion-dollar oil deal over Lukoil's overseas oil fields, refineries and gas stations, the New York Times reported on Saturday, according to Reuters.** The deal would benefit Middle Eastern business groups with ties to US negotiators Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner and is contingent on US government and Kremlin approval; the group pursuing it includes US investor Todd Boehly, two Middle Eastern groups that have done business with Kushner's or Witkoff's family, and an arm of the US government, the Times said. Putin raised the deal at a September 5 Kremlin meeting with Witkoff and Kushner, as a way to show Russians they could do business with the United States, according to a person familiar with the meeting. The White House, Treasury and Lukoil did not respond to requests for comment outside business hours.
