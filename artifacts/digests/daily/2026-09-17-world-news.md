## Today's throughline

A UN fact-finding mission delivered this war's first independent
international legal finding against a US strike — "reasonable grounds"
to believe Washington committed war crimes in two February attacks
inside Iran, including a school in Minab, with 172+ civilian dead — and
found Iran's own government crackdown amounted to crimes against
humanity. The Pentagon confirmed it has run its own internal probe since

## Iran

- **A UN Human Rights Council fact-finding mission — chaired by jurist
  Sara Hossain — found "reasonable grounds to believe" the United States

  "clearly identifiable" school in Minab (150+ dead including 120
  children) and a sports complex in Lamerd (22 dead via scattered
