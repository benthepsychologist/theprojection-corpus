## Today's throughline

US stocks slid again Thursday morning and the 30-year Treasury yield touched its highest since 2004 after the New York Fed's John Williams called another rate hike this year reasonable, with oil higher on stalled US-Iran talks. The S&P 500 was down about 0.5% and the Nasdaq about 0.8% as of about 9:40am ET, the 10-year yield reached roughly 5.15%, and Fed funds futures put the odds of an October hike at 77.5%, up from about 53% on Wednesday morning. Oracle fell 4% to 6% after Bloomberg reported it sent the developer of its New Mexico Stargate campus a force majeure notice on a project whose $18bn loan already trades below 90 cents, JPMorgan upgraded CoreWeave on compute pricing power, and France's 10-year yield hit an 18-year high near 4.7%. Xi and Trump were due to meet at the White House later in the day; China's Commerce Ministry confirmed the first US-China talks on artificial intelligence but no summit outcome existed yet, and Treasury's 7-year auction and its 20- to 30-year buyback come this afternoon.

- **Oracle sent the Blue Owl-owned developer of its Project Jupiter data center in New Mexico a force majeure notice, Bloomberg reported Thursday, seeking to put off payments if the 2.45-gigawatt Stargate campus fails to come online in 2028 as planned.** Oracle says the project "remains on our planned schedule" and it is "fully committed to New Mexico"; Blue Owl said the notice "does not change the financial commitments to this multi-year project."
