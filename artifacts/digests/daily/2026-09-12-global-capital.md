## Today's throughline

Nvidia's role in the AI buildout has so far been funding demand before it
existed — chip sales, lease guarantees, equity stakes in the companies
buying its compute. Saturday's reporting adds a new leg: Nvidia is in
talks to anchor Anthropic's IPO itself, at the same moment Anthropic's own
raise grew to as much as $100 billion at a $2-2.3 trillion valuation. That
turns Nvidia from a financier of AI compute demand into a potential
backstop of the exit liquidity for the company on the other side of that
demand — the same circular-financing question this map has tracked in
`ai-circular-financing-risk`, now showing up at the public-market stage
rather than only the private one.

## Deals & filings

- **Nvidia is in talks to become an anchor investor in Anthropic's IPO,
  weighing up to $10 billion, as Anthropic's own raise has grown to as
  much as $100 billion at a $2-2.3 trillion valuation — the largest IPO in
  history if it lands on those terms.** An anchor investor commits to buy
  a substantial share of an offering before it is marketed more broadly;
  this would be Nvidia's first anchor role in a frontier lab's listing,
  distinct from the direct equity stakes (Nebius, Naver, Intel, Groq, SB
  Energy, MediaTek, Hugging Face) and lending guarantees it already holds
  across the same AI-buildout ecosystem — see `nvidia-vendor-financing`.
  Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate is now put at over $65 billion
  by end of July 2026 (up from roughly $9 billion at end of 2025), with
  the same reporting citing $190-200bn 2028 revenue projections behind the
  valuation target — not yet a company-published or audited figure. The
  offering is still targeted to price before the November US midterms.
  Terms remain under discussion and unconfirmed by either company.
  ([Bloomberg, citing Reuters](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/nvidia-in-talks-to-invest-up-to-10b-in-anthropic-ipo-reuters),
