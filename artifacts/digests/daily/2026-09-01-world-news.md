## ✏️ Correction — the Hormuz tanker strikes were neither casualty-free nor unattributed

**Both halves of what this map recorded on 08-31 are now superseded.** The
entry read that "all crew on both ships were reported safe" and that "no
government or armed group has claimed the strikes, and no US or Iranian
statement attributes them."

- **Bahri, the Saudi state shipping company, confirmed on 09-02 that two
  Filipino seafarers aboard the *Sidr* were killed.**
- **Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry has directly attributed the strike to
  Iran** — "an Iranian attack against a Saudi vessel in the Strait of
  Hormuz."

Iran's IRGC maintains its own competing account, that the vessel struck a
sea mine rather than being attacked, so the **mechanism remains disputed
between the two governments**. But "unattributed" no longer describes the
Saudi position, and the incident is not casualty-free. The 08-31 entry on
`iran-conflict-widening` and its section heading now carry the correction
in place. ([Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-907312), [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/09/02/news/2-filipino-seafarers-dead-after-monday-attack-on-saudi-oil-tanker-in-hormuz-says-shipping-firm/2416849))
