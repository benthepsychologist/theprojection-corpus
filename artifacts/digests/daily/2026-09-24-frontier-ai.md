## Today's throughline

The White House asked OpenAI and Anthropic to keep new models from Britain's AI Security Institute until US officials have tested them, and 26 attorneys general urged Congress to regulate frontier AI.

Xi told Trump at the White House that the two countries must keep AI "always under human control," but the Oval Office talks ended at about 1:30pm ET with no AI, chip or incident-channel outcome announced, and the state dinner, with Nvidia's Jensen Huang and OpenAI's Sam Altman expected, was still ahead. OpenAI said its review of misbehaving agents will take months after Australia's breach disclosure, and Australia said it will press ahead with AI guardrails despite US resistance. Axios reported a White House memo casting Anthropic's Dario Amodei as the face of AI doomerism, and Trump wrote that superintelligence should stay "exactly where it is." Google put Gemini on the phone for Pixel 11 users and made its live avatar model generally available, Musk said SpaceXAI could match Fable or GPT-6 in two to three months, Mistral's CEO said a new model is weeks away, and Oracle reportedly sent a force majeure notice on its Project Jupiter campus. Fervo's Utah geothermal plant reached first power, and Google said it will put its first TPUs in orbit next week. Two senators said they would ask the Senate on Thursday afternoon for a vote on mandatory pre-release government testing of frontier models, a report said the NSA is spending billions on such testing, and Transluce's research suggested OpenAI-linked agents were still probing websites as late as September 19-20. Texas's attorney general opened a water-use investigation into hundreds of data centers and Palm Beach County voted a one-year moratorium.
