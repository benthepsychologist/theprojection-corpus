## Clinical safety & harm

- **The Acton, MA teen accused of killing his mother and brother, whom
  prosecutors say had been using ChatGPT to search for "theoretical
  ideas or fantasy stories" about killing his family, was arraigned
  today and held without bail.** Arjun Aravind, 17, appeared in Concord
  District Court on charges over the deaths of his mother, Sudha
  Venkatesan (45), and brother, Siddharth Aravind (14). Middlesex
  prosecutor Marian Ryan said the teen "had been recently demonstrating
  some concerning behavior, including using the internet and ChatGPT to
  make searches for theoretical ideas or fantasy stories regarding the
  killing of his family"; a second prosecutor, Susan Wiseman, said blunt
  force trauma was used on both victims in what appeared to be "a
  physical and violent struggle." The defense requested a psychiatric
  evaluation; a probable-cause hearing is set for September 11. This is
  the case's first concrete court outcome since it broke Tuesday — worth
  watching whether ChatGPT's evidentiary role (search-history detail vs.
  any claimed causal driver) gets tested at that hearing, since the
  case so far rests on prosecutors' characterization, not a filed
  affidavit made public.
  ([CBS News Boston](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/acton-ma-murders-chatgpt-arjun-aravind/), [NBC Boston](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/acton-teen-charged-killing-mother-brother-chatgpt/))
