## Policy, regulation & legal

- **⚖ Maine's AI-therapy statute took effect at midnight — first in the
  nation to actually bind.** LD 2082, enacted as **Public Law Chapter
  687** (signed **04-13-2026**), bars offering "therapy or psychotherapy
  services, including through the use of Internet-based artificial
  intelligence" to the public unless delivered by a licensed
  professional; licensees may use AI only for "administrative support or
  supplementary support," retaining "full responsibility for all
  interactions, outputs and data use." Chapter number and signing date
  are primary-confirmed (legislature.maine.gov); the **07-29 effective
  date rests on Maine's default 90-days-after-adjournment rule plus
  convergent legal trackers**, not on a date printed in the act.
  ([Maine Legislature](https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/bills/display_ps.asp?LD=2082&snum=132))
  <!-- k: t=state-therapy-chatbot-bans,ai-therapy-regulatory-reckoning axis=policy-regulation -->
- **xAI sued Minnesota's attorney general to block the state's "nudify"
  ban** (effective August 2026), calling it an "overbroad, content-based
  ban on free speech" and putting its own statutory exposure at
  **"$50 billion."** AG Keith Ellison answered publicly the same day:
  "AI nudification robs the target of their dignity." First time a
  frontier lab has gone to federal court to stop an AI-harm statute
  rather than lobby against it.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/spacexs-xai-sues-minnesota-over-law-to-ban-nudify-apps-.html))
