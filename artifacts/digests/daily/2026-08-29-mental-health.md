## Today's throughline

**Today's mental-health news is a calendar, and the honest report is
what is waiting rather than what happened.** California's **AB 2575** —
the bill that would make AI developers and facilities liable for
clinical harm, which failed 18-10 on Friday and was revived 40-0 the
same day — has had **no second Senate vote**: the bill's own status page
still reads "Reconsideration granted," with two legislative days left
before Monday's adjournment. **SB 903**, which would bar marketing a
chatbot as "therapy" and require licensed-professional oversight, has not
moved since 08-21 and sits on **tomorrow's** Assembly floor file as item
103. **SB 1119**, the broadest of the children's companion-chatbot bills,
is one floor vote from passage. The three resolve within 48 hours of each
other, and the ledger now carries all three plus two more — **AB 1979**
and **SB 503** — that have already passed both chambers and wait on the
Governor by 09-30. **Five California AI-in-care instruments are live at
once, where a week ago this map was watching one.**

**The one thing that did resolve today resolved to silence.** The

today with the silence unbroken — checked a fourth time, by two

- 🕰 **AB 1979 (Bonta) passed both chambers on 08-27 and is headed to the
  Governor** — consumer health chatbots brought under the Confidentiality
  of Medical Information Act, no clinical decision on AI output alone
  without human review, mandatory AI disclaimers on generated patient
  messages and a clear route back to a human provider. Sponsored by the
  California Nurses Association, opposed by the California Hospital
  Association; distinct from AB 2575. Caught late by today's sweep and
  filed to the 08-27 timeline; the Governor's deadline is 09-30.
  ([California Legislature, AB 1979 history](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billHistoryClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB1979),
