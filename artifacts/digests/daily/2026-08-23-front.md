⚠️ **A collector defect found this morning, and it is the subtlest thing
here.** The `rss` collector falls back to **fetch time** when a feed's
items carry no `<pubDate>`, and writes that into the buffer's `ts` field
indistinguishably from a real publication date. ScienceDirect's journal
feeds are exactly this case — no `<pubDate>` anywhere, the real date
sitting in the description as prose. This morning that stamped **38
articles from *Internet Interventions* with today's date**, of which 36
are *forthcoming* September and December issue contents and exactly one is
genuinely new. **This is the re-index trap arriving through the collector
rather than through an agent** — every discipline this map has for
catching a re-dated story assumes the re-dating happens upstream in a
newsroom, not in its own buffer. Routed as an ops brief; the collector is
the research seat's code, not this repo's.
