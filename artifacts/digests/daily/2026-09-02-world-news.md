## ✏️ Correction — the Hormuz tanker strikes

**Two seafarers are dead and Saudi Arabia has named Iran.** Both halves of
what this map recorded on 08-31 are superseded: the entry said "all crew on
both ships were reported safe" and that "no government or armed group has
claimed the strikes." Bahri, the Saudi state shipping company, confirmed
today that **two Filipino seafarers aboard the *Sidr* were killed**, and
Saudi Arabia's Foreign Ministry has **directly attributed the strike to
Iran** — "an Iranian attack against a Saudi vessel in the Strait of Hormuz."
Iran's IRGC maintains its competing account that the vessel struck a sea
mine, so the **mechanism stays disputed between the two governments** — but
"unattributed" no longer describes the Saudi position. The 08-31 entry on
`iran-conflict-widening` and its section heading now carry the correction in
place.
([Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-907312), [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/09/02/news/2-filipino-seafarers-dead-after-monday-attack-on-saudi-oil-tanker-in-hormuz-says-shipping-firm/2416849))
