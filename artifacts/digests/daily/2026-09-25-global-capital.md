By about 3pm ET the S&P 500 was up roughly 0.4-0.5%, the Nasdaq up about 0.4-0.5% and the Dow up 0.8-0.9%, with the 10-year Treasury near 5.19-5.21% and the 30-year near 5.50-5.52% (still just under Thursday's multi-decade-high closes); Brent had fallen further through the day to about $104, WTI to about $92.
