## Today's throughline

Treasury yields stayed near Thursday's multi-decade highs Friday morning — the 10-year between about 5.17% and 5.21% and the 30-year between 5.46% and 5.52% on CNBC's reads — while US stocks edged higher (S&P 500 +0.3%, Nasdaq +0.4%, Dow +190 points as of about 9:45am ET) and oil fell on hopes for a phased US-Iran deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, with Brent down about 1% near $105 and WTI about 1% lower near $93. Japan's Nikkei 225 closed up 1.30% at 66,364.20 on a weaker yen and a fifth straight gain, while Hong Kong's Hang Seng fell about 1% and mainland China and South Korea stayed closed for the Mid-Autumn Festival and Chuseok holidays. August durable goods orders were essentially flat ($338.6bn, beating a forecast decline) and the University of Michigan's final September sentiment reading fell to 48.1 from 51.7, with year-ahead inflation expectations up to 4.6%. Trump and Xi's state visit closed Friday morning with tea and a National Archives tour; USTR Jamieson Greer said "a lot more details" on the US-China negotiations will come Monday, and Beijing has still not matched Bessent's Wednesday statement that the trade truce runs to January 10 with its own confirmation. Akamai, up as much as 21% premarket on Thursday's $11.6bn Anthropic deal, led movers; Meta approaches a $2 trillion market cap after last week's 16.8% rally on its Muse AI agent.
