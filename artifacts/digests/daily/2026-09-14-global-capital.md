## Power & lobbying

- **Treasury sanctioned Russia's VTB Bank — the country's second-largest
  financial institution — on Monday for helping Iran evade sanctions,
  confirming Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent's 09-11 warning that "a
  large bank" would be designated that day "to honor the memory of our
  fallen citizens on 9/11."** OFAC added VTB to the Specially Designated
  Nationals list and the Sectoral Sanctions Identifications List under
  Executive Orders 13662, 13902 and 14024, naming its Tehran branch
  specifically alongside its St. Petersburg/Moscow headquarters and New
  Delhi/Shanghai/Beijing branches; VTB was already under separate
  Ukraine-related sanctions since 2022. **This resolves this map's own
  `bessent-large-bank-iran-sanction-0914` expectation (due today) as a
  HIT** — Bessent named neither the bank nor its country when he made the
  threat on 09-11, and the eventual target was a Russian institution
  rather than the Turkish or Gulf-region bank most coverage speculated
  about (Treasury had already sanctioned a Turkish bank, Golden Global
  Yatirim Bankasi, on 09-04, a distinct, earlier action).
