## Rate decision

- **The FOMC hiked 25bp to a 3.75%-4.00% target range on a unanimous
  12-0 vote — its first hike since July 2023, and the direct answer to
  this thread's own dated ledger question of whether July's 3-dissent
  hawkish bloc (Hammack, Kashkari, Logan) would grow, hold, or fold: it
  folded into full consensus.** Chair Kevin Warsh was hawkish at the
  press conference: "This summer's inflation readings do not tell me
  that underlying trends have meaningfully improved," and on relative-
  price shocks (oil, food), "what we can do and will do is ensure that
  any change in relative prices don't broaden out." The Fed's own Summary
  of Economic Projections shows 16 of 18 participants penciling in at
  least one more hike this year. Market reaction was muted: the S&P 500
  gave back an early gain to close near flat (+0.25%), Dow +0.15%,
  Nasdaq +0.44%, as Warsh's inflation-persistence language capped the
  relief rally; the 10-year had already eased to ~4.97% into the
  decision.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html), [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/federal-reserve-interest-rate-decision-september-16-2026), [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/watch-live-warsh-holds-briefing-after-fed-meeting-as-interest-rates-expected-to-rise))
