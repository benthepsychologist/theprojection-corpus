**A missile that hurt no one produced the biggest single economic
rupture of this five-month war.** The UAE suspended all trade and
financial transactions with Iran — its second-largest trading partner
and a critical re-export hub Tehran has leaned on to absorb sanctions —
directly citing Monday's ballistic-missile incident, even as Iran
continues to deny firing anything. Underneath that, the physical picture
on the strait has flipped since midsummer: Kpler now puts 80%+ of Hormuz

together with a new Iranian general's warning that any Gulf state
assisting the US "will be considered a collaborator," the strait is
being contested state-by-state now, not just ship-by-ship. And in Gaza,
an Israeli strike killed Hamas Nukhba commanders while Hamas pointedly
kept restating its ceasefire commitment — the disarmament-track
brinkmanship holding steady even as the tactical war does not.
