## Today's throughline

Two of this map's three war threads moved in opposite directions on the
same morning. **Russia's campaign against Ukraine reached a ninth
consecutive night** — at least three killed across Odesa, Dnipro and Kyiv —
and then did something new: **a drone struck the headquarters of Ukraine's
own security service, the SBU, in Kyiv directly.** Against that, the first
Trump-envoy visit to Kyiv, planned for this weekend with Witkoff and
Kushner, is **reportedly in doubt** — Witkoff "got scared," per the
reporting, and Russia would not commit to a pause in strikes for the
visit's duration. A diplomatic first is stalling on the security
conditions the strikes themselves create.

The Israel-Lebanon track went the other way: **Israel completed the
release of all five Lebanese detainees**, and Lebanon's reciprocal
commitment is now clearer — a search for the remains of Lebanese Jewish
civilians from the civil war. Small, concrete, and the first thing on that
thread in weeks that was an exchange rather than an escalation.

On Iran, the pressure moved from the military column to the economic one.
**The EU has "officially joined" Operation Economic Outcast**, per Treasury
Secretary Bessent — converting a unilateral US sanctions campaign into a
transatlantic one, with the EU's own statement folding Iran's military
support for Russia's war into the rationale for the first time. And
**South Korea is making "concrete preparations" to send forces to the
Strait of Hormuz**, reversing a stated position of waiting until the war
ended.
