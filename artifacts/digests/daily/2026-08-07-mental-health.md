## Clinical safety & harm

- **Consumer Reports tested general-purpose AI chatbots against its own
  experts' health and safety advice and found real, confident-sounding
  errors** — e.g. Google Gemini advised putting a carbon-monoxide alarm
  inside an attached garage, which can damage the sensor; CR's guidance
  is to use AI chatbots as a starting point only, never for diagnosis,
  and to keep sensitive health details out of them (no HIPAA-equivalent
  protection). One survey cited: 17% of adults ask an AI chatbot a health
  question monthly. Widely syndicated across local-TV sites 08-07–08-08.
  ([Consumer Reports](https://www.consumerreports.org/electronics/artificial-intelligence/can-you-trust-ai-for-health-questions-a8049609288/))
