## Sanctions & economic instruments

- **Treasury revoked three standing Iran civil-aviation authorizations —
  the overflight-payment allowance, the bunkering-and-emergency-repairs
  allowance, and the temporary-sojourn licence for civil aircraft — with all
  wind-down ending 12:01 a.m. EDT on September 23.** OFAC General Licence DD,
  signed by OFAC Director Bradley T. Smith on September 8 and published in
  today's Federal Register at 91 FR 57795, authorizes only transactions
  "ordinarily incident and necessary to the wind down" of anything previously
  permitted by 31 CFR §560.522 (allowable payments for overflights of Iranian
  airspace), 31 CFR §560.529 (bunkering and emergency repairs) and Iran
  General Licence J-1 (reexportation of certain civil aircraft to Iran on
  temporary sojourn). Any payment to a blocked person must go into a blocked,
  interest-bearing US account. The first two are long-standing codified
  authorizations rather than discretionary web licences, which is what makes
  their removal notable. This completes the aviation campaign this map
  covered on 09-08, when OFAC designated 36 aviation targets including Mahan
  Air and Iran Aseman under Operation Economic Outcast: that action grounded
  the airlines, this one removes the legal basis for anyone else to fly over
  Iran, refuel there, or bring an aircraft in.
  ([Federal Register / OFAC](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/11/2026-18576/publication-of-iran-related-web-general-licenses-cc-and-dd))
  <!-- k: t=iran-conflict-widening,red-sea-oil-shock e=iran,united-states axis=policy -->

- **The same notice discloses that OFAC blocked a Turkish investment bank,
  Golden Global Yatirim Bankasi, and two affiliates on September 4 under the
  Iran sectoral-sanctions order, with wind-down ending 12:01 a.m. EDT on
  September 19.** General Licence CC covers Golden Global Yatirim Bankasi
  Anonim Sirketi, Golden Global Varlik Kiralama Anonim Sirketi, Golden Global
  Portfoy Yonetimi Anonim Sirketi and any entity they own 50% or more of. The
  designation itself went unrecorded here a week ago. It matters because E.O.
  13902 is the petroleum, petrochemical and financial-sectors order —
  designating a Turkish *bank* under it extends Operation Economic Outcast
  from Iranian entities to the third-country financial plumbing around them.
  ([Federal Register / OFAC](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/11/2026-18576/publication-of-iran-related-web-general-licenses-cc-and-dd))

## Public health

- **Congo's Ebola outbreak has reached 6,843 cases and 3,310 deaths and is
  now spreading into M23-held North Kivu — and the figures this map carried
  yesterday were less than half that.** Africa CDC warned Thursday that new
  cases in North Kivu more than doubled in the latest three-week reporting
  period, from 165 to 381, while growth finally slows in Ituri where the
  outbreak began in May. It is now the fastest-growing Ebola outbreak on
  record, running under active armed conflict, a health-worker strike, and a
  vaccine shortage — Congo has received only 70,000 of the Ervebo doses it
  needs. ⚠️ **Correction to yesterday's thread-candidate line**, which cited
  "nearly 4,000 cases and more than 1,800 deaths": those were the figures
  from an earlier reporting period. The candidate was right that this is
  unmapped; it understated it by a factor of nearly two.
  ([AP / Africa CDC, 2026-09-10](https://www.wtop.com))

  March — 1,002 confirmed deaths against 187,484 infections — making it the
  country's worst on record and, per WHO and UNICEF, currently the world's
  worst.** The death toll crossed 1,000 around 09-09. WHO and UNICEF trace
