  Ukraine — six Zaporizhstal steel-plant workers caught by a ballistic

- **A magnitude-7.4 earthquake struck western Colombia at 7:34am local
  time (12:34 GMT) Monday — the country's strongest quake this century —
  with at least 224 confirmed dead and over 2,700 more reported missing
  on citizen-run tracking sites (unconfirmed by authorities) as reporting
  continued into Tuesday.** The epicenter was San Jose Del Palmar in the
  Choco region, roughly 400km west of Bogota; Cali and Pereira were
  hardest hit, with Risaralda, Caldas and Antioquia also affected and
  about 1,600 buildings damaged or destroyed. President Abelardo de la
  Espriella declared a national state of emergency and mobilized "the
  entire military and police apparatus," saying "there are no
  distinctions or ideological divisions when it comes to defending our
  people." The US pledged $15.5 million in emergency assistance, the EU
  activated its Copernicus satellite mapping service, and El Salvador and
  Mexico offered rescue teams. This sits outside this lens's six tracked
  conflict threads — a natural disaster, not a war narrative — but is
  exactly the scale of ambient event this lens exists to register
  regardless of thread; see this session's report for a flash-rail read.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/11/colombia-quake-frantic-search-for-survivors-as-over-2700-reported-missing), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/10/7-4-magnitude-earthquake-hits-colombia-killing-at-least-20))
