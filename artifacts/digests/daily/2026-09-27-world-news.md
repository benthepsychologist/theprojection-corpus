## Yemen / Iran

- **Iran's Revolutionary Guards said Sunday they captured a second US underwater drone in the Strait of Hormuz this month — a Remus 600 autonomous vehicle they say was operating for espionage.** The IRGC navy said personnel "successfully captured an advanced unmanned underwater vehicle (UUV) belonging to the terrorist US military" through "a coordinated, complex operation involving intelligence oversight and electronic warfare," and that the vehicle is now with specialists for data recovery. The Guards made a similar claim on 09-08 about a different model, the Dive-LD. US Central Command rejected today's claim, spokesman Capt. Tim Hawkins telling reporters the US retains "positive control of all of our operational drone assets" — a fuller response than the morning digest had found; CENTCOM's own account of the earlier Dive-LD episode was that the vehicle had malfunctioned rather than been seized.

## Russia / Ukraine

- **Russian drone and missile strikes killed at least 14 people and injured 57 across Ukraine overnight into Sunday, regional officials said — the heaviest single-night toll of the past week — hours after Russia's foreign minister dismissed Saturday's rare UN meeting with his German counterpart.** The Kyiv Independent's tally, compiled from regional military administrations, counted deaths in Zaporizhzhia (4), Donetsk (3), Sumy (2, with 28 hurt including four children), Dnipropetrovsk (2), Kharkiv (2) and Odesa (1, in what officials called a "mass strike"); Kyiv city reported no deaths but multiple injuries in the capital and its suburbs, including Vyshneve and Fastiv, the Associated Press reported. Ukraine's Air Force said Russia launched 170 Shahed-type drones overnight, 76 of them jet-powered, and that air defenses downed or suppressed 147; Zelensky said Russia has fired more than 2,200 attack drones, about 1,650 aerial bombs and 38 missiles at Ukraine in the past week alone. The barrage came after Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov dismissed Saturday's meeting with German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul, saying Wadephul "simply reiterated his official position condemning Russia" and "you can judge for yourself whether that counts as dialogue or not."
  ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-attacks-kill-14-injure-57-across-ukraine-with-odesa-oblast-targeted-in-mass-strike/), [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/news/world/2026/09/27/russian-strikes-kill-2-in-ukraine-after-moscow-rejects-german-calls-to-de-escalate/))

## Horn of Africa

- **Ethiopia's army chief said Sunday the military is meeting Tigray-front "provocations" with "wisdom and restraint," even as fears grow the country is sliding back into full civil war days after a rebel coalition launched a large-scale offensive across Tigray, Afar and Amhara.** Field Marshal Berhanu Jula's comments came three days after the army said it had killed 272 Tigrayan fighters in North Wollo, a zone of Amhara bordering Tigray. The offensive, by a coalition calling itself the Ethiopian People's Forces Alliance for Survival, began 09-23 and is the most serious escalation since the Tigray telecoms blackout and the Abala/Lalibela fighting noted in the 09-26 digest's "checked, quiet" list — this is real movement, not more of the same. In the same remarks, Jula accused Eritrea of funding and supplying the rebel coalition — which fights under the banner "Tsimdo" — describing it as an extension of a longstanding pattern of hostile alliances that he said cannot challenge the state without outside backing; Eritrea has denied the accusation, citing its own wariness that landlocked Ethiopia may be seeking Red Sea access through Eritrean territory. **(finalize add) The rebel coalition captured the Afar town of Erebti on Sunday after heavy artillery bombardment and was advancing toward Afdeera, about 100km further east, a humanitarian source said; Ethiopia's army commander separately extended his accusation of foreign backing to include Sudan alongside Eritrea.** Neither government has responded to the Sudan accusation as of this writing; this is real territorial movement, not more of the restraint-statement rhetoric the morning pass found.
