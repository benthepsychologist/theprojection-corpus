## Yemen / Iran

- **Iran's Revolutionary Guards said Sunday they captured a second US underwater drone in the Strait of Hormuz this month — a Remus 600 autonomous vehicle they say was operating for espionage.** The IRGC navy said personnel "successfully captured an advanced unmanned underwater vehicle (UUV) belonging to the terrorist US military" through "a coordinated, complex operation involving intelligence oversight and electronic warfare," and that the vehicle is now with specialists for data recovery. The Guards made a similar claim on 09-08 about a different model, the Dive-LD; the US has not responded to the new claim.

## Russia / Ukraine

- **Russian drone and missile strikes killed at least 14 people and injured 57 across Ukraine overnight into Sunday, regional officials said — the heaviest single-night toll of the past week — hours after Russia's foreign minister dismissed Saturday's rare UN meeting with his German counterpart.** The Kyiv Independent's tally, compiled from regional military administrations, counted deaths in Zaporizhzhia (4), Donetsk (3), Sumy (2, with 28 hurt including four children), Dnipropetrovsk (2), Kharkiv (2) and Odesa (1, in what officials called a "mass strike"); Kyiv city reported no deaths but multiple injuries in the capital and its suburbs, including Vyshneve and Fastiv, the Associated Press reported. Ukraine's Air Force said Russia launched 170 Shahed-type drones overnight, 76 of them jet-powered, and that air defenses downed or suppressed 147; Zelensky said Russia has fired more than 2,200 attack drones, about 1,650 aerial bombs and 38 missiles at Ukraine in the past week alone. The barrage came after Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov dismissed Saturday's meeting with German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul, saying Wadephul "simply reiterated his official position condemning Russia" and "you can judge for yourself whether that counts as dialogue or not."
  ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-attacks-kill-14-injure-57-across-ukraine-with-odesa-oblast-targeted-in-mass-strike/), [AP via KSAT](https://www.ksat.com/news/world/2026/09/27/russian-strikes-kill-2-in-ukraine-after-moscow-rejects-german-calls-to-de-escalate/))

## Horn of Africa

- **Ethiopia's army chief said Sunday the military is meeting Tigray-front "provocations" with "wisdom and restraint," even as fears grow the country is sliding back into full civil war days after a rebel coalition launched a large-scale offensive across Tigray, Afar and Amhara.** Field Marshal Berhanu Jula's comments came three days after the army said it had killed 272 Tigrayan fighters in North Wollo, a zone of Amhara bordering Tigray. The offensive, by a coalition calling itself the Ethiopian People's Forces Alliance for Survival, began 09-23 and is the most serious escalation since the Tigray telecoms blackout and the Abala/Lalibela fighting noted in the 09-26 digest's "checked, quiet" list — this is real movement, not more of the same.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/27/ethiopias-army-promises-restraint-amid-fears-of-new-civil-war), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/24/ethiopian-army-says-272-tigrayan-fighters-killed-as-conflict-escalates), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/24/nx-s1-5978861/conflict-ethiopia-tigray))

- **Venezuela's interim government freed 39 political prisoners Sunday, including a 2024 opposition presidential candidate and a campaign figure for opposition leader María Corina Machado, in a second round of releases following talks between the opposition and the government installed after Nicolás Maduro's removal.** Rights group Justicia, Encuentro y Perdón said the releases included Biagio Pilieri and former electoral official Enrique Márquez, plus eight women held since 2021 on "narco-terrorism" charges; the government said as of 09-18 it still held 344 people it classifies as political prisoners, down from 391 on 08-10.
