## Today's throughline

Saudi Arabia broke its silence on the Houthi strike claims, saying the
attack on its Yanbu export hub was thwarted and a ballistic missile aimed
at Riyadh intercepted, and when CME crude reopened Sunday evening the
market sided with the barrels: Brent and WTI extended their decline into
early Monday on satellite data showing Saudi crude moving through the
Strait of Hormuz at 2.9 million barrels a day. Gulf equities, the only
market open during the day, had read the statement mildly — Saudi
Arabia's index closed down 0.3% at 10,750 while Aramco finished 1.3%
higher. In Manhattan, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Trade
Representative Jamieson Greer spent about eight hours with Vice Premier
He Lifeng; Bessent called the talks "successful" and said the two sides
discussed a "U.S.-China AI Dialogue," with the US proposing a mechanism
for notifying each other of AI incidents that reach a national-security
level, but neither side said anything publicly about the tariff truce
that expires 11-10. Overnight, SoftBank opened an $11 billion
dollar-and-euro bond sale, pricing 09-24, to fund its next $10 billion
payment into OpenAI.

## Policy & the US-China meeting

  Chase's Manhattan headquarters at about 10:30am ET Sunday, on an agenda

  guardrails.** USTR's own release frames the meeting as preparation

## 🌙 Sunday evening — the meeting concludes, and CME crude answers the Yanbu claim with actual barrels

- **Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called Sunday's meeting with China's Vice Premier He Lifeng "successful" and said the two sides had discussed a "U.S.-China AI Dialogue," with the US proposing a mechanism for notifying each other of AI incidents that "rise up to a national security level."** He framed it as moving "from opaque to more transparency" between "the number one and the number two AI powers in the world," and said the two sides agreed to meet again. Greer separately said the US and China have "operationalized" the U.S.-China Board of Trade to help identify a "critical mass of goods" — consumer goods and low-tech items from China, energy, agricultural and potentially medical goods from the US — that could be carved out from future trade measures. Neither side's post-meeting remarks addressed the 11-10 truce expiry. No joint statement or written framework was issued, and the "AI Dialogue" is a proposal, not a mechanism yet in operation. Xinhua's own readout acknowledged AI was discussed without confirming the US proposal.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/20/bessent-he-lifeng-trump-xi-summit.html), [Reuters via Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20260921/bessent-says-us-china-had-successful-meeting-with-focus-on-trade-ai))

  Investment," dedicated to domestic investment, initially overseeing 45
  state-owned enterprises — roughly a third of QIA's assets.** Announced
  by Prime Minister Sheikh Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al Thani. For a
