## 🔬 Research & evidence

- **Psychologist Jean Twenge testified in the Oakland Meta trial that
  daily social media use likely caused a decline in adolescent
  wellbeing** — nearly four hours of testimony citing reviewed studies
  showing increases in depression, self-harm and suicide (up to 5% in
  one 100,000-person study spanning 2010-2023), with the largest effects
  among girls aged 10-17. Meta's attorney challenged the causal claim on
  cross-examination, noting the research didn't break down effects by
  specific app and sometimes conflated social media with other tech use.
  ([Courthouse News Service](https://courthousenews.com/meta-questions-psychologists-testimony-in-teen-mental-health-harms-trial/))
