✅ **The "arithmetically impossible" number was not an error at all, and
this map was wrong to call it one.** For two runs this digest reported
that the 08-26 5-year auction could not be stated because TreasuryDirect
returned an amount-accepted exceeding the amount offered. Reading
Treasury's own PDF explains it exactly: the public offering was **$70bn**
and the subtotal accepted is **$70,000,046,900** — but the release then
adds a **SOMA line of $8,998,900,900**, the Federal Reserve's System Open
Market Account rolling maturing holdings into the new issue on top of the
public offering, for a **total accepted of $78,998,947,800**. An API
consumer comparing that total against the announced $70bn offering size
sees an impossible number that is in fact entirely correct. **The
anomaly was a reading error on this end, not bad data**, and the same
structure is present in the 7-year release (a $5,656,451,900 SOMA add-on
lifting $44.000bn accepted to a $49.657bn total).

**The lesson, corrected:** the press-release PDF is the primary source
because it *shows the SOMA line as a separate row*, which is the piece
that makes the arithmetic legible. Both mirrors of the PDF — on
treasurydirect.gov and fiscaldata.treasury.gov — were diffed and are
byte-identical. All figures above were re-extracted from the PDFs
directly on this pass, not taken on a sweep's word.

**What the numbers say:** a 2.50 bid-to-cover with indirect bidders
taking $26.7bn of the $43.9bn competitive award is solid demand with the
award concentrated away from dealers — a data point against, not for,
the fiscal-dominance framing `treasury-long-end-intervention` tracks.
