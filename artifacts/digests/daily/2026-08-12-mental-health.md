## Policy, regulation & legal

- **Colorado's HB 26-1195 took effect today — the country's first law
  requiring a real, licensed therapist to stay in the loop for AI use in
  psychotherapy, with no delay, amendment, or legal challenge found.**
  The law bars AI from directly conducting therapeutic communication,
  generating unreviewed treatment recommendations, or performing
  unsupervised diagnosis/emotional-state detection; it still allows AI
  for administrative/documentation support, but only under a clinician's
  full responsibility throughout. It took effect by mechanical operation
  of law (signed June 3, effective 90 days after the legislature's May
  13 adjournment) rather than a specific enactment date — yesterday's
  digest already verified no delay/amendment/challenge on the legislative
  record, so today's development is simply the law actually binding.
  Colorado's separate, broader AI Act (SB 26-189) remains under its own
  federal preliminary-injunction fight (xAI v. Colorado); legal trackers
  confirm this bill and its companion HB 26-1139 are unaffected by that
  fight.
  ([Colorado General Assembly](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/HB26-1195))

- **A federal trial testing whether Meta and TikTok designed their
  platforms to addict and harm young users' mental health opened jury
  selection in Oakland today, after the Ninth Circuit refused to block
  it.** The consolidated case combines claims from Colorado, Kentucky,
  California, and New Jersey that the platforms were designed to keep
  minors hooked while misrepresenting their safety, plus a separate
  claim from 29 states that Meta illegally collected children's data.
  Jury selection is today; opening statements start 08-18, with Mark
  Zuckerberg and Instagram head Adam Mosseri both expected to testify
  across a seven-week trial — the largest test yet of youth
  social-media-harm litigation reaching an actual jury rather than
  settling first. It arrives one week after a separate New Mexico state
  court already ordered Meta to pay $567M into a youth-mental-health
  treatment fund (screening, prevention, referral, and care-coordination
  spending, not individual compensation) after finding the company
  created a public nuisance — two different courts now testing
  materially the same underlying claim at the same time, with Meta
  appealing the New Mexico judgment.
  ([Claims Journal](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/08/12/339459.htm), [PBS NewsHour, New Mexico ruling](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/new-mexico-court-orders-meta-to-pay-567-million-over-mental-health-harms-to-kids-online))

## Research & evidence

- **Definium's LSD-based drug hit its primary endpoint in a Phase 3
  generalized-anxiety trial — the second positive pivotal readout for
  the same compound in two months.** The 214-patient Voyage trial tested
  DT120 (an orally-disintegrating lysergide tablet) against placebo over
  12 weeks; anxiety symptoms (HAM-A scale) fell 11.6 points on drug
  versus 6.2 on placebo, a 5.4-point placebo-adjusted gap Definium's CEO
  called larger than any pivotal anxiety trial he's seen ("we had never
  seen a pivotal study deliver greater than five-point placebo-adjusted
  change on the HAM-A"). It follows Definium's June Phase 3 win in major
  depressive disorder for the same drug — a second psychedelic-adjacent
  compound (after Compass's psilocybin) now with two separate positive
  Phase 3 readouts in two different indications, with a second GAD trial
  due in September.
  ([STAT News](https://www.statnews.com/2026/08/12/definium-lsd-treatment-anxiety-study/), [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/definium-hits-another-ph-3-homerun-psychedelic-sea-change-anxiety))

## Clinical safety & harm

- **Middlesex County's district attorney has publicly attributed a
  17-year-old's double homicide of his mother and 14-year-old brother in
  Acton, Massachusetts, in part to his ChatGPT use — the first case this
  lens has recorded where a prosecutor ties a general-purpose AI chatbot,
  rather than a therapy or companion product, to a completed act of
  lethal violence.** Arjun Aravind, 17, is charged with two counts of
  murder plus assault-and-battery and vehicle-theft counts after his
  mother Sudha Venkatesan, 45, and brother Siddarth Aravind, 14, were
  found dead in their Acton home the evening of 08-11 (a 6:37pm
  welfare-check call); he was arrested the following morning in Wayland.
  DA Marian Ryan said Aravind "had recently been demonstrating some
  concerning behavior, including using the internet and ChatGPT to make
  searches for theoretical ideas or fantasy stories regarding the
  killing of his family" — a separate account has her describing him
  "creating characters asking what about this, what about that, which
  appear to relate to threats to his family not surviving." Arraignment
  is scheduled for today (08-13) in Concord District Court; no outcome
  yet. This is a materially different pattern from the suicide-focused
  Character.AI/Grok wrongful-death cases already on this map — a chatbot
  allegedly used to rehearse violent fantasy against others, not a
  companion relationship implicated in self-harm — and broke within this
  digest-day but was missed entirely by the original pass, caught only
  on this finalize sweep.
  ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/08/12/metro/acton-teenager-taken-into-custody/), [Boston 25 News](https://www.boston25news.com/news/local/acton-teen-accused-killing-mother-brother-used-ai-make-disturbing-searches-da-says/DJ57OMCCTVCINKVTJPZAZ2K3NU/), [NBC Boston](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/teen-charged-with-murder-of-mom-brother-in-acton-wrote-ai-stories-about-killing-da-says/))

## 🧪 Clinical trials

Three genuinely new registrations this window, verified against the
live registry's actual `StudyFirstPostDate` (queried directly by
condition rather than relying on the broad watchlist term-match pull,
which this lens's last several passes have found runs heavily to false
positives): **a 576-patient global Phase 3 trial testing Otsuka's
SEP-363856 (ulotaront) — the trace-amine-associated-receptor agonist
that failed its schizophrenia Phase 3 in 2023 — repositioned into
generalized anxiety disorder**, fixed 50mg/75mg doses vs. placebo over 8
weeks ([NCT07759128](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07759128)) ·
**a 68-patient Phase 3 trial from AIIMS Bhubaneswar (India) comparing
maintenance rTMS against maintenance quetiapine for preventing relapse
in treatment-resistant depression** after initial rTMS response — a real
head-to-head on what should follow acute rTMS success, not just whether
rTMS works acutely
([NCT07760168](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07760168)) ·
**"STRONG Moms" — a University of Arkansas feasibility trial of
telehealth-delivered Written Exposure Therapy for PTSD in pregnant and
postpartum people receiving substance-use treatment**, 80 participants,
a real attempt at the perinatal-psychiatric-PTSD-plus-SUD intersection
the Clancy-adjacent bullet above raises as an evidence gap
([NCT07759882](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07759882)). All
routine registrations, not results.
