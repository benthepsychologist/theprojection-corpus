## Today's throughline

President Trump and six AI leaders signed a voluntary "Super Intelligence" accord after OpenAI's DevDay, as the US 30-year Treasury yield touched 5.61%, its highest since 2002. The accord, signed on Tuesday after a White House lunch with about 20 tech chiefs, commits Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, Musk's SpaceX and Nvidia to internal controls and an outside auditor but carries no legal force; Trump also signed an order making federal agencies say "Super Intelligence" instead of "AI" and gave the science adviser 60 days to propose a definition. Stocks and oil closed lower (S&P 500 7,670.84, down 0.17%; Brent's November contract $102.59, down 2.6%) while the 30-year closed at 5.59% on Treasury's par curve. Iran's Hormuz deadline lapsed with a US reply delivered through Qatari mediators rather than a strike, three ships were struck by unidentified projectiles in the strait, and Russia warned NATO it could use nuclear weapons if Kaliningrad were isolated. In mental health, Governor Newsom signed a sober-housing funding bill, California's four AI health-care bills were still unacted on at the governor's Wednesday deadline, and the VA awarded $111.9 million in suicide-prevention grants.
