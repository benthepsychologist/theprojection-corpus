## Iran

## Elsewhere (no thread yet)

- **A flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv diverted to Saudi Arabia and landed safely on Wednesday after one pilot stabbed the other, and Prime Minister Netanyahu said the attacker apparently tried to crash the plane.** Three Israeli officials told Reuters the co-pilot, identified by one as an Omani national, attacked the captain before cabin crew entered the cockpit and took control of flight FZ1073; Netanyahu said a passenger and a crew member overpowered the pilot, who was arrested and is being questioned by Saudi authorities. Flydubai said only that "an altercation occurred in the flight deck," and the airport at Tabuk said both pilots were hospitalised. Flightradar24 data show a descent of nearly 14,000 feet in under 30 seconds before the first emergency signal, then a hijack code (squawk 7500) per the BBC. Netanyahu named the stabbed captain as Smit Machchhar, from India, and said his fighting back saved the 174 people aboard; a passenger told Israeli media he put the attacker in a chokehold and grabbed the controls, and a replacement flydubai plane brought most passengers to Ben Gurion airport on Wednesday evening. Israel's Foreign Ministry told its ambassadors it was an "attempted terrorist attack" against Israelis, the UAE and the Abraham Accords, and said Foreign Minister Sa'ar and his UAE counterpart agreed to cooperate against "extremist elements"; Israeli officials also say it was unrelated to any mental-health issue. Israeli media report the co-pilot is Omani and had flown for flydubai about nine months; Saudi, UAE and Omani accounts of the pilots and a motive had not been published as of about 3pm ET. The claim that the pilot tried to crash the plane rests on Netanyahu's account and on Israeli officials repeating it (Defence Minister Katz called it an attempted terrorist attack); flydubai's own statement says the "underlying reasons and motives behind this event are unknown" and urges against "premature speculation," and no Saudi authority or investigator has said what the pilot intended. The nearest first-hand account is passenger Yaniv Hayun, who went into the cockpit and said the attacker was "trying to destroy the instruments in order to disable the plane"; another passenger, speaking second-hand by phone, called it a suicide attack. The flight-tracker figures for the drop differ (about 14,000 feet in under 30 seconds by one reading, 17,400 feet in under two minutes by another).
