## Iran

- **The US military completed its withdrawal from Iraq on Wednesday, with Central Command saying the last forces had left Erbil Air Base, and Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi announced the end of the coalition's anti-ISIS mission at a "Sovereignty Day" ceremony in Baghdad.** About 1,500 US personnel had remained in the Kurdistan Region after the withdrawal from the rest of Iraq; the anti-ISIS coalition headquarters moves to Jordan while US counterterrorism operations continue in Syria. Pro-Iran militias have claimed the exit as a victory: the deadline for bringing their weapons under state control, originally September 30, has been pushed to June 30, 2027 at the earliest, Kataib Hezbollah and Nujaba say they will not disarm until foreign troops leave, and the Ashab al-Kahaf group warned that from Wednesday night any foreign presence "will face a military response."

## Elsewhere (no thread yet)

- **A flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv diverted to Saudi Arabia and landed safely on Wednesday after one pilot stabbed the other, and Prime Minister Netanyahu said the attacker apparently tried to crash the plane.** Three Israeli officials told Reuters the co-pilot, identified by one as an Omani national, attacked the captain before cabin crew entered the cockpit and took control of flight FZ1073; Netanyahu said a passenger and a crew member overpowered the pilot, who was arrested and is being questioned by Saudi authorities. Flydubai said only that "an altercation occurred in the flight deck," and the airport at Tabuk said both pilots were hospitalised. Flightradar24 data show a descent of nearly 14,000 feet in under 30 seconds before the first emergency signal, then a hijack code (squawk 7500) per the BBC. Israeli officials call it an attempted "terror attack" and say it was unrelated to any mental-health issue; Saudi, UAE and Omani accounts and a motive have not been published, and Israeli media differ on the pilots' nationalities.
  ([CNBC, Reuters](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/israel-flight-diverted-saudi-arabia-brawl-pilots-flydubai.html), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cqjdv7pmj9dno), [CNA, AFP](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/flydubai-plane-diverted-israel-saudi-pilot-fight-6421401))
