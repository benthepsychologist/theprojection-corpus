## Today's throughline

The Ohio guarantee stopped being a report and became a signed deal, and
the signed version is materially different from the reported one. Nvidia,
OpenAI and SoftBank's SB Energy jointly announced the PORTS-Pike campus in
Pike County with named executive quotes and hard structural terms — credit
support for an initial 4.25 IT-GW with an option on the remaining 3.75,
OpenAI as customer for 8 IT-GW, and a $1.5B Nvidia equity investment into
SB Energy. Two corrections fall out of that immediately: the "$3B" SB
Energy figure reported over the weekend was pre-announcement talk and the
real number is $1.5B, and **the release prints no dollar figure at all for
the guarantee** — the sub-$120B number this map has been carrying is WSJ's
reporting, well corroborated but not company-confirmed. Underneath, the
macro moved the other way: September Fed-hike odds kept cooling while
Japan's GDP missed, leaving the AI buildout's financing appetite and the
real economy's softness pointing in opposite directions on the same
morning.

## Deals & filings

- **Nvidia, OpenAI and SoftBank signed the Ohio guarantee, and the signed
  terms correct two numbers this map was carrying.** NVIDIA's newsroom
  published a three-party joint release with quotes from Jensen Huang,
  Sam Altman and Masayoshi Son: NVIDIA provides credit support on land,
  power and shell buildout to secure an initial 4.25 IT-GW at SB Energy's
  PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, with an option on the
  remaining 3.75 IT-GW, and OpenAI named as customer for 8 IT-GW. SB
  Energy and SoftBank will build at least 10 GW of new generation and
  invest at least $4.2B in regional grid infrastructure through an AEP
  Ohio partnership framed as ratepayer-protecting; OpenAI adds $40M to SB
  Energy's existing $40M community-benefits fund. **NVIDIA invests $1.5B
  in SB Energy — not the "up to $3B" reported over the weekend, which was
  pre-announcement talk.** And the release states **no dollar figure for
  the guarantee itself**, so the sub-$120B number remains WSJ-sourced
  rather than company-confirmed. This resolves the guarantee's structure
  question this thread has tracked since 07-27, where the figure moved
  $250B (07-28) → up to $750B in combined SK Group/OpenAI talks (08-04) →
  under $120B, phase-one only (08-15) → signed, phase-one, unpriced
  (today).
  ([NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute),
  [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project))
