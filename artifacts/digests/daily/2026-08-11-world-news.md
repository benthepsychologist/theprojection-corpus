  year-end phase-out, and its role has already been sidelined from the

  22,000-strong-at-peak force (now ~7,000 as of June 2026) has been

  International Crisis Group's David Wood warned that "if the Security
  Council allows a vacuum to emerge... it could become very difficult to
  ever fill that gap once more." This is structural context for, not a
  reversal of, the stalled-talks development logged 08-10.
