## The IIT Bombay death — the institute reverses itself

- **IIT Bombay suspended Professor Suryanarayana Doolla from his post as
  Dean of Administrative Affairs and apologised for its own 09-19
  statement describing the circumstances of Sahil Wakode's death**,
  saying those details "should not have been characterised" before an
  investigation established them. Director Shireesh B. Kedare ordered the
  suspension with immediate effect; Doolla remains a faculty member in the
  Department of Energy Science and Engineering. The institute separately
  rejected circulating reports that Kedare himself had resigned. The
  case has moved to the Mumbai Crime Branch, which is examining Wakode's
  phone for digital evidence after no suicide note was found in his
  hostel room; the FIR against Doolla (abetment of suicide, with
  Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Prevention of Atrocities Act
  provisions) remains an allegation under investigation, not an
  established finding. **The IIT Bombay Faculty Forum, separately, came
  out in support of Doolla**, saying he was "discharging his duties in
  line with the institute's Senate-approved academic policies" when he
  reported a student using a phone during an exam. Mumbai Police detained
  members of a student organisation and other students staging a protest
  outside campus Monday demanding Kedare's resignation and Doolla's
  arrest. Students separately met the administration Sunday night over an
  18-point charter of demands that includes an independent investigation
  into four deaths by suicide on the campus and mental-health workshops
  for professors, not only students.
  ([The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/iit-bombay-suspends-prof-doolla-withdraws-claims-on-sahil-wakode-as-crime-branch-takes-over-probe-1503641087.html/amp), [Organiser](https://organiser.org/2026/09/21/381888/bharat/iit-bombay-suspends-dean-prof-doolla-after-sahil-wakode-suicide-crime-branch-to-probe-case/), [The Hans India / IANS](https://www.thehansindia.com/mumbai/iit-bombay-protesters-demanding-directors-resignation-professors-arrest-detained-1124090))
