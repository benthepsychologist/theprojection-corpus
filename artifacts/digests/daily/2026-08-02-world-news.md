pass run today, prompted by a stray background note in one sweep and then
independently confirmed by a second sweep working from entirely different
sources, established that the Iran conflict did not begin on 07-23 or
07-28. It began **2026-02-28**, when a US-Israeli opening strike wave

Hormuz has been effectively shut since the war's opening day, not since
last week's tanker strikes. The thread has been reframed and the gap named
— everything before 07-23 is missing and wants a `/crawl`.
