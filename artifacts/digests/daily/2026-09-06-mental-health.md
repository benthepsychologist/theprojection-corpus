## Research & evidence

- **A King's College London-affiliated team argues in an arXiv paper that
  chatbot-associated "AI psychosis" should be evaluated as a distinct
  clinical entity, describing LLM sycophancy plus anthropomorphic design as
  "an echo chamber of one" that can amplify delusional beliefs, and
  weighing formal recognition — better case identification, standard
  research criteria, regulatory pressure on developers — against reifying
  a syndrome from limited anecdotal evidence with unproven causality.**
  Au Yeung, Morrin, Ng, Kraljevic and Dobson recommend clinicians take a
  "technological history" in psychiatric assessment and call for
  pre-deployment sycophancy and delusion-reinforcement testing plus
  post-deployment monitoring. Posted 08-25 and surfaced by The Decoder's
  explainer on Sunday; distinct from the June BJPsych Open commentary
  already on the thread. On `ai-psychosis` as the first concrete proposal
  for a formal entity the thread's core question has been waiting on.
  ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23937), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/chatbots-built-an-echo-chamber-of-one-and-now-psychiatry-has-to-decide-if-ai-psychosis-exists/))
