## Today's throughline

Israel's Supreme Court cleared the Joint List, Ra'am and MK Ofer Cassif to run in the October 27 election, reversing the elections committee, a day after Balad's leader quit the race under the court's pressure. Friday's other leads were France's school protests, which closed hundreds of schools with about 2,000 detained, Netanyahu's warning of a "very heavy price" for any state behind the flydubai attack, and the G7's agreement to release 100 million barrels of oil, starting with diesel, after Europe had rejected a US diesel export ban. By Saturday morning Ethiopian forces had retaken Mekelle's airport as TPLF fighters left the Tigrayan capital, Russia had hit a second Kyiv bridge, and the UAE called the flydubai co-pilot's attack a terrorist act carried out with a crash axe.
