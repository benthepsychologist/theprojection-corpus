## Today's throughline

Israel's Supreme Court cleared the Joint List, Ra'am and MK Ofer Cassif to run in the October 27 election, reversing the elections committee, a day after Balad's leader quit the race under the court's pressure. Friday's other leads were Ethiopian government forces closing on Tigray's capital, France's school protests closing hundreds of schools with about 2,000 detained, Netanyahu's warning of a "very heavy price" for any state behind the flydubai attack, and the G7's agreement to release 100 million barrels of oil, starting with diesel, after Europe had rejected a US diesel export ban. No public Iranian answer to the US reply and no strike on Iran had been reported by mid-afternoon, but a tanker leaving the Gulf was hit by a projectile in the Strait of Hormuz on Friday and Russian drones struck Kyiv's Southern Bridge a fourth time in two days.
