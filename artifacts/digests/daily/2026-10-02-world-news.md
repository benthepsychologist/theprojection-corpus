## Elsewhere (no thread yet)

- **Israel's Supreme Court on Friday overturned the Central Elections Committee's bans on the Joint List, Ra'am and MK Ofer Cassif, clearing all three to run in the October 27 election, and dismissed the case against Balad's Sami Abu Shehadeh after his withdrawal.** The nine-justice panel was unanimous on both slates, finding that the evidence did not meet the case-law standard for disqualifying a list; on Cassif, the Hadash lawmaker, it ruled 7-2 to let him run, with Justices Mintz and Stein dissenting, while all nine called his statements "outrageous" and "repugnant" and the majority held they did not reach the strict threshold for barring a candidate. The court released a summary and said full reasoning would follow; it said that in Abu Shehadeh's case the emerging outcome, before he withdrew, was to uphold his disqualification. Israel National News, Ynet and the Jerusalem Post (14:06 Israel time) carry the court's summary, which says the full reasoning will be published separately; the ruling text itself was not read and the court's own site could not be reached. Israel National News dates Abu Shehadeh's announcement to Friday morning, AFP to Thursday, so the withdrawal's hour is unsettled.
