## Iran

- **Iran's chief negotiator, parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf, warned Tuesday that "no infrastructure will remain safe" across the region if Iran's security is not guaranteed, as Foreign Minister Araghchi left New York still awaiting Washington's formal answer on reopening Hormuz.** Araghchi told state broadcaster IRIB he expects the official US response through Qatar by the end of Tuesday; Trump rejected the seven-day proposal on Saturday, and US officials say they want a single deal that includes Iran's nuclear programme. ## Yemen
