DUV lithography tools generally — has been stalled since 2026-07-14,

and Israel provisions rather than anything to do with chips. This map

Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a developer that assembles land, power and

figure in the joint release. Its existing stakes (Nebius 9.3%, Intel
$5B, Naver $1B, Groq ~$20B licensing) are all in *customers*. This one
is in the bottleneck that gates whether those customers can deploy at
all.

conciliatory line this map has logged from Iran's leadership, arriving
