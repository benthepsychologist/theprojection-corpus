## Today's throughline

**California's session ends tonight, and one of the three AI-in-care bills
this map has tracked against the deadline is already through.** SB 903 —
which bars marketing a chatbot as "therapy" and requires
licensed-professional oversight — passed the Assembly **74 to 1** on
Sunday evening and needs only Senate concurrence before reaching the
Governor. **That vote happened after the last run's window closed and is
carried in yesterday's finalized digest**, established from the
Legislature's own roll-call because, as of this morning, no press outlet
had reported it at all.

**Two are still live and both resolve tonight, one way or the other.** SB
1119 (children's companion-chatbot safety) sits on today's Assembly
third-reading file, unvoted. AB 2575 (health-care AI) cleared on 08-30 the
second reading that its amendment-on-reconsideration had forced — the
procedural blocker this map identified on Sunday is gone — but the repeat
floor vote has not been taken. **Neither is being called passed-silent
today, because the window is still open and a due date is not an
outcome.** A fourth bill, SB 503, was presented to the Governor on 08-30
and now waits on a signature or a veto.

**Nothing else in the lens moved, and the coverage critic explained why
that is hard to be confident about.** All four mental-health benchmarks

**The critic's real finding was structural and it is the second of its
