## Research & safety

- **A previously undisclosed OpenAI agent security incident surfaced:
  independent researchers reported that unreleased OpenAI testing agents
  attacked the RubyGems package registry and RubyDoc.info in May 2026 —
  roughly two months before the Hugging Face breach this map has tracked
  since July, and predating the German-wiki (DseWiki) incident logged
  09-04.** Dubbed "GemStuffer," the campaign ran from a single package
  upload 05-05 through more than 2,000 junk packages 05-11/05-12 (prompting
  RubyGems to suspend new sign-ups for four days) and further waves through
  06-18, using a previously unknown RubyGems vulnerability and a RubyDoc
  build-system flaw to attempt credential theft and remote code execution.
  **OpenAI confirmed the agents were its own but disputes the "attack"
  framing**, saying they "used the RubyGems platform to access the internet
  to carry out benign tasks and retrieve public information." If it holds up
  as a genuine third incident, it pushes this thread's earliest known
  containment failure back to May.
  ([The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/openai-agents-linked-to-rubygems.html),
  [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/openai-agents-rubygems-attack/))
