## Yemen / Iran

- **Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian told Al Jazeera on Saturday that Iran "no longer trusts talks with Washington" but has not walked away from the table, while Tehran waited for a formal US answer to its seven-day roadmap to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.** "We have no trust in the American side and do not know on what basis an understanding can be reached with it," he said in an interview aired Saturday, adding that Qatar and Pakistan are relaying Tehran's messages and that the talks rest on the June Islamabad memorandum, which lapsed last month. The Guardian reported Trump had not given Iran an official response and that Pezeshkian was flying home from New York after meeting UN Secretary-General António Guterres; Iran's military separately warned, per the semi-official Mehr agency, that all US bases and interests in the region would be targeted if Washington "makes a mistake." Mohsen Rezaei's "four or five days" from Thursday evening runs out about 09-28/29.

## US–China after the Xi visit

- **Beijing's own account of Xi Jinping's US visit lists eight outcomes but omits the US coal purchases and the rare-earth language in the White House fact sheet, and adds that the two militaries agreed to conclude a crisis-communication memorandum "at an early date," which the White House text does not mention.** Xinhua's statement, published Saturday, also says the two leaders agreed that no country or institution should impose tolls on international waterways, but its Iran wording is softer than the White House's "Iran can never have a nuclear weapon": Beijing says Iran "should duly fulfill its commitment not to develop nuclear weapons." Both cite $30 billion of goods each way, which Beijing calls reciprocal tariff reductions and the White House calls "consensus on recommendations for more favorable tariff treatment," and both cite a dialogue with a communication channel for incidents, next round in November, which the White House calls "Super Intelligence" and Beijing "AI." China's account does not mention Russia or North Korea; the White House says the leaders discussed both. Xi landed back in Beijing Saturday. Xinhua's statement names the next meetings, APEC in Shenzhen in November and the G20 in Miami in December; the White House sheet says only that each will support the other as host and that both leaders intend to attend.
  ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/09/26/china-lists-8-outcomes-from-us-summit-with-gaps-from-white), [CNBC, citing Reuters](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/26/china-us-tariff-cut-ai-dialogue.html), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/26/china-us-to-open-ai-communication-channel-after-summit-white-house-says))

## Russia / Ukraine

- **Russian and Ukrainian strikes killed at least 10 people on Saturday, and Russia's Defence Ministry said it hit a Kyiv data centre that supports mobile internet service and Starlink satellite communications.** Russian attacks killed four across Ukraine, AP reported: two women in Zaporizhzhia and two people in Sumy. A Ukrainian drone strike in Russia's Krasnodar region killed three, local officials said, and Moscow's appointed head of the occupied part of Zaporizhzhia region said Ukrainian attacks killed three there overnight. Kyiv's mayor said five people were injured in the capital, where Russian drones hit the Cosmonova data-processing site, which had already been damaged in Friday's strike, and a Ukrainian television channel said it halted broadcasts early Saturday because of the damage; Zelensky wrote that "Russia is escalating every single day" and called for stronger responses. No date has been announced for the proposed Ukraine-Russia-US meeting in the UAE.
  ([AP via WRAL](https://www.wral.com/news/ap/50b70-7-dead-as-russia-ukraine-exchange-strikes-and-aerial-war-intensifies/), [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/russian-forces-strike-starlink-data-center-in-ukraine-12492220))
