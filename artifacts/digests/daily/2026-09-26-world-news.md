## US–China after the Xi visit

- **Beijing's own account of Xi Jinping's US visit lists eight outcomes but omits the US coal purchases and the rare-earth language in the White House fact sheet, and adds that the two militaries agreed to conclude a crisis-communication memorandum "at an early date," which the White House text does not mention.** Xinhua's statement, published Saturday, also says the two leaders agreed that no country or institution should impose tolls on international waterways, but its Iran wording is softer than the White House's "Iran can never have a nuclear weapon": Beijing says Iran "should duly fulfill its commitment not to develop nuclear weapons." Both cite $30 billion of goods each way, which Beijing calls reciprocal tariff reductions and the White House calls "consensus on recommendations for more favorable tariff treatment," and both cite a dialogue with a communication channel for incidents, next round in November, which the White House calls "Super Intelligence" and Beijing "AI." China's account does not mention Russia or North Korea; the White House says the leaders discussed both. Xi landed back in Beijing Saturday. Xinhua's statement names the next meetings, APEC in Shenzhen in November and the G20 in Miami in December; the White House sheet says only that each will support the other as host and that both leaders intend to attend.
