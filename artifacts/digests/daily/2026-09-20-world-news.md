## Today's throughline

Two European elections closed within four hours of each other and both
went the way of parties the continent's governing centre has tried to
contain. Russia's three-day Duma vote ended with the Central Election
Commission reporting United Russia at roughly 57% on an early count and
turnout above every post-Soviet parliamentary election since 1993 —
figures that exist only as the state's own account, in a vote its one
openly anti-war party was barred from entering. In Germany, projections
put the AfD narrowly ahead of the SPD in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern and
Chancellor Friedrich Merz's CDU at 5.0% there, a collapse in a state it
once governed, while Die Linke became the strongest party in Berlin for
the first time. Iran's military command, separately, said it had been
told the United States is preparing to resume strikes and threatened
sustained retaliation against US bases and any regional country that
assists — an Iranian claim about American intentions, not a confirmed
American plan.

## Russia-Ukraine

- **Voting in Russia's three-day Duma election closed at 8pm Moscow time,
  and the Central Election Commission's early count puts United Russia at
  roughly 57.5% with turnout reported at about 56.7% — the highest of any
  post-Soviet parliamentary election, ahead of 2021's 51.72% and 1993's
  54.81%.** The CEC's other preliminary shares, drawn from somewhere
  between 14% and 17% of precinct protocols and certain to move: the
  Communist Party 13.7–14.2%, LDPR 9.3–10.7%, New People 8–9.7%, A Just
  Russia 5.2–6.6%. CEC chair Ella Pamfilova said final results will be
  certified on Friday 09-24. ⚠️ Every figure in this bullet is the
  Russian state's account of its own election, reported through TASS;
  there is no independent count, no full-scope international observation
  mission, and no non-state turnout series to check the CEC against —
  which has been true of every number this election has produced. The
  earlier same-day marker holds as a record of the day's progression:
  turnout was 45.1% at 7:50am Moscow on the final day, up from 32.58% at
  2pm Moscow on day two.
  ([TASS, turnout](https://tass.com/politics/2190303),
  [TASS, first results](https://tass.com/politics/1340305),
  [TASS, final-day turnout](https://tass.com/politics/2190221))

## Germany — two state elections

- **Projections in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern put the AfD at 37.9%, narrowly
  ahead of the governing SPD's 35.7%, with Chancellor Friedrich Merz's CDU
  at 5.0% — a party that has held the state's premiership falling to the
  threshold at which seats disappear entirely.** Die Linke took 6.5–6.7%,
  the Greens 5.4–5.6%, and the BSW 4.9%, below the 5% threshold on both
  broadcasters' numbers. ⏱ These are Hochrechnungen — projections from
  partial counts, timestamped around 20:10 CEST — not certified results;
  the Landeswahlleiter's official preliminary count is due 09-21 at about
  12:30 CEST, and a 2.2-point gap is well inside the range a projection
  can still move. ⚠️ This would be the AfD's **third** time as strongest
  party in a German state, not its first: Thuringia in 2024 was the first,
  and Saxony-Anhalt on 2026-09-06 was both more recent and more decisive at
  43.8%. On the numbers, Saxony-Anhalt remains the larger result of the two.
  ([Tagesspiegel](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/wahl-in-mecklenburg-vorpommern-2026-wann-kommen-hochrechnungen-und-prognosen-16058943.html),
  [t-online, with ZDF and ARD figures side by side](https://www.t-online.de/nachrichten/deutschland/id_101444106/mecklenburg-vorpommern-afd-in-prognose-knapp-vor-spd-cdu-bangt.html))

- **Die Linke became the strongest party in Berlin for the first time, at

## Saudi Arabia — air defence

- **Saudi Arabia is running low on missile interceptors and has asked
  France, Britain, Pakistan and Egypt for air-defence support, regional
  officials told the Associated Press.** It is the most consequential
  detail to emerge from this escalation so far: the Saudi response to
  Houthi missile and drone fire has rested entirely on interception —
  Mecca on 09-16, Riyadh on 09-19, and the Yanbu, Taif, Baysh and Farasan
  attempts the Saudi-led coalition says it thwarted — and interception is
  a magazine-depth problem before it is anything else. Four separate
  governments being asked for help is a measure of how thin that magazine
  has become. The same AP account records the first civilian casualty of
  the exchange on 09-17, when debris from an intercepted Houthi drone
  killed a Yemeni resident of Saudi Arabia. ⚠️ Sourced to unnamed regional
  officials, not to any government on the record; no Saudi, French,
  British, Pakistani or Egyptian confirmation was found. Carried because
  the claim is specific and consequential, flagged because it is
  single-sourced and anonymous.
  ([AP via NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/19/nx-s1-5974980/saudi-arabia-houthis-attempted-attack-capital))

## Canada-France

- **Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney met French President Emmanuel
  Macron at Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon, the French archipelago off
  Newfoundland — the first official visit there by a Canadian prime
  minister and the first by a French president in over a decade — to
  agree closer cooperation on aerospace, energy, critical minerals,
  quantum computing, satellites and supercomputing.** The venue carries
  most of the meaning: a French territory in North America, chosen for a
  bilateral about technology and resource supply chains at a point when
  Ottawa is visibly widening its options beyond Washington. ⚠️ What was
  agreed is cooperation in stated areas, not a treaty or a funded
  programme, and the substance will be legible only when something is
  signed.
