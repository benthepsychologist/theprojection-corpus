## Russia-Ukraine

- **Turnout in Russia's Duma election reached 45.1% nationally as of
  7:50am Moscow time on the third and final voting day, per the Central
  Election Commission via TASS, up from 32.58% at 2pm Moscow on day
  two.** Polls close at 8pm local across most of the country — 17:00 UTC
  in Moscow — with the last closing in Kaliningrad, and TASS says first
  results are expected after 9pm Moscow time. All of that falls after
  this pass closes, so **no result of any kind exists yet**, preliminary
  or final. No independent, non-state turnout figure was found to check
  the CEC's number against, which has been true of every figure this
  election has produced.
  ([TASS](https://tass.com/politics/2190221))

- **The Moscow e-voting cyberattack has produced no further detail.** CEC
  chair Ella Pamfilova said the attacks on Moscow's remote electronic
  voting system overnight 09-19 into 09-20 were "massive and intense" but
  did not interrupt voting, and that specialists repelled them. No
  attribution has been offered by anyone, and none has surfaced since.
