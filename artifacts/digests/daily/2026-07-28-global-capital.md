## Today's throughline

The credit market moved from soft-no to loud-no: Nvidia's CDS — whose
record single-day widening was **Monday 07-27**, not today — held wide
while the equity closed flat, and Korea crashed through circuit breakers,
the AI bear-turn's two legs (US credit, Asia equity) converging the day
before FOMC. The panel still said leak, not crack: HY OAS 2.81%, just
+4bp over three sessions.

**Then the evening took the FOMC's own script away.** At 5:45pm ET Iran

Jordan — all intercepted, no casualties, but the **first strike since the
07-24 pause**, which this digest had called "quietly held" eight hours
earlier. Oil went straight back up: **Brent +4.3% to ~$87.7**. So the
hike driver the morning read as reversing is live again with hours to go
before the decision — and the market can't agree on what that means, with
CME pricing a hike near twice as likely as the prediction markets do.
