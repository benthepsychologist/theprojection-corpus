## Today's throughline

Iran rebuild and "annihilating" it, and hours later his envoys held about
three hours of mediated talks with Iran's foreign minister at the UN, the
first in-person contact since June. The same day Zelensky met Trump without

## Yemen

- **As many as 169 people were reported killed in Yemen in three days as Houthi and Saudi-backed government forces fought for the Kahboub mountains and other heights over the Red Sea coast, with thousands fleeing.** Houthi officials told AFP at least 118 died in clashes and Saudi air and artillery strikes on Taiz, al-Jawf, Marib and Saada since Sunday; government sources said at least 36 died on their side. The Houthis said a Saudi strike on Mocha on Tuesday killed six civilians, including two children, and one on a prison in al-Jawf killed nine detainees — claims Saudi Arabia has not confirmed.
  ([Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/22/yemen-houthis-battle-strategic-red-sea-heights))
  <!-- k: t=yemen-civil-war axis=yemen -->
- **Yemen's government forces said they took full control of Mount Qarfan in southern Taiz on Tuesday, killing five Houthi fighters and wounding 20, according to the Taiz military axis chief of staff.** The Houthis had not commented; the peak gives fire control over valleys between the ash-Shamaytaun and al-Waziiya districts as the Houthis push to control the wider Kahboub range, which would cut their coastal gains off from Aden's hinterland.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/23/yemen-government-forces-claim-control-of-mount-qarfan-in-taiz))

Announced Monday 09-21, reported through Tuesday and Wednesday.

## Greenland & the Americas

- **Trump, Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen and Greenland's Premier Jens-Frederik Nielsen signed a security agreement at the UN letting the US "modernise and expand" Pituffik Space Base, establish new bases at Narsarsuaq and Mestersvig, and fly and operate across Greenland's territory, waters and undersea approaches.** No non-NATO country may set up military installations or a persistent presence without unanimous approval by Denmark, Greenland and the US, investors from sensitive sectors outside NATO and the EU are barred, and the pact has no end date; Denmark and Greenland keep sovereignty, and an independent Greenland would remain in NATO. Trump described it as giving the US "permanent control over security"; Frederiksen called it "a deal that can last forever."
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/22/us-signs-tremendous-arctic-security-deal-with-denmark-greenland), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/trump-address-united-nations-general-assembly-iran-war-rcna599085))
  <!-- k: axis=arctic -->
- **Trump told the UN his administration is "seeking a fundamental change in the situation in Cuba," which he called "a failed state" that "will fall," and said Marco Rubio is "deep into negotiations with Cuba"; the Cuban delegation walked out of the hall.** He also called the January capture of Nicolás Maduro "proof that the United States will no longer permit threats to America to gain a foothold anywhere in the Western Hemisphere," and met Venezuela's interim president Delcy Rodríguez on the sidelines, their first meeting since Maduro's seizure.
