**Ukraine declared its largest counteroffensive claim of the war so far,
and three other conflict threads sharpened into their most consequential
moves yet, none resolving.** Kyiv says it has retaken 745 km² and 26

claims it downed 553 drones overnight, and Kyiv itself reports empty
supermarket shelves from the sustained strike campaign. In Lebanon,

same day Lebanese and Israeli reporting converged on a sharper fact:
Israel has besieged roughly 40 Hezbollah fighters trapped in a tunnel
network beneath the Ali al-Taher ridge, cutting their supply lines and
reportedly awaiting only a US "green light" to seize the position by
force — the most concrete step toward renewed ground combat this thread
has logged. Iran's widening war hardened on two fronts: the UAE formally
blamed Iran's Revolutionary Guard for Thursday's tanker drone attack as

escalated his own rhetoric sharply — floating that he'll "declare the
Strait of Hormuz a territory of the United States" once Iran is
"defeated" — while Treasury previewed economic measures against Iran
"never before seen." In Gaza, Jared Kushner, Nickolay Mladenov and Tony
Blair will travel to Israel and Egypt next week specifically because
Netanyahu rejected Trump's own 15-point plan, and the West Bank's Qusra
siege entered its sixth day as Defense Minister Katz moved enforcement
power over Israeli settlers from the army to the police — a change
critics call a step toward de facto annexation.
