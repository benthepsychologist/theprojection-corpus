## Deals & filings

- **Anthropic's own investors are reportedly targeting a $2 trillion
  valuation, or higher, for an October IPO — more than double the $965B
  private-market print this thread has carried since May.** The
  Financial Times reports "half a dozen" of Anthropic's backers expect
  annualized revenue to reach $100-120B by year-end (versus $47B in May)
  and are pricing an October debut around $2T, though FT's own sourcing
  says senior Anthropic executives "had not fixed a valuation target...
  even in private conversations," and the company itself declined to
  comment. Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan are leading the
  offering; Anthropic filed confidentially in June and is now in its
  quiet period, so there is no company confirmation of the number
  itself — only investor expectation.
  ([Yahoo Finance/FT](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-investors-target-2-trillion-132255261.html))
