## Today's throughline

**Three dated pressures converged on the Fed inside five hours, and none
of them resolved.** July PCE came in hot on the headline and in line on
the core; the White House's deadline for Lisa Cook to answer its removal
notice arrived and passed with no public response from her; and Canada's
retaliatory tariff schedule — set two weeks out, on 09-08 — put a dated

labour data argues for, the Cook fight removes neither a governor nor
the threat of removing one, and the tariff arrives before the meeting
rather than after it. Nvidia reports after today's close, which makes
