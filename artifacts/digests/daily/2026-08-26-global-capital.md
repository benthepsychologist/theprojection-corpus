## Today's throughline

**Three dated pressures converged on the Fed inside five hours, and none
of them resolved.** July PCE came in hot on the headline and in line on
the core; the White House's deadline for Lisa Cook to answer its removal
notice arrived and passed with no public response from her; and Canada's
retaliatory tariff schedule — set two weeks out, on 09-08 — put a dated
supply-side price shock in front of the September FOMC. **The common
structure is that each one tightens the Fed's constraint without
settling anything**: the inflation print argues against the cut the
labour data argues for, and the tariff arrives before the meeting rather
than after it. **One of the three did resolve, in the back half this
digest originally could not see: Cook filed.** Her attorneys sent a
dated written rebuttal to White House Counsel on the deadline day —
the formal response the map had spent two runs carefully declining to
call either way, correctly. Then Nvidia reported after the close and the
day's centre of gravity moved: a beat, a $108bn guide that assumes
nothing from China, and — in the 10-Q filed the same day — **$105
billion of guarantees to an OpenAI affiliate, a $25 billion equity pool
for AI labs, and $500bn+ of third-party capital lined up through Apollo,
BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman and KKR.** The
circular-financing argument this lens has tracked as an analyst thesis
is now an SEC disclosure with a number on it.
