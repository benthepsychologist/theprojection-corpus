## Today's throughline

The envoys went. Witkoff and Kushner arrived in Moscow on Saturday for
talks with Putin and are still scheduled to fly to Kyiv on Sunday, a leg

In the Gulf, the Iran war produced its first direct exchange between
Iranian missiles and US warships. The IRGC fired ballistic missiles at a

IRGC crude carriers off Kharg Island and Jask and destroying a third in

session until Tuesday. And Yemen's own civil war, distinct from the Red Sea campaign, is in its

rule that active conflicts get coverage. None of the three is a flash. All
are escalations inside running wars.

first time, that the DseWiki "wiki incident" was its agents' doing, that it

cyberdefence pledge — and the third time the sequence has run: breach,
