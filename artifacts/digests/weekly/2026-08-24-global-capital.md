## The week's throughline

**The Treasury mechanism this map named "soft-form financial repression"
two weeks ago got its own funding source identified this week, and a
brand-new thread opened for it immediately started earning its keep.**
CNBC's Steve Liesman reported the Treasury General Account sits at
roughly $935–950bn — $350-400bn above the prior administration's target
— which two officials say funds the doubled bond-buyback ceiling behind
the 30-year's 19-year-high touch. By Monday afternoon the commentary had
escalated from "repression" to the stronger claim "fiscal dominance,"
monetary policy subordinated to fiscal financing need. `treasury-long-
end-intervention`, opened last week to hold three dated tests, saw one
land directly in its lap: Lisa Cook's removal-response deadline passed
08-26 with a real filing (her counsel submitted a formal written
rebuttal) rather than the silence this map had been bracing for, while
Jackson Hole and Warsh's own keynote are still ahead. Underneath the
macro story, Anthropic's IPO pitch escalated three times in five days —
a >$100bn raise, then a $2 trillion-plus target valuation, then a claimed
>$30 trillion total addressable market — before drawing its first real
analyst pushback (NYU's Damodaran: all of the S&P 1500's tech companies
combined generate about $2.4tn in annual revenue, a twelfth of the
claimed addressable market). SoftBank stacked financing legs rather than
resting on one: its record ¥1tn retail bond got firm terms (banks
reportedly declined the institutional risk, forcing the retail route),
then a second $10-20bn wholesale leg opened two days later with existing
notes selling off the same day. And the week's other live front — the
US-Canada tariff fight — went from a one-sided US escalation to a dated
Canadian retaliation (effective 09-08) and, by Wednesday, the first
domestic political cost: Republican senators publicly breaking with the
White House over it.

## By radar question

### Q7 — Where is capital and economic power concentrating?

The macro-backdrop sub-scope produced the week's lead story a second
straight week, and this time with a named mechanism: the TGA's
$935-950bn balance, $350-400bn above target, reported as the funding
source behind the doubled buyback ceiling. The 30-year touched a
19-year high overnight (~5.33%) before pulling back to 5.228% by the
open, and commentary's framing hardened from "repression" to "fiscal
dominance" over the course of Monday. Treasury's own rebuttal — no
auction schedule changed, the first buyback op isn't until 09-09 — sits
alongside a muted actual market reaction: July's PCE ran hot on headline
(3.7% y/y) but yields barely moved, and Wednesday's 5-year auction
produced an ambiguous demand signal (2.39 bid-to-cover against a 2.46
ten-auction average — a source headlined this "above average demand"
while reporting numbers that read the opposite way, logged without
asserting a direction). All three dated tests this thread was opened to
hold are now resolving in real time: Cook's deadline landed 08-26 with
a genuine formal filing, not the silence the prior week's note
anticipated; Jackson Hole runs 08-27-29; Warsh's own keynote as chair is
08-28 — neither yet covered by a daily. On capital in AI markets: Nvidia
snapped a seven-day losing streak (its longest since before ChatGPT
existed) the day of its own print, closing +2.2%; Sam Altman said AI
has "not had the iPhone moment" the day before it; Nvidia deepened a
reported stake in Perplexity at a valuation above $30bn, up 50% in a
year; and the pre-print semiconductor selloff (Intel −5%, AMD −4%, TSMC
−3%) read the >15% AI-server price rise as demand-softening — a read
Nvidia's own print then contradicted with a raised hyperscaler capex
guide. On wealth and power: Bessent's Iran sanctions package ("Operation
Economic Outcast," corrected this week from a stale "Economic Fury"
name this map had been carrying) named China directly as not exempt —
buying roughly 90% of Iran's oil exports — and threatened removal from
the dollar system for any facilitating entity; China's Foreign Ministry
gave its first on-record response the same week, warning against
disrupting the relationship. Texas AG Ken Paxton turned data-center
opposition into a US Senate campaign plank, including repealing Texas's
own sales-tax exemption for qualifying data centers — a state
Republican running against the industry his own state has courted.

### Q2 — extending: instrument shapes and dilution loops

SoftBank's ¥1tn retail bond got dated terms this week (7-year paper,
4.3-4.9% indicative coupon, pricing 09-04) — Bloomberg's reporting that
banks were reluctant to take the institutional risk is a direct,
observable price on the credit-risk question this map has otherwise
argued about qualitatively for a month. Two days later SoftBank stacked
a second leg: a proposed $10-20bn wholesale/international bond to
refinance the OpenAI bridge loan, with its existing 2036 notes selling
off the same day — two distinct financing-stress signals on one entity
inside 48 hours. Anthropic's own financing story is this week's clearest
illustration of narrative outrunning realized revenue: a >$100bn IPO
raise reported Monday, a >$2tn target valuation by Tuesday morning, a
claimed >$30tn total addressable market by Tuesday afternoon — against
$11.6bn in actually-realized Q2 revenue — before Wednesday's first
analyst pushback put a number on the gap. Separately, the SEC subpoenaed
four major banks (Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Citigroup, Bank of America)
over their exposure to Situational Awareness, the leveraged AI-thesis
fund whose forced unwind this map has tracked since it broke in late
July — a new instrument/exposure-risk fact this week, filed as a late
catch after falling between two digest days. No update this week on the
Broadcom $70-80bn Anthropic SPV's tranche-split structure noted last
week; no second non-US company followed Alibaba's open-dilution shape,
though Shein's Hong Kong IPO (filing 08-23, pricing 08-31, ~$27bn versus
a $98.2bn 2022 private mark) was considered and held as a comparator
note on `frontier-lab-ipos` rather than promoted to its own thread.
