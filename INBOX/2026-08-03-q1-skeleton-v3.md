## 1. The question

> "what exactly are the hyperscalers spending all that money on"

> "what 750B a year BUYS from TSMC and samsung and intel to nvidia and
> broadcom to oracle and to the frontier labs and the hyperscalers… this
> is the largest mobilization of resources in human history, raw not
> necessarily percentage, and I want to understand it."

Two native views, in Ben's words: *"where the money is going as a system
and then second how it breaks down by actors."* The category rollup
(system) and the node view (actors).

## 1.5 Why this shape — the plan's own justification

*(Part of the plan, not commentary — reviewers are fresh-context and the
reasoning must live in the artifact.)*

**What the plan aims to accomplish.** A **living model** of the AI
buildout with receipts: a complete calendar-2025 baseline, forward
quarterly buckets updated by on-demand delta passes, so news becomes
model-delta. Its instruments: the **category rollup** (what the money
buys, by kind of thing, at a stated frontier) and — **delivered when q2
lands, not by q1** (ruled 2026-08-03, R-18) — the **burn metric**
(external revenue vs. build spend, computed under a named cut). q1
builds the metric's denominator and the schema both questions share.

**Why the unit of record is a flow between entities.** Public numbers
are ambiguous ~3× because press collapses four stages of money
(guidance, commitment, contract, delivered asset) and because company
totals nest (Meta's capex contains Nvidia's revenue contains TSMC's).
Summing announcements multi-counts; allocating by captured margin needs
margin data that isn't disclosed. Recording **flows between
entity×activity nodes** needs only what the world publishes (revenue
lines, COGS, capex, contracts) — and the unit of record is the one
choice that determines what a sourcer asks of each source, the
expensive-to-change class. Edges were chosen so observations survive
every later change of parameter, category, priority — or filter.

**Why boundaries are filters, not ontology** (ruled 2026-08-03, R-16).
The map records flows classification-free. "Inside/outside" is a
**consolidation filter** applied at query time: flows between members of
the filtered group are transfers; flows crossing it are the group's
external costs and revenues. Filters are named, versioned, plural,
cheap; the map is singular and expensive. Consequences: circularity
handling stays mechanical (intra-group elimination under whichever cut
is applied); "how much is the industry paying itself" is a query under a
named cut; and totals can never move *unexplained* when tracing
deepens — coverage feeds the map, classification lives in the filter,
and any movement arrives as a typed, version-stamped delta (a
coverage-extension is a legitimate, labelled reason for a boundary total
to move; an unlabelled one is impossible by construction).

**Why provenance and reliability are separate axes.** Companies are
scope-accurate but selectively silent; independent measurement
(satellite, customs, permits, interconnection queues) is incentive-free
but noisy. Ranking by source type would hardcode deference to the most
interested parties. Every observation carries a provenance class
(descriptive) and a reliability weight (assigned with rationale,
revisable by track record); a crafted independent estimate may outweigh
company guidance.

**Why nothing is dark by definition.** Darkness is an outcome or a
priority, never a schema property. Predictions of sourceability are
written before sourcing and graded after; every sourcing attempt that
ends unmeasured leaves a dated record (§4), so "looked and found
nothing" is auditable per edge, in every tier — not just where probes
run.

**Why physically complete but bounded.** The question is literally what
the money buys — copper, concrete, earthmoving, labor included. The
materiality floor (F7) caps the regress; per round 1's finding, the
floor now conditions **all** tracing, not just the outermost hop, so the
bound is a rule, not whatever priority didn't reach.

**Why everything provisional is labelled.** Rework stays cheap only if
tunable values are data (the ⚙ convention, §12) — and the one guarded
asymmetry stands: **you can rework anything except what you didn't
capture.** Sources rot. The capture discipline — and, per round 1, the
stage/basis fields and inference lineage — are therefore front-loaded,
while every parameter and every filter stays revisable.

**What success looks like.** The bar is Ben's and deliberately still
open; a reviewer evaluates the design against the aims above.

## 2. Rulings register — authoritative

R-01 – R-15 stand as ruled 2026-08-02 (full text in v2, which remains on
disk; compressed here for a self-sufficient read):

- **R-01** flow map is the primary construction.
- **R-02** layers are activities, not companies; nodes are
  entity×activity; within-firm splits are modeled.
- **R-03** circularity is a consolidation adjustment — intra-group flows
  are transfers (now: under a named filter, per R-16).
- **R-04** no source is The Authority; observations carry assigned truth
  values; discrepancies flag, never resolve by fiat.
- **R-05** provenance ≠ reliability; independent measurement is
  first-class and may outweigh company guidance.
- **R-06** nothing is dark by definition; all twenty activities are live;
  darkness is an outcome or a priority.
- **R-07** end state is the full physical model, with sane terminal ends.
- **R-08** living model: baseline + forward buckets + delta passes;
  the delta ledger is a first-class product surface.
- **R-09** coverage predictions are hypotheses, graded, never exemptions.
- **R-10** CAPI is consumed opportunistically; origination is q1-native.
- **R-11** red teams run only after Ben reviews their brief.
- **R-12** prior work (the 56 staged records) is audited before trust.
- **R-13** v1 scope is q1; the system-revenue question is q2. Annotation
  corrected per round 1: bringing q2 online is *no redesign — one
  promoted object plus additive fields, cheap before sourcing* (not
  literally "zero schema changes").
- **R-14** starting points are never hardcoded — the ⚙ convention.
- **R-15** the delta pass runs on demand; weekly is a heartbeat floor.

New, ruled 2026-08-03:

- **R-16 · Boundaries are filters over the flow map, not memberships to
  define.** Ben, verbatim: *"we are mapping flows between entities and
  'inside/outside' is not about membership, membership is just a filter
  that classifies companies into a system or group, and all THAT has to
  do is count capital flows as transfers if they are inside the filtered
  group."* Consequences: the map records entity-to-entity flows at the
  finest gettable granularity, classification-free; a consolidation
  filter is ⚙ data — named, versioned, plural; every consolidated total
  and delta is stamped with (map version, filter version, ⚙ parameter
  set); "transfer" is filter-relative, never an intrinsic property of a
  flow.
- **R-17 · Premature boundary rules are rejected; ambiguity is named,
  not legislated.** Ben, on the proposed revenue-side boundary rule:
  *"It's a filter not a rule. We can draw the line at cloud providers.
  We can draw it at retail inference if we have a good enough map. The
  quest for a rule here is a modeling question not a data question.
  Different ways of cutting… If we can decompose revenue at that level
  we will. If we can't, we'll name the ambiguity."* Consequences: no F9;
  cuts are alternatives, not verdicts; reseller-level flows ARE
  collected (*"we do indeed want to collect information on the
  'inference reseller' level"*); a surface that could not decompose past
  a reseller says so on its face.
- **R-18 · The burn metric is delivered at q2** (option (a), the honesty
  fix). q1 ships the denominator and the shared schema; no bounded
  revenue item is pulled forward into q1.

## 3. The model — a flow map with conservation

**Nodes** are entity × activity units (`samsung/foundry`,
`google/chip-design`, `openai/model-training`). Where disclosure doesn't
support the split, an edge terminates at the entity and the node carries
the **aggregated** state until an allocation claim splits it.

**Coverage states, per edge and node** (restored in round 2 — §4's
attempt log renders here):

| state | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **measured** | at least one observation above the reliability threshold |
| **aggregated** | measured at the parent, not yet split (e.g. a capex total a filing refuses to break down) |
| **unmeasured** | looked, or not yet looked — **the attempt log says which** |

**Node instantiation (round-1 fix, replaces "how far the money is
traced" as the effective bound):** an entity×activity node comes live
when its AI-attributable flow clears the ⚙ materiality floor; purchases
by non-instantiated entities collapse into an **"other inputs"** edge at
the parent — visible in aggregate, not traced. Instantiation is a
**map-coverage** event (it never changes any filter classification), and
it is dated, so the map itself is versioned.

**Edges** are money flows between nodes, each carrying:

- **type** — asset purchase · capacity/service payment · equity · debt ·
  prepayment · revenue · non-cash consideration. *(Round-1 fix, wording
  corrected in round 2: **type by what the payment procures.** Use of
  another party's capacity or services = `capacity/service payment`,
  never build. Creation or acquisition of an asset the buyer will
  operate = `asset purchase` — **regardless of contract form**: an EPC,
  construction, or engineering contract for a datacenter the buyer will
  operate is asset acquisition, not a service. Prepayments convert per
  F3 when spent on assets.)*
- **destination category** (purchase edges only; financing and revenue
  edges take `n/a — financing`; prepayments may carry an optional
  `intended_category` annotation excluded from all totals) — ⚙ §12.
- **period** — quarter or finer; commitments carry a commitment-shaped
  period (span + schedule-if-known) per §4.
- **commitment_ref** — nullable pointer to the commitment object a flow
  draws down (the q2 seam; the field exists from day one so no flow
  needs re-attribution later).
- **observations** — per §4.

**Conservation and closure (round-1 fix):** at each node, money in =
money out + retained — **where the node closes**. Each node carries a
**closure state** (conservation-testable: yes/no + why); `retained` is a
claim requiring its own observation before conservation is asserted;
the check runs on full-basis, pre-F8-attribution flows, per (basis,
period) cell. At public filers the check runs the map's assembled edge
set against the entity's own filed totals — a real coverage/double-count
diagnostic; at opaque privates the closure state says honestly that it
cannot run.

**The map vs. the cuts (R-16).** The map is classification-free. A
**consolidation filter** is a named, versioned, dated membership list
stored as ⚙ data; applying it to the map yields that cut's transfers
(inside↔inside) and boundary flows (crossing). v1 ships one spend-side
starting cut, **`cut:core-buildout`** ⚙ — roughly: hyperscaler AI
operations, frontier labs, neoclouds, AI chip lines, semicap, memory,
packaging, networking, datacenter builders, AI-dedicated power projects;
capital providers and governments outside it (their money reads as
inflow). q2 defines revenue-side cuts (`cut:cloud-frontier`,
`cut:retail-inference`). Cuts are cheap and plural by construction — a
new question gets a new cut, never a re-sourcing.

**Totals (round-1 fix — two, named, and every rendering states its
frontier and filter):**

- **Gross build at the operator frontier** — the sum of `asset purchase`
  edges whose buyer node operates the acquired asset rather than
  reselling it, under a named cut. The quantity v2 loosely called
  "final-buyer spend" reads here. **The category rollup's read set**
  (round-2 fix): operator-frontier asset purchases **plus**
  non-decomposed `capacity/service payment` crossings of the cut,
  rendered under the "purchased compute capacity (services)" category —
  which is how a surface that could not decompose past a
  capacity seller says so on its face (R-17).
- **Consolidated boundary view** — flows crossing a named filter, in and
  out: the cut's external cost and revenue.

Interior edges feed resolution and the optional value-added lens —
never either total.

**The flow rules:**

- **F1** — totals come only from filter-crossings and from category
  rollups at a stated frontier; never sum edges along a chain
  (Meta→Nvidia→TSMC is one dollar at three hops).
- **F2** — every hop is recorded; nothing is "assigned once." Per-node
  value-add is an optional later lens where both sides are measured.
- **F3** — equity, debt, and prepayments are financing edges; under any
  filter whose group contains both endpoints they are transfers. An
  investment converts to build once, at the asset-purchase edge where it
  is spent.
- **F4** — commitments enter flows at the deployed rate per period,
  computed from stage-marked observations (§4) joined via
  `commitment_ref`; face values never enter flow totals.
- **F5** — the substance test: an event enters the map iff real
  resources or claims on capacity change hands; payment form sets edge
  *type*, never existence; revaluations of held stakes are not events.
- **F6** — within-firm allocation is claims with observations and
  reliability; `aggregated` is a first-class honest state.
- **F7 (rewritten, round 1)** — **the ⚙ materiality floor conditions all
  tracing**: a node instantiates, and its purchases are traced, only
  when AI-attributable flow clears the floor; everything below collapses
  to "other inputs" at the parent. Every rendering and total that relies
  on the floor states so on its face.
- **F8** — dedicated-to-AI assets count in full; shared assets count at
  attributable share when a defensible estimate exists (flagged with
  method), else sit aggregated at the parent. Conservation runs pre-F8.

**Origination (round-1 fix, two levels).** Discrete, disclosed financing
events (a bond, Nvidia's equity tranches, earmarked vendor financing)
carry per-edge tags (debt · equity · vendor · sovereign — round-2 trim:
operating cash is never a discrete financing event and lives ONLY in
the pool, so it is not an edge-tag value). The **push-through** question — "who funded a dollar of
build" — additionally requires the **(entity, period) funding pool**:
sources in (including operating cash) and uses out, observable from
disclosures, with per-edge attribution derived via an explicit ⚙
allocation convention (pro-rata to start). The "industry paying itself"
headline is queryable convention-free at the financing-edge level under
a named cut; the pool serves the finer question.

**The typed delta ledger (round-1 fix).** Every delta is typed:
**world-event · revision · coverage-extension · parameter-or-filter
retune** — and every published total is stamped with (map version,
filter version, ⚙ parameter-set version). A consumer can always tell
"the world moved" from "we traced further" from "we retuned."

## 4. Evidence — observations, not levels

**An observation** =
`{value, stage, basis, as_of, period, source, provenance_class,
capture_ref, reliability, rationale, method_note, derived_from?}`.

Round-1 additions, all capture-class and therefore in from observation
one:

- **stage** — guidance | commitment | contract | delivered. The plan's
  own diagnosis (four stages of money) now has a carrier; F4 reads
  `delivered`-stage observations.
- **basis** — cash | accrual | delivery. The two sides of one edge may
  legitimately sit in different quarters; reconciliation must know why.
- **value is typed** — point · upper-bound · lower-bound ·
  scope-qualified (with the scope stated, e.g. "excluding Anthropic").
  Bounds participate in reconciliation as **constraints**, never as
  averageable figures. *(Adopted from the q2 round's W-1 — the field
  lives here because the observation schema does.)*
- **period** admits a commitment shape — a span plus
  schedule-if-known — so "up to $500B over four years, unscheduled" is
  representable without lying about a quarter.
- **derived_from** — required on every inference-class observation at
  intake: the observation ids / capture refs it derives from.
  Invalidation propagation is a v1.x delta-pass feature; until then,
  "list everything downstream of X" must be answerable from the field.

**Provenance classes** (descriptive, unranked): regulatory filing ·
company statement · named-source press · independent measurement
(satellite, customs, permits, interconnection queues, power-draw,
hiring data) · analyst/model estimate · inference from other claims.

**Reliability** — numeric 0–1, one-line rationale required, revisable by
track record. Rendering bands per ⚙ §12.

**Reconciliation** runs **within (stage, basis, period) cells**; a
weighted read across stages is undefined by rule. Two further flag
classes join the disagreement flag:

- **cross-stage consistency** — delivered-to-date ≤ contracted ≤
  committed, per edge; violations flag.
- **single-class** — an edge above a ⚙ size threshold whose observations
  are all company-provenance flags for independent-measurement tasking:
  the supply-side analogue of the disagreement flag, so silent deference
  cannot accumulate invisibly.

**The attempt log (round-1 fix):** any sourcing attempt, in any tier,
that ends unmeasured writes a dated note of what was tried, attached to
the edge or node. §3's coverage table reads "unmeasured — looked, or not
yet looked; **the attempt log says which**." §7 remains the
activity-level prediction/grade layer.

**Kept:** the capture discipline — every observation, of every class,
gets its source captured (hash-bound where a document). Stage is
expensive-recoverable from captures; that is exactly why the field
exists from day one, while re-reading is still cheap.

## 6. Time — baseline plus living deltas

Calendar-2025 baseline; forward quarterly buckets; observations dated;
commitments amortize per F4. The delta pass runs **on demand** (R-15),
weekly heartbeat ⚙ as the drift floor; every pass emits **typed** deltas
(§3) and stage transitions are first-class delta events. The delta
ledger is a product surface equal in rank to the map (R-08).
**Instrumentation (round-1 W-2):** the first delta passes measure their
own per-pass labor cost (items promoted × the reliability-and-rationale
discipline per item); a ⚙ budget or tiered-rationale row joins §12 only
if that data demands it — the affordability of the evidence bar at a
weekly heartbeat is an unmeasured operating assumption until then.
