## 2. Rulings register — 2026-08-02, authoritative

Everything below is decided. Future sessions and the red team argue with
the *consequences*, not the rulings.

- **R-01 · Flow map is the primary construction.** Not a
  scoreboard-with-residual, not a margin/value-added identity. (Ben: "I'm
  thinking flow map" — treated as ruled; flag if wrong.)
- **R-02 · Layers are activities and products, not companies.** Entities
  straddle: Samsung runs foundry, memory, packaging, and washing machines;
  Google is chip designer, hyperscaler, and frontier lab; OpenAI is a
  hybrid. Nodes are entity × activity. Within-firm splits are part of the
  modeling, not an inconvenience.
- **R-03 · Circularity is a consolidation adjustment.** Intra-system
  investment is not build; intra-system revenue is not system revenue.
  Both are transfers until converted into a real-asset purchase or until
  they cross the system boundary.
- **R-04 · No source is The Authority.** Every figure is an observation
  with an assigned truth value; multiple observations per edge; a
  discrepancy beyond tolerance flags the edge — it is never resolved by
  fiat. ("You can't make one source of information THE AUTHORITY unless
  it's perfectly accurate.")
- **R-05 · Provenance and reliability are different axes.** Estimates and
  inference — satellite intelligence, car counts, shipments in and out —
  can be very good, and a well-crafted independent measurement can
  plausibly OUTWEIGH a company's own opaque or unreliable guidance.
  Piecing together credible information IS part of the work, not residue.
- **R-06 · Nothing is dark by definition.** All twenty activities are live
  model nodes from day one. Darkness is an empirical outcome (looked,
  found nothing) or a priority choice (didn't go after it in v1) — never a
  structural property of the model.
- **R-07 · The end state is the full physical model.** All the copper,
  concrete, palladium; earth moving, concrete curing, workers working —
  that is literally what the money is buying. Chinese refining to the
  extent money goes there. But sane terminal ends are required, or we're
  measuring steel for bolts on the tractor that produced the biofuel that
  moved the oil that made the gas for Bill's Thursday commute.
- **R-08 · The model is a living system, not a report.** Calendar 2025 is
  the starting snapshot; the point is to model forward with weekly or even
  daily passes updating Q1 2026, Q2 2026… Two desired outcomes: model
  granularity, and the week-to-month-to-quarter DELTA as the system moves.
  "News and information as ongoing model/system delta."
- **R-09 · The dark register is hypotheses, not declarations.** Predicted
  sourceability gets written down and then GRADED against what sourcing
  actually finds — it never exempts anything from being looked at.
- **R-10 · CAPI does little work for capital flow.** It is
  individual/ownership-focused. Grab what's grabbable, no dependency.
  Funding-origination modeling is q1-native.
- **R-11 · The red team runs only after Ben reviews its brief.** Red teams
  are often set up to produce arbitrary opposition; this one's brief is an
  artifact he edits first.
- **R-12 · Prior work is audited before it is trusted.** The 56 staged
  records get reviewed "to make sure none of it was dumb" before any
  figure sources from them.
- **R-13 · v1 scope is q1 — where the money goes.** Inference selling and
  downstream revenue belong to the later system-revenue question (q5).
  The schema carries revenue-type edges now, so bringing q5 online later
  requires no redesign.

## 3. The model — a flow map with conservation

**Nodes** are entity × activity units (`samsung/foundry`, `samsung/memory`,
`google/chip-design`, `google/dc-operation`, `openai/model-training`).
Where disclosure doesn't support the split, an edge terminates at the
entity and the node carries the **aggregated** state (see coverage states
below) until an allocation claim splits it.

**Edges** are money flows, each carrying:

- **type** — asset purchase · equity · debt · prepayment · revenue ·
  non-cash consideration
- **destination category** — what kind of thing the money bought (§11 has
  the proposed list for Ben's edit)
- **period** — the quarter (or finer) the flow belongs to
- **observations** — one or more, per the evidence model in §4

**Conservation** replaces the strawman's single identity: at each node,
money in = money out + retained. A node that doesn't balance shows a
*local* imbalance — diagnostics pointing at a specific actor, not one
system-wide residual.

**Coverage states**, per edge and node — three, not two:

| state | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **measured** | at least one observation above the reliability threshold |
| **aggregated** | measured at the parent, not yet split (e.g. a capex total a filing refuses to break down) |
| **unmeasured** | looked, or not yet looked — the probe record (§7) says which |

**The flow rules** (the strawman's six anti-double-counting rules,
rewritten under the rulings; two new ones added):

- **F1 — Totals come only from boundary crossings and category rollups.**
  Never sum edges along a chain: Meta→Nvidia→TSMC is one dollar visible at
  three hops.
- **F2 — Every hop is recorded; nothing is "assigned once."** The
  strawman's margin-assignment rule is dissolved (it was the value-added
  method it claimed to defer). "What it buys" reads off destination
  categories; per-node value-add is an optional later lens where both
  sides of a node happen to be measured.
- **F3 — Financing edges are transfers.** Equity, debt, and prepayments
  between in-system entities never enter build totals; build totals count
  only purchase edges acquiring real assets or capacity. An investment
  converts to build at the moment it is spent — Nvidia's $100B into
  OpenAI enters build when OpenAI buys something real with it, once.
- **F4 — Commitments enter at the deployed rate, per period.** Stargate's
  "up to $500B over four years" and Oracle-OpenAI's ">$300B over five"
  contribute what is actually deployed each quarter. Magnitude comes from
  reconciling ALL observations (filing, delivered-capacity math, credible
  press) under §4 — no single source is authoritative, and disagreement
  flags the edge.
- **F5 — The substance test.** An event enters the map iff real resources
  or claims on capacity change hands. Payment form (cash, stock, warrants,
  prepaid compute credits) sets the edge's *type*, never its existence:
  AMD's 160M-share warrants against 6GW of purchases are a real edge at
  disclosed issue value. Revaluations of stakes already held — Amazon's
  $53.4B Anthropic mark, Microsoft's ~$3.2B — move nothing and are not
  events; at most they annotate ownership state.
- **F6 — Within-firm allocation is modeled, explicitly.** Entity×activity
  splits are claims like any other, with observations and reliability.
  "Aggregated" is an honest first-class state, not a failure.
- **F7 — Terminal ends (NEW — threshold is Ben's call, §11).** An edge is
  modeled when it is a direct purchase by an in-system activity, or one
  hop further AND the flow clears a materiality floor. Below the floor, or
  past the hop limit, flows collapse into an "other inputs" edge at the
  parent — visible in aggregate, not traced. This is what makes the full
  physical model (copper, concrete, refining) reachable without the
  tractor-bolts regress.
- **F8 — Attribution for shared infrastructure (NEW — stance is Ben's
  call, §11).** Dedicated-to-AI assets count in full. Shared assets (grid
  power, general construction capacity) count at attributable share where
  a defensible estimate exists — flagged as an estimate with its method —
  else they sit aggregated at the parent.

**Origination (q1-native, per R-10):** every financing edge carries a
source-of-funds tag — operating cash flow · debt · equity · vendor
financing · sovereign. This is what makes "how much of this is the
industry paying itself" a queryable view instead of a vibe.

**The boundary** is no longer a roster: the twenty activities define the
*core*, and edges extend outward until F7 terminates them. Inside/outside
stops being a yes/no per company and becomes "how far the money is traced."

## 4. Evidence — observations, not levels

The strawman's four-level ladder conflated where a figure comes from with
how much to trust it (R-05). Replaced:

**An observation** = `{figure, as_of, period, source, provenance_class,
capture_ref, reliability, rationale, method_note}`.

**Provenance class** — descriptive, NOT ranked:

- regulatory filing (legal liability makes it accurate for what it states —
  and scope-limited to what it deigns to state)
- company statement (earnings call, IR deck, press release — accurate-ish,
  selectively silent, incentive-laden)
- named-source press
- **independent measurement** — satellite imagery, customs and shipping
  manifests, construction permits, grid-interconnection queue filings,
  power-draw data, hiring data, vehicle counts. Incentive-free, noisy,
  and a class this project actively cultivates rather than treats as
  residue
- analyst/model estimate
- inference from other claims in the map

**Reliability** is assigned per observation — not inherited from class —
with a required one-line rationale, and it is revisable: a source
repeatedly contradicted by later evidence loses weight (track-record
updating). A crafted independent measurement CAN outweigh a company's own
guidance; when it does, the rationale says why.

**Reconciliation:** an edge's magnitude is the weighted read across its
observations. Disagreement beyond tolerance → the edge flags. Flags are a
product surface (they mark where the system is lying to itself or to us),
not a cleanup queue.

**Kept from the strawman:** the capture discipline. Every observation, of
every class, gets its source captured (and hash-bound where it's a
document). What died is the rank-by-source-type; what survives is
show-your-receipts.

**Open (§11):** numeric reliability vs bands, and where the
measured/unmeasured rendering threshold sits.

## 5. Scope and priority — v1

All twenty activities are live nodes (R-06). Priority is about sourcing
effort, and only that:

**Tier A — source hard in v1:** power generation for AI · grid
interconnection · semicap equipment · foundry · memory/HBM · advanced
packaging · chip design · networking · datacenter construction ·
datacenter operation (the buyer side) — plus origination tagging on every
financing edge found along the way.

**Tier B — reconnaissance probe in v1:** minerals & refined inputs · land ·
water · labor · tax incentives · capital sources · model-training spend ·
inference selling · downstream revenue · end-of-life.

**A probe is a bounded pass answering one question:** does a disclosure
regime or a measurement path exist for this activity, and at what cost?
The probe's record is what later distinguishes "we looked and found
nothing" from "we didn't look" — the strawman wanted a declared register
to do that; the probe log does it empirically (R-09).

Full-physical depth (R-07) arrives by deepening Tier B under F7's
materiality rule, not by widening the schema — the schema already holds it.

## 6. Time — baseline plus living deltas

- **Baseline:** calendar 2025, complete and disclosed, built first.
- **Forward:** quarterly buckets (Q1 2026, Q2 2026, …) with observations
  dated `as_of`; commitments amortize into periods per F4.
- **The delta pass:** rides the collection pipeline that already runs
  daily — collected items become candidate observations; a recurring pass
  promotes them, updates edges, and emits "what changed in the system this
  period." The delta ledger is a first-class product surface (R-08), equal
  in rank to the map itself.
- **Open (§11):** v1 cadence — weekly delta pass, tightening later, is the
  proposal.

## 7. Coverage hypotheses (replaces the "known-dark register")

Per R-09: each activity gets a *predicted* sourceability (high / medium /
low) and a predicted best source class, written before sourcing — then
graded after v1:

- predicted-dark but found → a method win; record how, because it
  generalizes.
- predicted-sourceable but dark → investigate why; the miss is information.

The strawman's own working hypothesis is kept as an explicit falsifiable
claim to score: *roughly half of the money is sourceable, and the
unsourceable half concentrates at the two ends — minerals and power at the
bottom, real inference revenue at the top.*

  governance separately; this design states requirements (observations,

## 10. Red-team brief — DRAFT for Ben's review, not yet dispatched (R-11)

**Reviewer setup:** fresh context; receives §3 and §4 of this document
only — not the workshop discussion, not the rulings register's rationale.

**The charges — every finding must be a WORKED EXAMPLE naming entities, an
amount, and the rule(s) that fail:**

1. Construct a dollar these rules count twice.
2. Construct a dollar these rules never count at all.
3. Construct a dollar whose placement is arbitrary — the rules permit two
   placements and nothing decides between them.
4. Find an entity or flow whose F7/F8 treatment (terminal end, attribution)
   materially flips a headline total.
5. Name any rule that does no work — one that never binds on any realistic
   flow. (Dead rules are drag.)

**Discipline clauses, written to prevent opposition theater:**

- A finding without a worked construction is discarded unread.
- **Zero findings is an acceptable, reportable outcome** and is stated as
  such in the brief — the reviewer is scored on the quality of
  constructions, not the quantity of objections.
- No rhetorical or philosophical objections to the flow-map choice itself;
  the rulings register is out of scope.

**Pass bar:** more than two substantive constructions → revise the design
and re-run. Two or fewer → fold them in and proceed to sourcing.
