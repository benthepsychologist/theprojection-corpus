## 2. Notes on the non-obvious rulings

**2.1 · R-1 — one routing addition to Blue's remedy.** F9's ⚙ threshold ("AI-resale share of purchased capacity") decides where the burn metric's external crossing lands, and both Green (§5) and Red agree billions move on the placement. A parameter that moves the headline is not a silent rule-insertion — it lands as **§10 decision (h) for Ben**, with Blue's F9 as the proposed starting position. Two further requirements: the predicate's own measurand ("revenue share attributable to reselling AI capability") is estimate-driven, so classification-is-a-claim (per q1 R-04) is load-bearing, not decoration; and the predicate needs one definitional line separating *reselling AI capability* (Cursor) from *selling a product that embeds AI* (a bank's chatbot) — the gray zone is exactly where sourcers will improvise.

**2.2 · R-3 — Blue's partial defense fails in one direction.** Blue defends aggregate (a) ("the boundary filter *is* the dedup") — true for **chains** (`serves_ref`): everything downstream of an external commitment is intra-system and never enters the sum. But it is false for **containment**: a government-wide frame agreement and the per-agency commitments under it (the OneGov shape from R-7) are *all* external-buyer commitments into the system, so boundary-committed double-counts frame+constituents without `part_of_ref`. Red's "uncomputable as specified" was closer to right than Blue allowed; both relations are needed for both sanctioned aggregates. Remedy unchanged (the seam package already contains both relations) — but the record should not carry Blue's narrowing as the reason.

**2.3 · R-4 — amend §6 itself; don't dodge it.** Blue's fix rests on "§6 bans *published* headline totals; an internal deduped sum is legitimate." That distinction will not survive this system's own ethos: computed numbers become product surfaces here (q1 R-08 makes the delta ledger first-class; §7 itself calls the grade "the finding of the year" — it will be published). The clean fix: **§6 gains a sanctioned aggregate (c)** — deduped, side-filtered intra-system committed face, computable only via `serves_ref`/`part_of_ref`, and always rendered with its dedup method stated on its face (the same "labelled on its face" condition F7 already carries). Blue's restated hypothesis is otherwise correct and adopted. One extension: pin the **burn metric's** window convention too (same trailing window both sides), not just the hypothesis's — the metric inherits the identical stock-vs-flow ambiguity.

**2.4 · R-5 — the split, precisely.** Red's charge that the ladder is "rank-by-type sneaking back" is wrong — Blue's measurand-hygiene defense is correct, and the derived-estimate machinery is genuinely inherited. Red's operational claim is right — a sourcer operating decision (d) as written zeroes the two largest external sellers, and a one-directional emptiness in the quantity §7 is graded against is a confirmation bias regardless of intent. Outcome: Blue's one-clause amendment to decision (d), adopted verbatim. Counted as a substantive outcome because it changes what sourcers derive and record for the biggest sellers on the map.

**2.5 · R-6 — both seats adopted a mis-homed placement rule.** "Stages 1–2 attach to commitments" is half right. Stage 2 (booked contract) → commitment: correct. **Stage 1 (claimed run-rate) attaches to no commitment** — an "OpenAI at $X ARR" leak has no counterparty and no contract; there is nothing to attach it to under the rule as written, so sourcers will improvise, which is the failure R-6 exists to prevent. Correct placement: stage-1 observations attach to the **seller's aggregated revenue edge to `ext/*` (unallocated)** — representable once R-8's node class lands — stage-marked, excluded from period totals, eligible to spawn derived stage-3 estimates per the amended decision (d). Blue conceded R-6 without stress-testing the remedy; this is the round's clearest case of a remedy needing the same adversarial pass the charge got.

**2.6 · R-7 — Red and Green are both right, and Red is the precondition.** Green's calibration-set framing (government as the one domain where ground truth exists to grade the overlay and surveys against) is the best unclaimed idea in the round — but a calibration set built from IDIQ ceilings and Carahsoft-as-counterparty calibrates against the wrong quantity. Red's spec (task-order actions and outlays, `contract_vehicle` captured, vehicle→OEM unwinding map as a named Tier A deliverable, nominal-price awards flagged as adoption-not-revenue) is what makes Green's calibration role real. Write both into the sweep's purpose.

**2.7 · R-10 — one register-hygiene addition.** The seed row "OpenAI → Amazon: $50B investment + $100B/8yr compute" bundles a **financing event** with a **capacity commitment**. The split rule Blue proposes (commitment entries vs backlog observations) should carry a third clause: financing components are not commitment rows — they are q1 financing edges, surfacing on the commitment only as `funding_linkage`. Otherwise the register quietly becomes a second, worse home for q1's spend-side objects.

**2.8 · W-1 — point-vs-bound semantics (surfaced via Green; neither Red nor Blue caught it).** q2 §4 plans to record hyperscaler AI-segment disclosures as "**upper bounds** that *contain* inference," and R-10's reconciliation needs scope-qualified backlog observations ("$364B *excluding* Anthropic"). But q1's reconciliation is a **weighted read across observations** — and averaging an upper bound with point estimates is a category error the current observation shape cannot even detect, because `figure` carries no bound/point/scope discriminator. Green quoted the "upper bounds" seeds approvingly without noticing the reconciliation math has no bound semantics; Red's `measurand`/`stage` field covers ladder stage but not this. Fix folds into seam-package item 3 at the cost of one enum on the typed value (point · upper-bound · lower-bound · scope-qualified), with bounds participating in reconciliation as constraints, never as averageable figures. **Tag: SUBSTANTIVE** — it changes what the observation record captures.
